# Brand: I Am Ath (I Am A Health)

tags: [brand, supplements, DTC, performance-marketing, celebrity-partnership, subscription, global]

## Voice & Tone
- Science-first credibility with premium accessibility
- "The brand has to live beyond the celebrity" — product-led, not personality-led
- Transparency as a core asset (AI content labeled, clinical trials published)
- Premium and international positioning at all times

## Target Audience
- Global health-conscious consumers (ships to 31 countries)
- US = 40% of business; UK, Australia, Hong Kong, Singapore, UAE, Germany as major markets
- Customers motivated by convenience (16 supplements → 1 drink) and performance optimization
- Athletes and high-performers (product-led celebrity users are the ICP avatar)

## Brand Colors & Aesthetics
- Red (intentional contrast to Athletic Greens / AG1 category default of green)
- Contrast positioning baked into visual identity from day one

## Strategy & Positioning
- One-drink-replaces-16-supplements USP (daily ultimate essentials)
- Clinical trial-backed claims: 95% felt better energy at 90 days, 85% better gut health, 80% better sleep, 75% sharper cognitive performance (San Francisco Research Institute)
- NSF certified; science advisory board including Dr. Don (integrative oncologist, Mayo Clinic) and Bobby Rich (David Beckham's trainer)
- Celebrity co-founders/partners: David Beckham (co-founding partner, equity in parent Prenetics/Nasdaq), Aryna Sabalenka (world #1 tennis), Giannis Antetokounmpo (NBA), Ollie Bearman (F1)
- All celebrity partners are active daily product users — authenticity is non-negotiable
- 100% D2C globally, 98% own website, 2% Amazon; no retail (yet)
- Three SKUs: Daily Ultimate Essentials, Longevity, Back on Stack (Essentials + Longevity combo)
- Parent company: Prenetics (Nasdaq-listed)

## Do's
- Lead with product science before celebrity (celebrity amplifies, product converts)
- Structure celebrity deals as minimum 3-year with equity component
- Maintain weekly calls with celebrity partners' teams
- Use 90-day subscription framing — gives product time to work, creates habitualization
- Be transparent about AI content (labeled it, went viral for it)
- Run 1,500+ ads at scale; diversity of creative is what unlocks Meta spend ceiling
- Keep retail at arm's length until leverage is maximized

## Don'ts
- Don't let it become "just another celebrity brand" — product must stand alone
- Don't over-index on video at the expense of statics (75% statics, 25% video mix)
- Don't add retail distribution too early — it loses focus and reduces brand control
- Don't accept short-term (1-year) celebrity deals
