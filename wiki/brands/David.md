# Brand: David

## Overview

David is a protein bar company founded by Peter Rahal (co-founder of RXBAR, acquired by Kellogg for $600M in 2017) in September 2024. In 255 days, David reached a $725 million valuation. On pace for $100M+ revenue in its first year. Expanded to 3,000+ retail locations in 8 months. Backed by Greenoaks Capital; Dr. Peter Attia (Chief Science Officer) and Dr. Andrew Huberman are investors and advisors.

**Core product specs:** 28g protein / 150 calories / 0g sugar — vs. market standard of ~20g protein / ~200 calories.

---

## Voice & Tone

- Minimalist, serious, no-frills
- Evidence-based language — science, not lifestyle
- Confident without being loud — "a quieter, more grown-up version of RXBAR"
- Anti-marketing in execution: lets the product numbers speak
- Avoids trend language (no "paleo," no "clean," no "functional") in favor of precise nutritional claims
- Tone matches Dr. Peter Attia's clinical seriousness more than a wellness influencer

---

## Target Audience

- Fitness-oriented adults who track macros (protein-forward, calorie-aware)
- Science-literate consumers — Peter Attia / Andrew Huberman audience
- People in the "anti-snacking, anti-carbohydrate" behavioral shift
- Anyone who has outgrown paleo/wellness trend language and wants evidence-backed nutrition
- Primary discovery channel: TikTok awareness → retail conversion

---

## Brand Brief

"Don't overeat calories, get an adequate amount of protein, and don't spike your blood sugar."

Three constraints embedded in one sentence. Every product decision, design decision, and marketing decision maps back to this brief.

---

## Strategy & Positioning

**The consumer surplus advantage:** David delivers meaningfully more value than market alternatives at the same price point.
- Market standard: 20g protein, ~200 calories
- David: 28g protein, 150 calories, 0g sugar
- The gap is large enough to generate organic word-of-mouth without paid advertising

**Science over trends:** Where RXBAR bet on ingredient purity (paleo alignment), David bets on scientific optimization. Uses cutting-edge ingredients like EPG (esterified propoxylated glycerol) — a fat substitute that digests differently with reduced caloric impact. Backed by nutritional literature, not diet culture.

**Credibility infrastructure:** Rather than fitness influencers, David's inner circle is clinical — Dr. Peter Attia (longevity/nutrition MD) as CSO, Dr. Andrew Huberman as investor. The product's scientific claims are the brand positioning, not aesthetics.

**Distribution sequencing:** TikTok generates awareness → retail is where trial happens (lowest barrier). Both channels work together rather than competing.

---

## Launch Playbook

1. 20,000-unit open Instagram giveaway — captured email list from all applicants
2. Strategic influencer seeding to carefully selected creators trusted by target demographic
3. Minimal but high-quality owned content — emphasis on product specs and mission
4. Encouraged honest user-generated reviews and unboxing content
5. Multiple organic videos hit 100K–200K+ views with no paid promotion
6. Within 8 months: 3,000+ retail locations

The launch didn't create artificial hype — it let a product with genuine consumer surplus generate its own momentum.

---

## Do's

- Lead with the numbers (28g / 150 cal / 0g sugar) — they are the marketing
- Clinical, serious tone that matches the Attia/Huberman credibility ecosystem
- Encourage honest reviews — the product supports them
- TikTok for awareness, retail for conversion
- Let UGC carry the story; don't over-produce

## Don'ts

- No paleo positioning — Rahal explicitly calls this a "death trap" and "laziness trap"
- No wellness trend language
- Don't over-market what the product can prove on its own
- Don't obscure the specs with lifestyle imagery
