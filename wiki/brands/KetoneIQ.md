# Brand: Ketone-IQ (HVMN)

## Voice & Tone
- Science-forward but accessible — translates research (DoD grants, Oxford PhDs) into plain-language benefit claims
- Founder-led authenticity: Michael Brandt is an active marathon runner and biohacker who uses his own product
- Tagline energy: "brain has the energy of coffee with the calmness of meditation"
- Avoids hype; positions as a *primitive* fuel source (like water), not a trend supplement
- Ambitious long-term vision framing: "ketones will be 10% of global calories"

## Target Audience
- **Primary:** Elite athletes and endurance runners (marathoners, Ironman, ultra runners)
- **Secondary:** Special Operations / military operators treated as performance athletes
- **Tertiary / mass market:** Anyone doing knowledge work, studying, driving — anyone with a brain doing metabolism (i.e., everyone)
- Key insight: starts at the performance fringe (athletes, Navy SEALs) and works toward mass adoption — same trajectory as caffeine moving from sprinters to a billion cups/day

## Brand Colors & Aesthetics
- Not detailed in source — performance/science aesthetic implied
- Distributed through premium grocery (Sprouts, targeting Whole Foods tier)

## Strategy & Positioning
- **"Tree trunk primitive" positioning:** ketones are not a fringe supplement — they are a fundamental metabolite every human body already makes. Positioned as the next nutritional primitive after water.
- **Military-to-consumer validation arc:** First proved product with DoD Special Operations Command ($6M research grant), then elite athletes (Team Ineos, top American marathoners, NBA team investors), then premium grocery retail (Sprouts launch)
- **Grassroots distribution sequence:** DoD → elite athletes → specialty health grocery (Sprouts) → broader retail
- **Investor/partnership architecture:** Joe Montana fund, NBA team owners, Jake Paul co-founder connection (anti-fund), Sarah Hall (top 3 American marathoner), Team Ineos — credibility network that doubles as distribution channel proof
- **Science as moat:** VP of Product (Stanford + MIT Chemical Engineering), Head of Science (Oxford PhD in cardiac metabolism, principal investigator on DoD contract) — subject matter depth is the brand's defensible asset
- **Dual goal horizon:** 3-month operational focus (Sprouts door visits, sell-through) + 10-year galaxy brain vision (ketones as global nutritional primitive, Mars travel) — both active simultaneously

## Do's
- Lead with the science and the DoD credibility to establish legitimacy before moving to mass market claims
- Use elite athlete partnerships as proof-of-concept distribution, not just awareness
- Frame the product as a "nutritional primitive" — not a new supplement, but a newly-accessible molecule your body already makes
- Let the founder's personal marathon story humanize the science story

## Don'ts
- Don't position as a niche biohacker supplement — the long-term vision is universal metabolic health
- Don't over-rely on caffeine comparisons (intentionally distanced from stimulant positioning)
- Don't abandon the specialty/premium tier before establishing sell-through velocity
