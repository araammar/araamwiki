# Concept: TikTok-to-Amazon-to-Retail Distribution Model

## Summary / Definition
Dom Iacovone's 2026 CPG launch and distribution model: start on TikTok (organic + affiliate + paid), let it spill over to Amazon, then use the combined velocity data to push into retail. TikTok is described as "the new DTC" — it has replaced Facebook/Meta as the primary customer acquisition channel for new CPG brands.

Dom: *"If anyone's starting a brand today, that's the model you pick and that's the model you run with."*

## Why It Matters
The old model (influencer-backed DTC → specialty retail → FDM → club) still holds structurally, but the starting point has shifted. TikTok Shop and TikTok-driven awareness have compressed the timeline from DTC to retail. Brands can get retail attention faster because TikTok produces velocity data that retail buyers now respect.

## How It Works / The Mechanism

**Stage 1: TikTok**
- Seed product to TikTok creators (influencer + affiliate)
- Run TikTok paid ads (80% of Dom's total ad spend goes to TikTok in 2026)
- Run TikTok Shop for direct commerce (or drive to Amazon/brand site if TikTok Shop isn't ready)
- Target: the younger, TikTok-native audience that transacts in-app

**The TikTok economics model:**
- Run TikTok at **break even**
- Let the awareness spill over to Amazon and DTC, where margins are better
- Don't try to extract all your margin from TikTok — use it as a paid distribution engine that funds itself

*"The secret sauce right now is you run TikTok at break even and you let it all flood over everywhere else."*

**Stage 2: Amazon**
- ~40% spillover from TikTok to Amazon (based on tracking tools)
- Maintain Amazon ads spend even if TikTok is driving organic Amazon traffic — Dom suspects Amazon algorithmically punishes brands that reduce their spend
- Amazon "bestseller" badge (e.g., #1 pre-workout on Amazon for RAW) creates massive shelf credibility and retail buying leverage
- Amazon is where older demographics go to verify and purchase after seeing TikTok content

**Stage 3: Specialty Retail (Vitamin Shoppe / GNC)**
- First retail stop — smaller footprint, better margins, better serving sizes, premium price points
- Primary value: **learning how to manage a retailer** (trade spend, forecasting, relationships) before entering FDM
- Dom: *"If you don't get it there, you are going to get swallowed in FDM."*

**Stage 4: FDM (Food, Drug, Mass)**
- Publix, HEB, Walmart, CVS
- Lower price points, tighter margins
- Requires price pack architecture to avoid cannibalization between channels

**Stage 5: Club (Sam's Club / Costco)**
- Highest volume, lowest margin (~15% margin vs. 40–50% elsewhere)
- But extremely high velocity — you win on volume
- Can't jump to club before building brand; club won't take you and can't build a brand on its own
- Club is where you make money, not where you make brand

**The anti-club-first lesson:**
The trap: seeing club's economics and trying to go there first. Dom: *"You have to build up to those numbers. Club isn't going to build a brand."*

**TikTok's limitations for exit:**
PE buyers still discount TikTok-heavy revenue. If $200M of revenue is all TikTok, their first question is: what happens if TikTok gets banned?

Dom's answer: **get it off TikTok as fast as possible.** Convert TikTok velocity into retail and Amazon presence. Those channels are defensible. TikTok is a launch weapon, not a business moat.

## What to Steal / Application
- **If launching a CPG brand in 2026:** TikTok (organic/affiliate/paid) → Amazon → specialty retail → FDM → club. Don't skip steps.
- **Run TikTok at break even as a traffic engine.** The margin lives downstream at Amazon, DTC, and retail.
- **Use Amazon rankings as retail leverage.** "#1 on Amazon" is something retail buyers understand and respect.
- **Never let your business be 100% TikTok-dependent.** Convert velocity into multi-channel presence as fast as possible.
- **Specialty retail is a training ground** — learn retailer management there before you're in Walmart.
