# Concept: Founder Email

## Summary / Definition
A plain text email written in the voice of the founder, CEO, or a named personality at the brand. No heavy graphics, no product-promo layout — just a human writing to another human. Deployed for big moments: product launches, sales, milestone celebrations, brand mistakes, or behind-the-scenes storytelling.

## Why It Matters
Most brand email is over-designed promotional content that signals "this is an ad." The founder email breaks that pattern. It reads as personal correspondence, not marketing collateral. The absence of design is the signal — it communicates that something real is being said. In an era of distrust (see [[distrust-economy]]), the plain text format is itself a credibility move.

## How It Works / The Mechanism
**When to use it:**
- Launching a product — tell the story behind it
- Running a sale — founder personally explains the deal or frames the moment
- Spotlighting an underappreciated product — tell the story of something the audience hasn't noticed
- Celebrating a milestone — let the brand feel human and share the moment
- Responding to a mistake or issue — direct, honest, no corporate speak

**Format:**
- Plain text (no HTML design templates or heavy graphics)
- First-person voice of founder or specific named personality
- Narrative structure — tell a story, don't just announce
- Optional: single image if it adds to the story, not decoration

**Where it lives in the funnel:**
- Welcome automation: founder introduces themselves and brand backstory when someone first subscribes
- Post-purchase automation: founder welcomes new customer, explains the brand's values or origin
- Broadcast moments: Black Friday, product launches, brand crises

**The key mechanic:** When a subscriber has been receiving polished HTML emails and suddenly gets a plain text message from "Alex, the founder," the open rate and read rate spike because it pattern-interrupts. The brain reads it as a personal message, not a campaign.

## What to Steal / Application
- Put at least one founder email in the welcome flow — have the founder (or brand voice) introduce themselves before any product pitch lands
- Reserve the format for high-stakes moments so it retains its signal value — overuse commoditizes it
- Pair with social media storytelling content: if the founder is already telling their story on Instagram/TikTok (see [[brand-social-media-from-scratch]]), the email is a deeper, more intimate version of the same content — cross-reinforcing
- Use for brand recovery moments (shipping delays, product issues) — a plain text founder message converts skeptics better than a polished "we're sorry" template
