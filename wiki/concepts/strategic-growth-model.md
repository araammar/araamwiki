# Concept: Strategic Growth Model (SGM)

## Summary / Definition
The SGM is an annual operating planning framework used by the Quality Group (Dom Iacovone's holding company). At the start of each year, leadership identifies four key initiatives — the only four things the company will focus on — and builds the entire organization's goals, resources, and KPIs around those four building blocks.

Dom calls it a "sustainable growth model." The framework eliminates all activity not tied to those four pillars. No meetings that don't ladder up to an SGM initiative. No shiny objects.

## Why It Matters
Most founder-operators are "run-and-gun" — reactive, opportunistic, easily distracted. The SGM installs guard rails. The beautiful part: it eliminates all the noise. If something doesn't connect to one of the four initiatives, it doesn't get done this year.

Dom: *"Between us, I see a new shiny object and I want to look at it. Then the next thing you know you've been distracted for two months chasing nothing and you go back two months behind."*

## How It Works / The Mechanism

**Step 1 — Annual SGM kickoff (January)**
- Identify the four most important initiatives for the year
- Assign quantifiable KPIs and end dates to each
- Examples from RAW: creatine push, fixing core business, Bum Energy growth, retail expansion

**Step 2 — Top-down / bottom-up construction**
- Top-down: CEO delivers the goals from above
- Bottom-up: teams answer "what does it take?" — headcount, inventory, door counts, velocity targets, etc.
- This translation of big goals into granular sub-goals gets every level of the org aligned and motivated

**Step 3 — Cascading alignment**
- Every employee — warehouse, marketing, video, finance — should be able to articulate which building block they're working toward
- If the marketing team is shooting protein content when the SGM says creatine, the org is misaligned

**Step 4 — Weekly operating rhythm**
- Monday: finance touch base (net revenue + CM1 only — the two most important weekly metrics)
- Tuesday–Thursday: dedicated to primary brand (RAW/Bum at corporate HQ)
- Also: ops/SNOP meeting (high-level overview of everything happening in the company), stagegate innovation meeting (new product go/no-go)
- Friday: work from home catch-up with all portfolio companies

**Step 5 — Course correcting**
- Finance sees everything first — numbers signal when a pivot makes sense
- A viral moment or unexpected category surge may warrant adjusting an initiative, but only if it still hits the financial end goal (CM1, EBITDA targets)

**Multi-company version:**
Dom runs SGM kickoffs for ALL of his brands simultaneously. Each company's CEO owns the execution. Dom meets with each CEO once per week to track KPIs.

## What to Steal / Application
- Use for any business with more than one growth priority. Four initiatives max — not ten.
- The top-down/bottom-up construction is the key: leadership sets goals, team builds the operational plan to hit them. This closes the alignment gap.
- Apply the cascade test: can the most junior employee in your company explain which initiative their work serves?
- Running multiple companies? Run a separate SGM for each. Let each CEO own theirs. You track the headline numbers.
