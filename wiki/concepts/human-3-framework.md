# Concept: Human 3.0 Framework

## Summary / Definition

Human 3.0 is Dan Koe's personal developmental model — a synthesis of three established frameworks that map human psychological evolution. It provides a diagnostic tool for understanding where a person is in their development across multiple life domains, and a directional map for what to do next.

The three source models:
1. **Nine Stages of Ego Development** (Susanne Cook-Greuter) — maps how the human self-concept evolves through distinct developmental stages
2. **Spiral Dynamics** (Beck & Cowan) — maps how values and worldview evolve through color-coded stages driven by life conditions
3. **Ken Wilber's AQAL Model** ("All Quadrants, All Levels") — a meta-framework integrating interior/exterior and individual/collective dimensions of reality

Koe synthesized these into a usable personal development map with three components: quadrants, levels, and phases.

## Why It Matters

Most self-development frameworks focus on either external behavior (habits, productivity, discipline) or internal psychology (beliefs, mindset, identity). AQAL recognizes that both dimensions matter and that the individual and collective are both in play. Human 3.0 applies this to practical self-development by giving someone a multi-dimensional map rather than a single-axis improvement model.

The practical value: instead of asking "how do I improve?", you ask "which quadrant is underdeveloped, and what level am I at in it?" This diagnoses the actual problem rather than applying generic solutions.

## The Four Quadrants

Koe's interpretation of Wilber's four quadrants as four fundamental domains of reality:

| Quadrant | Domain | What It Covers |
|----------|--------|----------------|
| **Mind** | Personal interior | Thoughts, beliefs, consciousness, ideas |
| **Body** | Personal exterior | Habits, behavior, physical appearance, health |
| **Spirit** | Collective interior | Culture, shared beliefs, relationships, community |
| **Vocation** | Collective exterior | Role in society, systems, work, output |

These four cover almost everything from a sweeping generalized lens. A problem in any area of life can typically be traced to one or two of these quadrants being underdeveloped at the person's current level.

## The Three Levels

Rather than using the full 9-stage ego development spectrum, Koe maps it into three usable tiers:

- **Level 1 (Low Consciousness):** Conformist stage — following social scripts, doing what's expected, low self-authorship
- **Level 2 (Mid Consciousness):** Individualist stage — developing personal perspective, questioning received norms, building self-direction
- **Level 3 (High Consciousness):** Integrated stage — operating across multiple perspectives, high agency, genuine self-authorship

The characteristics of each level manifest differently in each quadrant — so someone can be Level 3 in Vocation (highly self-directed in their work) but Level 1 in Body (following default habits, no intentional health practice).

## The Phases Within Each Level

Within each level, the [[vertical-vs-horizontal-development]] and [[anti-vision-and-three-phase-leveling]] models apply:

- **Dissonance** — current level feels mundane, signal of readiness to move up
- **Uncertainty** — between levels, lost, in transition
- **Discovery** — found the next level, moving in

Progress through each level requires three acquisitions:
1. **Knowledge** — understanding what's happening at that level
2. **Skill** — competence to operate there
3. **Experience** — lived judgment from having operated there

## The Diagnostic Prompt

Koe created a publicly accessible AI prompt linked from his newsletter (pinned on his Twitter). The prompt conducts a 20-minute question-and-answer session, then generates a comprehensive document that identifies:
- Which quadrant(s) have the most developmental gap
- What level each quadrant is currently at
- The specific phases and what is needed to progress
- The steps forward

One user reported crying at the accuracy of the output.

## The "Multi-Dimensionally Jacked" Vision

The aspirational target of the Human 3.0 model: someone who is firing at all cylinders across all four quadrants simultaneously, progressing through the levels in each. Not a one-dimensional optimizer (wealthy but disconnected, fit but purposeless), but a genuinely integrated person.

This is the Dan Koe brand's underlying thesis: that entrepreneurship and personal development are the same project. Building a business is a vehicle for developing your mind, body, spirit, and vocation simultaneously.

## What to Steal / Application

- Run a quick self-assessment: across the four quadrants (Mind / Body / Spirit / Vocation), which is most underdeveloped? That's the diagnostic starting point.
- Don't try to advance all four simultaneously — identify the one that is most limiting the others and address it first.
- The level framework helps diagnose why generic advice doesn't work: Level 1 problems require different solutions than Level 2 problems, even in the same quadrant.
- For content creators: this model is a way to position broadly (life development) while having specific frameworks (the quadrants and levels) that provide actionable content depth.
