# Concept: Starbridge — Product Analysis
tags: [B2B, SaaS, GTM intelligence, SLED, public sector, product analysis, competitive, signal-based outreach]

## Summary / Definition

Starbridge is a go-to-market intelligence platform built exclusively for companies selling into SLED accounts (State, Local, Education). It surfaces buying signals before RFPs are published — board meeting mentions, contract expirations, grant approvals, strategic plan language — so sales teams can get in front of deals before they're locked. The tagline used in live pitches: **"The bionic ear to your market."**

The core insight: **80% of RFPs are pre-written for a selected vendor.** By the time a deal hits the public marketplace, it's already decided. Starbridge helps you get in before the RFP exists.

Website: Starbridge.ai

---

## The Problem They Solve

Companies selling to government and education accounts face three compounding problems:

1. **They wait for RFPs.** By the time the bid is public, a vendor is already positioned to win. They're competing on paper, not on relationships.
2. **Their data is wrong.** ZoomInfo and similar tools use LinkedIn as a primary source. Public sector entities are notoriously bad at updating LinkedIn. Contact data is stale.
3. **RFPs live on 50 different platforms.** No one portal. Finding every relevant bid requires human monitoring across dozens of hosting systems.

Starbridge solves all three: surface intent before the RFP, scrape real staff directories for accurate contacts, aggregate all RFP platforms in one place.

This is the actual moat — what they can see that competitors can't:

| Source | What It Gives You |
|---|---|
| **FOIA requests** (bulk) | Sent to every public entity in the US — extracts contract data, vendor info, spend line items |
| **Board meeting minutes** | What city councils, school boards, county commissions are literally saying in meetings |
| **Strategic plans** | Long-term capital and technology priorities, published by public sector entities |
| **Committee agendas** | Granular upcoming decision items |
| **Grant approvals** | When a municipality gets a federal grant, they have new budget to spend |
| **Purchase order data** | Line-by-line what competitors are selling, who they're selling to, exact amounts |
| **Staff directory scraping** | Real-time scrape of actual public sector staff websites — not LinkedIn |
| **RFP databases** | Aggregated across all hosting platforms (problem: every agency uses a different one) |
| **Custom web agents** | Fully configurable search agents for any signal the client specifies |

Key differentiation on contact data: "Public sector entities are really bad at updating their LinkedIn. The way we approach contact data is literally going out to those staff directory websites in public sector and scraping in real time." — Zach (Starbridge director, warm handoff call)

Charter school limitation: charter schools are private, so they don't have to share purchase records or board minutes. Web scraping still works for charter school strategic plans.

## Full Feature Set

### 1. RFP Sourcing + Tracking
Aggregates RFPs from all hosting platforms into one feed. AI scores each RFP for fit with your ICP. Solves the fragmentation problem — one prospect said their biggest bottleneck was "access to all the RFPs and submitting them timely because they run through different hosting platforms."

### 2. AI Proposal Writer
Expedites RFP submission. Compliance matrix built in. "Helping teams punch above their weight class."

### 3. Meetings Intelligence
Monitors board meeting minutes, strategic plans, committee agendas. Pulls in anything the public sector entity says in an organized meeting. Configurable by geography and account type.

### 4. Purchase Order Data
Competitor contracts, line-by-line. See what vendors are selling, who they're selling to, what amounts, when contracts expire. This is where Starbridge separates from GovSpend — historical vs. forward-looking.

### 5. Contact Data
Real-time scrape of staff directory websites. Job change alerts configurable (e.g., "alert me whenever there's a new director of facilities at my target accounts"). Avoids the LinkedIn staleness problem entirely.

### 6. Grant + Funding Alerts
Capital improvement plan budget approvals, FEMA grants, federal infrastructure funding. A school that just got a $4M capital improvement budget approved now needs vendors.

### 7. Custom Web Agents
Fully configurable search around any signal the client defines. Example used in demo: "fire department modernization initiatives" — pulls all public mentions, sources the reasoning, surfaces which departments are actively talking about equipment upgrades.

### 8. Account Scoring System
Layer scoring criteria over all signals. Example: "if a fire department has a facilities leader, has spent on asset management platforms, has gone to RFP for janitorial services in the past 3 years, and has this enrollment size — score them a 5." Reps know exactly which accounts to prioritize.

### 9. "Bridge" — AI-Enriched Lead List
Their packaged output format. Takes signals from all sources, applies AI analysis, outputs an enriched, scored, prioritized lead list. Each entry includes: contact info, signals, competitive intel, suggested outreach angle, email snippets or cold call scripts.

## Competitive Positioning

### vs. GovSpend
"GovSpend tells you where money was spent. Starbridge tells you where and when it's about to be."

GovSpend is backward-looking (historical spend). Starbridge is forward-looking (intent before the deal). Teams often use both: GovSpend for context, Starbridge for outbound timing.

### vs. ZoomInfo
- ZoomInfo is horizontal (every market). Starbridge is vertical-only (SLED).
- ZoomInfo uses LinkedIn. Starbridge uses actual staff directory scraping.
- ZoomInfo shows who to call. Starbridge shows who to call, what they're about to buy, and what message to send.

### vs. Pursuit / NationGraph
Closest alternatives. Starbridge differentiates on: "deeper buying signals like competitor contract expirations, live meeting monitoring." Teams switch from Pursuit when they want to move from reactive intel to proactive outreach.

## ICP (Who Buys Starbridge)

Micah's qualification criteria from the campaign brief:

- **Actively sells into SLED** (state, local, education) — this must be a real part of their revenue, not aspirational
- **VP / manager level or above** (not SDRs, not individual contributors who don't make decisions)
- **Pain points present:** time-consuming proposal process, lack of real-time insights, trouble identifying high-intent opportunities before bids drop
- **Budget:** SLED revenue must be significant enough to justify a dedicated tool

Companies that called: facility management, restoration services, defense contractors, dental/medical equipment companies with government contracts, architecture firms doing public projects, janitorial services, technology companies with government verticals.

## Sales Motion

1. **Cold outreach** via contracted reps (Glen Coco marketplace model — $1,000/qualified meeting payout to Micah)
2. **15-minute screen share framing** — "not a full demo, just showcasing the dashboards and AI in action"
3. **Warm handoff** — rep passes booked prospect to director/AE (Zach) who runs the full discovery + demo
4. **Demo approach:** fully customized to prospect's specific market. If they're targeting fire departments, the demo shows fire departments. If K12, it shows schools in their state. Not generic.
5. **Next step:** larger stakeholder demo OR send materials for internal evaluation
