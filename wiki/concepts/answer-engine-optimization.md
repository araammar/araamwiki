# Concept: Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

## Summary / Definition

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of ensuring your brand appears — and appears favorably — when someone asks an AI system (ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity) for a recommendation in your product category. As AI answer engines increasingly replace traditional search clicks, AEO is becoming the new SEO.

The shift: **60% of searches now end without a click.** Google Gemini, ChatGPT, and Perplexity return answers directly, skipping websites entirely. The engine picks who it recommends. Most brands have zero visibility into whether they're even in that conversation.

---

## Why It Matters

Traditional SEO was about ranking on the search results page — getting someone to click through to your website. AEO is about being the brand the AI *names* when someone asks "what's the best [product in your category]?"

If the AI doesn't know you exist, or if your brand's presence across the web is thin or unclear, you will not be recommended — regardless of how good your product is. This is a new distribution failure mode that brands haven't had to think about before.

The research method used to be: Google rankings + backlinks + SEO game. That playbook is now degrading. The buyer research funnel has shifted:

```
Old funnel:  Search query → Results page → Click → Website → Purchase
New funnel:  Ask AI → AI names a brand → Brand lookup or direct purchase
```

---

## How It Works / The Mechanism

AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) are trained on web data and updated continuously. They form opinions about which brands are credible, well-known, and recommended in a given category based on:

- **Reddit mentions and discussions** — high-authority signal for AI systems
- **Social media presence and post recency** — platforms like TikTok, Instagram, YouTube
- **Review content and third-party citations** — what others say about you, not what you say about yourself
- **Website clarity** — whether your brand's web presence clearly communicates what category you're in

### The Visibility Score

Tools like HubSpot AEO measure brand visibility across the three major AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity). The output is:

1. **A visibility score** — how often you appear vs. competitors when your category is queried
2. **A citation gap map** — where competitors are getting cited and you're not
3. **A plain-English action plan** — what to write, what to fix, what to post to improve visibility

Example: A shoe bag brand with no AEO work scores 0% visibility. The tool identifies that a Reddit post and several platform-specific social posts about the product would begin registering the brand across AI systems.

---

## What to Steal / Application

1. **Audit your AEO visibility now.** Ask ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity: "What's the best [your product category]?" If your brand doesn't appear, that's a gap that will only grow.

2. **Get on Reddit.** Reddit is disproportionately weighted by AI training data. A single helpful, authentic Reddit post in your category is worth more for AEO than ten SEO-optimized blog posts.

3. **Make category membership explicit.** Ensure your website, social bios, and content clearly state what category you're in — not just what your product does. AI needs to know what box to put you in to recommend you.

4. **Track AEO alongside traditional analytics.** As the zero-click share of search grows, organic website traffic becomes a less reliable proxy for brand discoverability.

---

## The Broader Shift

AEO is one symptom of the larger fragmentation of discovery channels:
- SEO (Google) is degrading due to zero-click AI answers
- Social discovery remains strong but is platform-specific and algorithm-dependent
- AI recommendation is a new third channel that operates on different rules

Brands that treat AEO as optional are betting that AI recommendation won't become a primary discovery mechanic. That bet is getting worse each quarter.
