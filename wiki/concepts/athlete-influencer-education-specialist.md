# Concept: Athlete/Influencer Education Specialist

## Summary / Definition
A dedicated internal role at RAW Nutrition — modeled on a German counterpart strategy from the Quality Group's European operations — whose job is to sit one-on-one with each athlete/influencer partner and train them to post and promote authentically, effectively, and in ways that actually drive revenue.

The role is equal parts **relationship manager** and **content coach**. The person filling it (Isabelle, at RAW) doesn't just manage contracts — she develops the influencer's ability to grow their own audience, which is the only way the brand benefits long-term.

## Why It Matters
Most brands sign influencer deals and send a product. The influencer posts a photo holding the product with a discount code underneath. Nothing sells.

Dom's insight: *"A lot of these people don't really know how to do it right. They don't want to look corny. But when you really educate them on how to do it right, it doesn't come off corny at all — it comes off very real and authentic. And it makes a massive difference in their compensation."*

There's also a structural problem with audience decay: after 3 months, a brand has seen the influencer's entire audience. Unless the influencer is growing 10–20%+ month-over-month, the brand is generating diminishing returns. The solution: help the influencer grow.

## How It Works / The Mechanism

**What the specialist does:**
1. Sits one-on-one with each athlete/influencer on the roster
2. Coaches content creation: why do you take this product, when do you take it, what's the benefit, why is it better than competitors?
3. Teaches format: 3 stories in a row to tell a narrative vs. a single static post, how to structure a video endorsement authentically
4. Timing: what time of day to post, what platform combination works best
5. Personal brand development: if the influencer doesn't grow their own following, the brand exhausts their audience within 3 months

**The 3-month decay problem:**
Brand signs influencer for 12 months. In the first 3 months, the brand extracts ~90% of the total value it will ever get from that person's existing audience. Without new audience growth, months 4–12 produce diminishing returns.

Counter-strategy 1: **Short, higher-paid contracts.** Many brands now do 3-month deals at 2x the monthly rate instead of 12-month deals. Pay 20K/3 months instead of 10K/12 months. Get the first-3-month spike and move on to a different demographic.

Counter-strategy 2 (what RAW does): **Invest in the influencer's growth.** If Isabelle can help the influencer build their audience by 10–20%/month, the brand's addressable audience grows with them.

**The authenticity coaching framework:**
Problem: influencers post corny, sponsor-looking content because they're afraid of looking like a shill.
Solution: teach them that authenticity IS the format.
- Answer the questions a real consumer would have: why do you use it, when, how does it actually work?
- Talk about it the way you'd talk about it to a friend
- Specificity + conviction = authentic. Vague praise = corny.

**The compensation incentive loop:**
When influencers learn that authentic, educational content drives significantly higher conversion (and therefore higher affiliate commission), they become self-motivated to do it right. The education specialist creates this lightbulb moment.

## What to Steal / Application
- **Create this role if your influencer roster is more than 5–10 people.** The leverage is massive.
- **The "3 stories in a row" tactic is immediately applicable.** Instead of a single post, tell a micro-story arc across consecutive stories.
- **Coach to specificity.** "This pre-workout is amazing" sells nothing. "I take this 20 minutes before training, specifically before leg day because I need 45 minutes of sustained energy without a crash" sells.
- **Track influencer audience growth as a KPI, not just their post performance.** If they're not growing, you're depleting a fixed asset.
- **Short contracts for newer/mid-tier influencers.** Long contracts for proven partners who are growing. Differentiate.
