# Framework: The 7 Levels Of Advertisers
tags: [leads, Hormozi, roadmap, framework, scaling, levels]

## Summary / Definition

Hormozi's roadmap of advertising maturity. Seven discrete levels from "your friends know about your stuff" to "executives run your billion-dollar leads machine without you."

> *"These levels will help you identify where you are on the advertising totem pole so you know what to do to get to the next level."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Leads*

Acquisition.com uses this exact roadmap to scale portfolio companies from a few million per year to $100,000,000+.

---

## Level 1 — Your Friends Know About The Stuff You Sell

**Primary Action**: Warm outreach

You make one offer to one avatar on one platform. The moment you get engaged leads, you can start making money.

> *"For me, this started with reaching out to everyone I knew."*

See [[warm-outreach-system]]. This is where every business starts.

---

## Level 2 — You Consistently Let Everyone You Know About The Stuff You Sell

**Primary Actions**: Do as much warm outreach as you can + post as much content as you can

You know the exact inputs to get an engaged lead. By scaling inputs, you get consistent customers. But you're still maxing your **personal** work capacity.

> *"For me, on top of warm reach outs, I maximized my personal work capacity with paid ads, using a case study as my lead magnet. But looking back, I wish I would have started with posting free content. So I suggest that."*

The Level 2 entrepreneur is the highest-output single human in the business.

See [[content-unit-framework]] + [[give-ask-ratio]].

---

## Level 3 — You Get Employees To Help You Do More Advertising

**Primary Action**: Hire people to advertise profitably on your behalf

You've maxed your personal advertising inputs. The only way to do more is more people.

> *"For me, I hired a videographer and a media buyer to take most of the paid ads work off my plate."*

This is the first leverage milestone. Output is no longer limited by Hormozi's daily hours.

See [[employees-as-lead-getters]].

---

## Level 4 — Your Product Is Good Enough To Get Consistent Referrals

**Primary Actions**: Focus on product until you get consistent referrals (25%+ of new customers), then go back to scaling advertising with a bigger team

The Level 4 transition is **product quality**. Your acquisition machine is fine. Your delivery is where the leverage now lives.

> *"This is when I realized that my ads were shut off but I was still getting referrals every week. So, I doubled down on referrals. I built goodwill using customer feedback to update my product every two weeks. I also started a strong referral program with big incentives at the same time."*

> *"This is where most people mess up. They let their product slip and never recover."*

Critical warning: At Level 4, the trap is over-focusing on acquisition and letting product slip. **You can't recover from product decay at scale.** Goodwill compounds; so does ill-will.

See [[customer-referrals-system]].

---

## Level 5 — You Advertise In More Places In More Ways With More People

**Primary Action**: Advertise profitably using at least two methods on multiple platforms

You expand to new audiences on your best platform. You make ads with all placements and media types the platform supports. After your team gets consistent results, you expand again: another platform, lead-getter, or Core Four activity.

> *"For me, I hit two birds with one stone. I expanded my paid ads to include potential affiliates. And this paved the way for my affiliate programs."*

See [[more-better-new-framework]] for the scaling structure. Hormozi notes Level 5 is where affiliates first become feasible (you have the capacity, organizational maturity, and capital).

---

## Level 6 — You Hire Killers

**Primary Action**: Get battle-hardened executives and department heads to take over new advertising activities and channels

You don't hire "potential." You hire experienced leaders specializing in exactly what you want. Your executives grow departments specific to an advertising method or platform — without you.

> *"It took me three years to figure out two things. One, that I needed veteran executives with experience suited to my problems. And two, that they needed stronger incentives."*

By the time Hormozi realized this, he had **sold** the companies that needed it. Acquisition.com applied the lesson going forward.

> *"Once I started Acquisition.com I realized the power of expanding the pie to get more of the right people invested in winning. This is how we crossed $100M+ then $200M+ in portfolio revenue and beyond."*

The Level 6 unlock: equity-based incentives for executives that align long-term winning with personal upside.

This level is intentionally left as a placeholder. Hormozi commits to filling it in once he reaches it.

## Last Points — The Reality Check

> *"I know this looks clean. But it never is. Real business is messy. It takes a lot to find what audiences, lead magnets, methods, and platforms work best. And you can only find out what works if you try."*

> *"Nobody can ever know the absolute best thing to do. But I do know this: the more you advertise, the more people find out about the stuff you sell. The more people who know about the stuff you sell, the more people will buy it. This is the key to the $100M Leads Machine."*

## How To Identify Your Level

Quick diagnostic:

| Symptom | Likely Level |
|---|---|
| "I'm reaching out to friends/family/network but barely making sales" | Level 1 |
| "I have steady customers from my own efforts, but I'm working all hours" | Level 2 |
| "I just hired my first marketer/SDR/content person" | Level 3 |
| "I'm getting referrals consistently, time to focus on product or scale next" | Level 4 |
| "I'm on multiple platforms, multiple methods, I just need to scale them all" | Level 5 |
| "I'm hiring executives to run departments I built" | Level 6 |
| "I'm at a billion in revenue" | Hormozi will tell you :-) |

---

## What This Roadmap Solves

> *"This leads us to Section V: Get Started. I want to put everything together for you in a nice bow so you know exactly what to do next."*

The roadmap solves the "which lever next?" problem. Every business owner asks at every plateau: *what should I focus on?* The 7 Levels tell you.

- At Level 1, you don't need executives — you need warm outreach
- At Level 4, you don't need cold outreach — you need product quality
- At Level 6, you don't need to learn paid ads — you need executives who already do

Mismatching your level to your focus = wasted effort.

## Application For Caret/Colandrum

Pre-launch / early-launch stage = **Level 1.**

Path forward:
- Level 1: Warm outreach to agencies in network
- Level 2: Layer on LinkedIn content + cold email
- Level 3: Hire 1-2 SDRs + 1 content producer
- Level 4: Survey early customers, build product + referral program
- Level 5: Add affiliates (other agency-tool businesses)
- Level 6: Hire VP Marketing + VP Sales with PE-track-record
- Level 7: When Hormozi writes it

The plan = match focus to current level, don't skip ahead.
