# Framework: More, Better, New — Core Four On Steroids
tags: [leads, Hormozi, scaling, framework, rule-of-100, constraints]

## Summary / Definition

Hormozi's scaling system for any of the Core Four advertising methods. Three sequential moves:

1. **More** — Do more of what's working (Rule of 100)
2. **Better** — Find and break the constraint (one test per week)
3. **New** — New placements, new platforms, new core four activities

> *"If you are doing the core four and still not getting as many engaged leads as you want — there are two ways to boost any of the core four. I use these every time I want to increase the engaged lead flow in a portfolio company."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Leads*

## The Origin Story — Chiropractor Saturation

A business owner at one of Hormozi's events claimed he'd saturated the chiropractor niche.

> *"What're you doing in revenue?"*
> *"$2,000,000 a year."*
> *"How much do you spend on advertising?"*
> *"About $30,000 a month on Facebook."*
> *"What's your conversion rate from click to close?"*
> *"I don't know."*
> *"So you don't track overall throughput?"*
> *"I guess not."*
> *"Okay . . . What other platforms do you advertise on?"*
> *"None."*
> *"How much content do you make for chiropractors?"*
> *"None."*
> *"How much cold outreach do you do?"*
> *"None."*
> *"And the $30k you spend, on one platform, for a two million dollar business, saturated the $15.1B chiropractor industry? Does that sound reasonable?"*

A second chiropractor in the audience volunteered: *"I'm in the chiropractor niche too, and I spent $30k in advertising, across four platforms, last week..."*

> *"He got the point."*

See [[size-of-the-pie-fallacy]] for the full diagnosis.

## Step 1: MORE — The Rule Of 100

> *"Even with no improvements at all, if you double your inputs, you'll get more engaged leads. Make twice the reach outs, post twice the content, run twice the ads, double the ad spend, etc. You won't regret it. Unless, of course, you hate money."*

### The Rule Of 100 Statement

> *"You advertise your stuff by doing 100 primary actions every day, for one hundred days in a row. That's it."*

> *"I don't make many promises, but this is one. If you do 100 primary actions per day, and you do it for 100 days straight, you will get more engaged leads. Commit to the rule of 100 and you will never go hungry again."*

### Per Core Four Method

**Warm Reach Outs:** 100 reach outs per day
- Email, text, DM, calls, etc.

**Post Content:** 100 minutes/day making content
- Release at least one per day per platform
- As you get better, post more

**Cold Reach Outs:** 100 reach outs per day
- Email, text, DM, cold call, flyers
- Expect lower response rates → use automation

**Paid Ads:** 100 minutes/day making paid ads
- Direct response media ads, direct mail, seminars, podcast spots
- 100 days straight running them
- Use the daily budget from [[paid-ads-money-mechanics]] (reverse-engineered from sales goals)
- Aim for Client Financed Acquisition

## Step 2: BETTER — Constraint Identification + Testing

> *"Getting better gets you more leads for the same effort. We want that. And you can only get better by doing one thing — testing."*

### How To Find The Constraint

> *"Every action a lead takes before they become a customer is a potential 'drop-off' point. So I do the most testing at whatever step the most leads drop off. I call these 'constraints.' Constraints are the points where the smallest improvements create the biggest boost in results."*

Hormozi's worked example. Three-step funnel:

- **30% Optin** (give contact info)
- **5% Apply** ← constraint (biggest drop-off)
- **50% Schedule**

Now imagine improving each step by 5 percentage points:

| Step | Before | After | Change | Lift |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Optin | 30% | 35% | +5 pp | **+16%** (1.16×) |
| Apply | 5% | 10% | +5 pp | **+100%** (2×) |
| Schedule | 50% | 55% | +5 pp | **+10%** (1.1×) |

The same absolute improvement at the constraint produces **6× the lift** of improvement at the largest step.

### The Testing Discipline — One Test Per Week Per Platform

> *"I test one thing per week per platform. And I do it for four big reasons."*

1. **Multiple tests at once confuse causation** — you don't learn what worked
2. **Steps affect each other** — change Step 1, more people opt in but fewer apply = net loss. You wouldn't know unless isolated
3. **Forces prioritization** — infinite tests possible, finite time. Don't waste a test on "red vs bright red"
4. **Long enough to see real results** — too short = no data; too long = wasted opportunity. One week is Hormozi's sweet spot for his scale

### Monday Testing Cadence

Every Monday across every company:
- Run one split test per platform
- Give it a week
- Next Monday, do three things:
  1. **Pick the winner** for each platform test
  2. **Write down the result** in a log of all tests — start the next test a zillion improvements later, not at square one
  3. **Design the next test** to beat the current 'best' version

If you can't beat the current version in 4 tries (or 1 month), **move to the next constraint.**

## Step 3: NEW — When More + Better Stops Yielding

> *"After you've improved your marketing efforts through 'more' and 'better' the only thing you have left is — 'new places in new ways.'"*

### The Order Matters

> *"The order I pick my next 'new' comes down to one thing: what will get me the most leads for the amount of work?"*

The progression:

> **New placements → New platforms → New core four**

#### New Placements (Same Platform, New Format)

Most platforms have multiple placements:
- **Instagram**: Stories, Messenger ads, Posts, Reels
- **YouTube**: Shorts, longs, community posts
- **Facebook**: Newsfeed, Marketplace, Messenger, Reels

If you're succeeding with Reels on Instagram, try Stories. New placement, same platform.

#### New Platforms (Same Method, New Platform)

If short-video Reels works on Instagram, try Shorts on YouTube. Same content method, new audience.

If you've cracked Facebook Messenger DMs, try Instagram Messenger DMs.

#### New Core Four Activity (Entirely New Method)

If you're doing only paid ads, add content. If you're doing content + paid ads, add cold outreach. If you're doing content + paid ads + cold outreach, add affiliates.

### When To Use New

> *"When the returns you get from doing more+better are lower than what you could get from a new placement or new way of advertising."*

The decision rule:
- Calculate marginal return on more spending in current method
- Calculate expected return on a new placement/platform/method
- Pick the higher one

Most business owners stop way before this — they assume the niche is saturated. See [[size-of-the-pie-fallacy]].

## Why More + Better Compound

> *"First, you do way more of the advertising that works until it 'breaks.' Then, the next drop off point becomes obvious. Then you keep that level of advertising up while you go back, fix the constraint, and make it better. So really, better and more work with each other more than they work separately."*

The cycle:
1. Do more → discover a new constraint
2. Test to break the constraint → restore efficiency
3. Do more at the higher baseline → discover the next constraint
4. Repeat

Each cycle raises your floor.

## The Investment Question Hormozi Asks First

> *"The first question I usually ask myself before we invest in a company that needs to get more customers is 'What's stopping them from doing ten times what they're currently doing?'"*

This is the framework expressed as a single diagnostic. It almost always identifies the bottleneck immediately:
- "We don't have enough capacity to fulfill" → operations bottleneck
- "We don't have the cash to spend more on ads" → cash-flow bottleneck → Client Financed Acquisition fix
- "We don't know if it would work at 10×" → testing bottleneck → run the test

> *"Sometimes, nothing — so we just do more. Other times we just need to make something better first."*

## Application For Caret/Colandrum

1. **More**: 100 cold outreaches per day → 100 days straight before declaring it doesn't work
2. **Better**: Identify the constraint — is it open rate? Click rate? Demo show rate? Demo-to-close rate? — and test one variable per week at that step
3. **New**: Once cold outreach is maxed, add posting content. Once both are maxed, add paid ads to existing content. Once that's maxed, add affiliate program for adjacent agencies.

The progression — bootstrapped via cold outreach + content, then accelerated with paid ads, then leveraged via affiliates — mirrors Hormozi's own portfolio path.
