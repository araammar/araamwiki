# Framework: The One-Page Advertising Checklist
tags: [leads, Hormozi, execution, framework, planning]

## Summary / Definition

Hormozi's prescribed daily-execution plan. **One page.** Five steps. Fills out in ~5 minutes. Designed to leave no room for excuses, distractions, or delusions.

> *"Many skip planning, or worse, they write a one hundred page plan that never gets used. So skip the atrocious waste of time that is writing pages of baloney."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Leads*

---

## The 5 Steps

### Step 1: Pick The Type Of Engaged Lead To Get
- Customers, Affiliates, Employees, or Agencies

(You choose what type of lead-getter activity you're advertising for. Most people start with customers and stay there until they need help; then they switch to advertising for employees, agencies, or affiliates.)

### Step 2: Pick Rule Of 100 Or Open To Goal
- Commit to your daily advertising actions

See [[open-to-goal-vs-rule-of-100]] for the mode.

### Step 3: Fill Out The Advertising Checklist For That Daily Action
- Which Core Four method
- Which platform
- Which lead magnet or offer
- How many primary actions per day

### Step 4: Do This Daily Action Until You Have Enough Money To Afford Paying Someone Else To Do It

Keep doing the action yourself until profit allows hiring. This is the bridge from solo execution to leveraged execution.

### Step 5: When You Do, Go Back To Step 1

Make employees your new target lead type. Repeat steps 1-4 until you have the help you need. Then scale again.

---

## Why The Single Page

> *"Harness the power of laying out your action steps on a single page. It leaves little room for excuses, distractions, and delusions. You either did the stuff or you didn't."*

The constraint forces clarity. You can't hide behind 100 pages of strategy when the day's plan fits on one. There are exactly 5 decisions to make. Each decision is binary.

> *"You can fill out your one page advertising checklist in about five minutes. And once the naked truth stares back at you, all you have left is to do it."*

---

## The Bigger Logic

Hormozi designs this as the final operational artifact of the entire book. Every concept in $100M Leads collapses into:

- Pick a lead type (Customer / Affiliate / Employee / Agency)
- Pick a Core Four method (Warm / Cold / Content / Paid Ads)
- Pick a daily action volume (100 or Open to Goal)
- Execute

The other 600+ pages of the book exist to inform each of these 4 decisions. Once they're made, the work is just doing.

## Sample Checklist (Hypothetical)

For an early-stage solo founder:

```
GOAL: Customers
MODE: Rule of 100
METHOD: Warm Outreach
PLATFORM: Phone + Email
OFFER: Free 30-day pilot
DAILY ACTION: 100 warm reach-outs per day
DURATION: 100 days
```

For a scaling business at Level 4:

```
GOAL: Customers + Affiliates (two checklists, parallel)
MODE: Open to Goal
METHOD: Posting Content + Paid Ads
PLATFORM: LinkedIn + Facebook
OFFER: Lead magnet (free audit)
DAILY OUTCOMES: 30 booked demos, 2 new affiliates signed
```

## Connection To The Habit Stack

The checklist is the *what*. Hormozi's high-ROI habit stack is the *when and how*:

- Wake 4-5 AM
- Get right to work (no rituals)
- No meetings until noon
- Work the one-page plan in that block

After the dedicated block: handle fires, talk to humans, manage the day-to-day.

---

## Why This Beats A 100-Page Plan

> *"Reading doesn't get people interested in the stuff you sell . . . advertising does. If you're not telling anyone about the stuff you sell, then you aren't getting anyone interested in the stuff you sell. Period."*

A 100-page plan is reading + writing. A one-page checklist is action.

The book's closing argument: planning is a trap if it replaces doing. Make planning small. Make doing big.
