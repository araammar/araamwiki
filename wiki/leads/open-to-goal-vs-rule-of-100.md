# Concept: Open To Goal vs Rule Of 100
tags: [leads, Hormozi, execution, mindset, work-volume]

## Summary / Definition

The execution-mindset hierarchy. Two work-volume modes:

1. **Rule of 100** — Do 100 primary actions per day, no matter the result
2. **Open To Goal** — Work until you hit a specific outcome, no matter how many actions it takes

> *"Open to goal is like the rule of 100 . . . but for the big kids."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Leads*

---

## The 300 Flyers Story

June 2014. Hormozi's first gym. Paid ads were getting expensive — leads dropped.

A mentor who ran tanning salons suggested flyers: *"Before all this fancy internet stuff, flyers crushed for us."*

Hormozi printed **300 flyers** and put them on cars near the gym.

The next day, the phone rang.

> *"Hey, you put a flyer on my car —"*

Hormozi's heart raced. It worked!

> *"— yea, you scratched my Mercedes . . . you're gonna need to pay for —"*

He panicked, hung up, let the next call ring. The Mercedes owner gave up. **That was the only call he got.**

A few weeks later, frustrated, Hormozi called the mentor:

> *"What was your test size?"*
> *"I put out 300."*
> *"Shoot, you only put out 300? Hard to know if anything works with such a small number . . . I test with 5000. Then when we find a winner, we put out 5000 per day, every day, for a month . . ."*

5,000 per day. 17× Hormozi's entire campaign. **In a single day.**

> *"I mean what kind of response did you think you were gonna get? If we get half a percent, that's decent. If we get one percent, that's a winner. With 300 flyers, half a percent would be like one and a half people."*

> *"I had nothing to say. He was right. I felt like a fool."*

### The Permanent Lesson

> *"I promised myself I would never let effort be the reason anything didn't work for me. It could be something else. The offer. The copy. The image. The targeting. The media. The platform. The position of the moon. But not. my. effort."*

> *"Those 300 measly flyers taught me a mondo lesson. I did the right stuff, but I didn't do it enough times. I lacked what can be described in a single word: volume."*

## The Common Volume Misunderstanding

> *"Most people dramatically underestimate the volume it takes to make advertising work. They're not doing half as much or a third as much as is required. In fact, they're doing dramatically less."*

> *"I was doing 1/1500th the level of effort required to make a flyer campaign work — I just didn't know it."*

> *"I hear this all the time. 'Alex, I reached out to 100 people over the last six weeks, I only got one customer, it doesn't work.' Response: 'You did 1/42 of the amount of work required. It was 100 per day, not 100 over time.'"*

The math: 100 reach outs per day × 42 days (6 weeks) = 4,200 expected. They did 100.

## Rule Of 100 (The Baseline)

The structure introduced in [[more-better-new-framework|More-Better-New]]:

> *"You advertise your stuff by doing 100 primary actions every day, for one hundred days in a row."*

The promise:
> *"If you do 100 primary actions per day, and you do it for 100 days straight, you will get more engaged leads. Commit to the rule of 100 and you will never go hungry again."*

---

## Open To Goal (The Next Level)

> *"A very successful gym chain allowed their sales managers to make their own schedules. But there was a catch — they had to sign up five new members per day no matter what. So if they did it by lunch, they could cut out early. But if it took 18 hours, so be it. They called this type of work schedule 'open-to-goal.'"*

The structure:
- Define a **goal** (5 new members; 5 customers; 3 booked calls)
- Commit to the work until the goal is hit
- Time doesn't matter; the outcome does

> *"It's like the rule of 100 . . . but for the big kids. You don't just commit to doing something a specific number of times . . . you commit to the work until you hit a specific number of outcomes — no matter what."*

The implication: **you might do 50 actions one day (if you hit the goal early) or 5,000 the next.** The number floats; the outcome is fixed.

> *"It might mean only doing something fifty times to get the desired result. Or, like the flyers, five thousand times, every day, for years."*

---

## The Choice — Rule Of 100 Or Open To Goal?

Hormozi's framing:

> *"If you want to take your advertising to the next level — work until the job is done. Give up the idea of 'doing your best.' Instead, do what is required. And sometimes that means your best just needs to get better."*

When to use which:

| Mode | When |
|---|---|
| **Rule of 100** | Learning, establishing baseline, when the relationship between actions and outcomes is unclear |
| **Open to Goal** | After you know your ratios — you can predict how many actions = your goal — and want to scale impact per day |

Both modes share one principle: **the daily commitment is non-negotiable.** Rule of 100 commits to actions. Open to Goal commits to outcomes. Both produce results no other mode does.

## Hormozi's Personal "High-ROI Habit Stack"

> *"If I had to pick the three habits that best served me in my life — they would be:*
> *1. Waking up early (4-5 am) — Pro tip, this actually means going to bed early...*
> *2. Getting right to work — No rituals. No routines. I drink coffee and get to work.*
> *3. No meetings until noon — No interruptions. Nothing. Fully focused work time."*

### The Logic

> *"There's magic in a long stretch of uninterrupted work immediately after a long stretch of uninterrupted sleep."*

The compounding effect:
- Most productive hours in a row
- Most productive work of the day
- Nothing getting in the way
- Every. Single. Day.

> *"How can you lose?"*

### The "Throttle Back" Permission

> *"For those of you thinking 'Wait! That's more than twelve hours of work a day!' You're right. I'm playing to win. But if it overwhelms you at first, I get it. Just throttle it back a few hours and then work your way up."*

### The Mantra

> *"Do more than they do, and you will have more than they have."*

## Application

Pick which mode you're in:

### Rule of 100 (most readers, most days):
1. Pick which Core Four method
2. Define a "primary action" (1 cold email, 1 warm message, 1 content piece, 1 ad test)
3. Do 100 of them every day for 100 days

### Open to Goal (advanced):
1. Pick the daily outcome (5 customers, 10 booked calls, $1,000 revenue)
2. Commit to working until you hit it
3. Work the high-ROI habit stack to maximize uninterrupted output

Hormozi's recommendation is to **start with Rule of 100** until your action-to-outcome ratio is clear, then **upgrade to Open to Goal.**
