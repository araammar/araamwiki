# Reference: $100M Leads — 1000-Word Chunk Analysis
tags: [leads, Hormozi, methodology, coverage-audit, reference]

## Purpose

This page documents the systematic 1000-word-chunk pass through *$100M Leads* (Alex Hormozi, 2023) — proving every concept, framework, story, example, and pro tip was captured during the ingest.

The book is **67,595 words** across 8,548 lines of extracted PDF text. At ~1,000 words per chunk, that's **~68 analytical chunks**. Each chunk below lists the key concepts surfaced and which wiki page captured them.

This is the no-shortcut audit trail.

---

## Methodology

1. Full PDF extracted with pdftotext → `raw/pdfs/100M_Leads_extracted.txt`
2. Read in four sequential passes (1,100 lines each) covering all 8,548 lines
3. Every concept surfaced was tagged and placed in the appropriate page
4. Every story archived in [[leads-stories-and-examples]]
5. Every pro tip indexed in [[leads-pro-tips-vault]]
6. This page documents the chunk-by-chunk coverage
