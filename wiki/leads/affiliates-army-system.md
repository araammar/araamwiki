## The Prestige Labs Launch Story

December 1, 2018. Hormozi was about to roll out Prestige Labs to gym owner affiliates. Preparation:
- Over a year of work
- **$1M+** custom-built affiliate software and training
- **$3M** in inventory bought ahead of launch

> *"And, in just a few hours, we would roll it out to our gym owner affiliates. I felt like a kid on Christmas Eve."*

**Launch day**: Hormozi finished the 2-hour presentation soaked in sweat.

**3 weeks later**: $150,000 in total sales. $3M worth of product sat in an air-conditioned warehouse.

> *"It didn't work."*

At that rate (including operating costs + affiliate payouts), it would take 5 years to break even. The premium product would expire well before then.

> *"Who am I to think we would sell all that stuff? I just wasted MILLIONS. How could I be so stupid?"*

**Week 4** — something wild happened:

| Day | Sales |
|---|---|
| Monday | **$100,000** |
| Tuesday | **$110,000** |
| Wednesday | **$92,000** |
| Week 4 total | **>$450,000** |

The trend continued: $429K, $383K, $411K, $452K. Averaged **300+ orders/day across 400+ active affiliates.**

> *"The best part is, I didn't advertise or sell any of the products at all. No paid ads. No sales team. Nothing. The affiliates did everything — and the affiliate machine I built still prints money to this day."*

## Why Affiliates Are The Highest Leverage

> *"Each affiliate you get adds another stream of leads and customers."*

**Scenario 1 (no affiliates):**
- Sell 10 customers/month worth $10,000 each
- $100,000/mo cap → $1.2M/yr
- **Low Leverage**

**Scenario 2 (affiliates):**
- Same effort gets you 10 *affiliates*/month instead of 10 customers
- Each affiliate brings one $10,000 customer/month
- Month 1: $100K. Month 2: $200K. Month 3: $300K (additive)
- 12 months: **$7.8M**
- **High Leverage**

### The ALAN 3-Level Affiliate Pyramid

Hormozi's software company ALAN grew via three affiliate levels:

1. **Agency super-affiliates** → bring agency leads
2. **Agencies** → bring local business leads
3. **Local businesses** → bring end consumer leads

One super-affiliate's compounding math:
- Adds 10 agencies/mo
- Each agency brings ~5 local businesses/mo (total: 50)
- Local businesses bring ~50 leads each (total: 2,500)
- ALAN worked those leads at $5/lead → $12,500/mo per super-affiliate
- Month 2: $25,000/mo. Month 3: $37,500/mo. (Compounding)

> *"With only a few agency super-affiliates, we scaled to $1,700,000 per month within six months of launching."*

## How Affiliates Differ From Referrals

| | Referrers | Affiliates |
|---|---|---|
| Who they are | Your customers | Independent businesses |
| Why they refer | Goodwill | Money or free stuff |
| Volume per person | 1-3 friends | Sometimes thousands of customers |
| Structure | Casual | Formal agreement |
| Time investment | None ongoing | Recruitment + training + integration |
| Leverage | High | Highest |

> *"Affiliates seem like referrals on the outside, but are much different under the hood."*

## The 6 Steps

### Step 1: Find Your Ideal Affiliates

> *"The ideal affiliate has a business with a warm audience full of people like your customers."*

Ask:
- What do my best customers buy? → Who provides that?
- Where do they go? → Surrounding businesses?
- What do they like to do? → Service providers?
- For B2C: What businesses do their *employers* operate?

In a nutshell: **Who's got my leads?**

#### Hormozi's ALAN Example

When starting ALAN, agency owners were the ideal affiliate. He made a list of 200 products/services for agencies and categorized them:
- Softwares
- Products
- Equipment
- Services
- Groups they belong to
- Events they attend

> *"If you find a business that falls into multiple categories, there's a high chance they've got lots of good leads for you and that they'd make a great affiliate."*

### Step 2: Make Them An Offer

You advertise the affiliate offer the same way as any other offer: callout + value + CTA. The twist: **affiliates are businesses, so you're offering them a new way to make money.**

#### Callout Types

- Owner themselves — *ATTENTION SPA OWNERS*
- Their customers — *Do you work with busy professionals who spend all day in meetings?*
- Their promise — *To the heroes who heal the stress of others...*
- Their products — *If you sell lotions or scented oils this is for you...*
- Your own customers — *Do you know anyone who owns a spa?*

#### Value Structure

> *"Most affiliate money making offers show value like this: Make more money from your current customers and get more leads than your current offer (**dream outcome**) . . . with a high chance of working since your customers already want the product (**perceived likelihood**) . . . without needing to build, deliver, or provide customer support for the product yourself (**effort and sacrifice**) . . . so you can start selling it tomorrow (**time delay**)."*

The Value Equation, applied to affiliates.

### Step 3: Qualify Them

> *"Potential affiliates become actual affiliates when they understand and agree to your terms. And — just like customers — we want to get them their first win as fast as possible."*

Get them invested. Two ways:

#### Way 1: Make Them A Customer
They buy (and ideally use) the product to keep affiliate status. Lowest-barrier investment.

> *"The more money an affiliate invests in your product, the more money they make. If they don't believe in your stuff enough to buy it, they probably shouldn't sell it."*

#### Way 2: Make Them An Expert (Certification)
They pay for onboarding/training that certifies them. The product purchase can be credited toward the certification.

Hormozi's pricing rule:

> *"I recommend 10-20% of what the average active affiliate makes in the first twelve months. So if your average affiliate makes $40,000 per year selling your stuff, then charge $4000-$8000 to onboard and train them."*

The 10-20% range maximizes the number of people who become *active* affiliates:
- Too low → not invested
- Too high → not enough sign-ups

### Step 4: Figure Out What To Pay Them

#### What They Get Paid For

Two basic things:
1. **New customers**
2. **Repeat customers**

Over time, you can pay for earlier-funnel events (lead magnets downloaded, appointments set, etc.) — but start with these two.

#### How Much They Get Paid — The 3-Tier Payout

Pay affiliates based on your **maximum allowable CAC.**

Example: Single-use product at $200, $40 cost to fulfill, target 3:1 LTGP:CAC ratio.
- $200 - $40 = $160 left
- $120 to business, $40 to affiliate
- **Max affiliate CAC = $40**

Hormozi structures payouts in 3 tiers:

| Tier | % of Max CAC | Payout | Condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | 25% | $10 | Anyone who agrees to initial terms (sign up + buy products/certification) |
| **Tier 2** | 50% | $20 | Activated (finished certification + launched + completed initial milestones) |
| **Tier 3** | 100% | $40 | Sustained performance (e.g., 5 customers/mo on subscription) |

#### The Hidden Side Benefit

> *"This tiered method also has a hidden and very profitable side effect. The average payout is much less than your maximum allowable CAC."*

Example mix: 20% from Tier 1, 20% from Tier 2, 60% from Tier 3:
- Blended payout = $30 instead of max $40
- LTGP:CAC moves from 3:1 to **4:1**
- Cutting marketing costs 33% → typically 10-20% more net profit at year-end

### Step 5: Get Them Advertising — The Launch

> *"Treat them like customers. Give them something good, fast. And nothing does that for affiliates like big launches and lots of cash."*

The launch framework: **Whisper → Tease → Shout.** See [[whisper-tease-shout-launch]] for the full mechanics.

### Step 6: Keep Them Advertising — Integration

The strategy to *start* them differs from the strategy to *keep* them. **Integration** is the long-term play.

Three integration strategies, easy to hard:

#### Strategy 1: They Give Your Lead Magnet Away With Their Stuff
Their core offer includes your lead magnet as a value-add.

Examples:
- **Sample/Trial**: Personal training studio gives a free massage (yours) with every training package
- **Reveal Problem**: Free posture assessment with every training package
- **Multi-Step**: First of 3 treatment steps free

**Hormozi did this with gyms**: every new gym member got a free nutrition consult; Prestige Labs upsold supplements at the consult.

#### Strategy 2: They Sell Your Lead Magnet
Affiliate sells your lead magnet at a discounted price → keeps the cash.

> *"Giving affiliates all the cash from selling a lead magnet you fulfill becomes all profit and no work for them — an attractive proposition for any business."*

You make money on the upsell to your core offer.

**Hormozi's gym example**: gyms sold a nutrition consult with Prestige Labs at $99-$199 and kept all of it. Prestige then upsold supplements during the consult.

#### Strategy 3: They Sell Your Core Offer Directly

Affiliate sells your entire program/service and keeps a higher % of LTGP.

**Hormozi's gym example**: taught gyms to hold nutrition consults with white-labeled products, then upsell supplements directly and split the money.

> *"All three strategies work. They're just different."*

Prestige Labs uses Strategy 1 (twice/year big event) and Strategy 3 (ongoing).

## Three Case Studies

### Case Study #1: National Tax Preparation Services ($50M Friend Business)

Helps people starting businesses for the first time. Doesn't compete with Legalzoom.

Strategy: partner with people who train new entrepreneurs. Offers every affiliate's customer a **free LLC setup** (high-cost lead magnet).

**Launch**: big seminar to affiliate's audience. People take the free LLC.

**Integrate**: affiliates integrate the free LLC into their core offer. The team gets on phone with the new business owners, sells bookkeeping, tax prep, etc.

> *"He hasn't spent a dollar on paid ads. His true advertising costs are two things. One, delivering his free lead magnet (the LLC setup). And two, paying a percentage of every first sale to the affiliates that sent them."*

### Case Study #2: Prestige Labs (Hormozi's Supplement Company)

Gym Launch sells gym owners. Prestige sells supplements for active adults. Gym Launch is the perfect affiliate.

**Launch**: gym owners get advertising materials for a free 28-day challenge. They use warm outreach + content. Owners sell supplements at the challenge. Everyone wins.

**Integrate**: Every new gym member sees a nutrition orientation. Owner sells $50-$1000 of supplements at the orientation.

The math:
- 4,000 gyms × 14 new sales/mo × $200 avg order = **a lot of money every month**

### Case Study #3: Chiropractor Portfolio Company

Strategy: high-volume businesses with people who need adjustments. Gyms fit the bill.

**Launch**: gym owner promotes a 3-hour workshop on correct exercises and posture. Free or $29-$99/person. Chiropractor splits with gym owner.

> *"Hint: if you give the affiliate 100% of the money, they'll want to do it more."*

Math: gym gets 30 people at $99 = **$2,970 profit for zero work** beyond emails and posts. Chiropractor pitches services and gets new patients.

**Integrate**: Chiropractor convinces gym owner to include 1-2 free adjustments with every new gym membership. Win-win: gym membership becomes more valuable, every new member becomes a chiro lead.

> *"They repeat this process with thirty gyms, and get more patients than they can handle."*

## The Returns Math

Unlike Core Four, affiliate ROI compares CAC-to-get-affiliate with LTGP-from-customers-affiliate-brings.

### Worked Example

- **CAC for affiliate**: $4,000
- **Avg affiliate sells**: $10,000/mo × 12 months = **$120,000 total sales**
- **Gross margin**: 75% → $90,000 gross profit from those sales
- **Pay affiliate**: 40% × $90,000 = $36,000
- **Net to business**: $120,000 - $30,000 cost - $36,000 payout = **$54,000**
- **Ratio**: $54,000 / $4,000 CAC = **13.5:1**

> *"Not too shabby."*

### Improvement Levers When Below 3:1

1. **Lower CAC** — better ads, offer, sales process for getting affiliates
2. **Increase LTGP AND lower CAC** — better launch process
3. **Increase LTGP** — better integration

> *"The way you do business, your leadership, and the value of your product all come into play. You are only as good as the goodwill you have with your affiliate partners."*
