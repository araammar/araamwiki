## The Four-Question Framework

Hormozi reduces all of business growth to two levers:

1. **Get more customers**
2. **Make them worth more**

This book focuses entirely on #1. And to get more customers, you need:

1. **More leads**
2. **Better leads**
3. **Cheaper leads**
4. **Reliably (think 'from lots of places')**

> *"Bottom line: All else being equal . . . when you double your leads, you double your business."*

## The Origin Story (March 2017)

Hormozi opens with the disaster that birthed this book. Two gyms refunded $150,000 worth of customers in one week — one owner stood on a chair telling everyone to refund and go home; the other told customers to refund and pay him half price instead.

Leila said: *"It's a hundred and fifty grand. We won't be able to pay my friends."*

The next morning at 3 AM, he sat scribbling ideas. The breakthrough came not from any of his planned actions but from a single panicked phone call to a Boise gym owner who insisted on being helped. Hormozi quoted **$6,000** as a "biggest number I could think of to get him off the phone." The owner said yes.

> *"What. I stood there slack-jawed, frozen in disbelief. Six. Thousand. Dollars."*

Then he raised it: $8,000. Yes. $10,000. Yes. In a single day he sold all 8 planned gym launches as licensing instead — **$60,000 in pure-profit cash.**

> *"I think we're still in the gym business . . . I think we were just doing it wrong."*

That month: **$215,000 in profit**. First full year: **$6,820,000 revenue**. Next year: **$25,900,000 / $17,000,000 profit**. Built into 4,500+ gyms, Prestige Labs, ALAN (sold for big money), and eventually Acquisition.com — a portfolio doing **$250,000,000+/yr** generating **20,000+ leads/day**.

> *"All this was because of a girl who believed in me, a credit card, and the ability to get leads."*

See [[leads-stories-and-examples]] for the full archived narrative.

## The Core Mental Models

The body of this section explores each model in depth. Quick orientation:

### 1. Lead vs. Engaged Lead
- **Lead** = a person you can contact
- **Engaged Lead** = someone who *shows interest* in what you sell

**Engaged leads are the true output of advertising.** See [[engaged-leads-definition]].

### 2. Core Offer vs. Lead Magnet
- **Core offer** = your main product/service for money
- **Lead magnet** = complete solution to a narrow problem, given free/cheap, that reveals a bigger problem solved by your core offer

See [[lead-magnets-framework]].

### 3. The Core Four
The only four ways one person can let other people know about anything:

| | 1-to-1 (private) | 1-to-many (public) |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm audience** | Warm Outreach | Posting Content |
| **Cold audience** | Cold Outreach | Paid Ads |

See [[core-four-overview]].

### 4. Lead Getters
Other people who do the Core Four on your behalf, in four flavors:
- **Customers** → referrals
- **Employees** → systematic advertising
- **Agencies** → skill transfer
- **Affiliates** → independent advertising armies

See the Lead Getters section pages.

### 5. The Rule of 100 / Open To Goal
- **Rule of 100**: 100 primary actions per day for 100 days straight
- **Open To Goal**: work until you hit a specific outcome, no matter what

See [[open-to-goal-vs-rule-of-100]].

### 6. The 7-Level Roadmap
From "friends know about your stuff" to "executives run your billion-dollar advertising machine without you." See [[seven-levels-of-advertisers]].

### 7. The Many Sided Die
The closing parable. The only way to lose is to quit. See [[many-sided-die-parable]].

## The Business Model Behind The Book

> *"I give this book and the course that comes with it for free (or at cost) in hopes of earning your trust."*

Hormozi's stated model:
1. Provide better free products than the marketplace's paid products
2. Earn the trust of entrepreneurs who make over $1,000,000/year in profit
3. Invest in those entrepreneurs to fast-track their growth
4. Help everyone else for free, for good

Acquisition.com takes equity in profitable bootstrapped companies and uses portfolio resources to scale them. This isn't a coaching company; it's a private equity model.
