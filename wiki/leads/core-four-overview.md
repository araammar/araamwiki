# Framework: The Core Four — The Only 4 Ways To Advertise
tags: [leads, Hormozi, framework, advertising, core-four]

## Summary / Definition

The structural heart of $100M Leads. Hormozi reduces all advertising to a single 2×2 matrix:

| | **1-to-1 (Private)** | **1-to-many (Public)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm audience** (people who know you) | Warm Outreach | Posting Content |
| **Cold audience** (strangers) | Cold Outreach | Paid Ads |

> *"These are the only four things you can do to let other people know about the stuff you sell."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Leads*

Every other lead-generation tactic in business is a variation of one of these four.

---

## The Two Underlying Axes

### Audience Type — Warm vs Cold

**Warm audience** = people who have given you **permission to contact them**:
- Friends, family
- Followers
- Current customers
- Previous customers
- Contacts of all kinds

**Cold audience** = people who have **not** given you permission:
- Other people's audiences
- Purchased contact lists
- Lists you build by scraping
- Platform-mediated access (paid ad placements)

> *"The difference matters because it changes how we advertise to them."*

The trust gap is the operating difference. Warm requires less convincing, gets higher conversion, but has hard size limits. Cold has unlimited supply, but starts every interaction at trust zero.

### Communication Mode — 1-to-1 vs 1-to-many

**Private (1-to-1)** = one person gets a message at a time
- Phone call, email, text, DM
- Even a 10,000-person email blast is still "1-to-1, done really fast by machines"

**Public (1-to-many)** = many people get the message at once
- Social media posts
- Billboards
- Podcasts
- Paid ad placements

> *"Automation, which we cover later, is one of the many ways we can get leads on steroids. Like audiences, the difference between public and private communication matters because they change how we advertise."*

## The 4 Methods Combined

### Quadrant 1: Warm Outreach (Warm + Private)

Reach out 1-to-1 to people who know you. Highest conversion rate, smallest possible audience.

> *"It's the cheapest and easiest way to find people interested in the stuff you sell. It's super effective — and most businesses don't do it."*

See [[warm-outreach-system]] for the 10-step framework.

### Quadrant 2: Posting Content (Warm + Public)

Post publicly to your existing audience. Grows your warm audience for future use.

> *"What I didn't understand was — the content you create isn't the compounding asset — the audience is."*

See [[content-unit-framework]] for the Hook/Retain/Reward mechanics.

### Quadrant 3: Cold Outreach (Cold + Private)

Reach out 1-to-1 to strangers. Requires list-building + personalization + volume.

> *"Cold outreach takes a long time. At least, it did for me."*

See [[cold-outreach-system]] for the 3-problem / 3-solution framework.

### Quadrant 4: Paid Ads (Cold + Public)

Pay platforms to put your message in front of strangers en masse. Fastest scale, biggest risk.

> *"Paid ads are the fastest way to scale how many leads you get."*

See [[paid-ads-callout-value-cta]] for the ad-creation system and [[paid-ads-money-mechanics]] for the economics.

## Why Only Four?

> *"Two types of audiences (warm + cold) × two communication modes (private + public) = the only four things one person can do to let other people know about anything."*

This is a complete taxonomy. Every "new" lead generation tactic you encounter is one of these four with a different label:
- "Influencer marketing" = Posting Content (their audience) or Paid Ads (you pay them)
- "PR" = Posting Content (other media outlets' audiences)
- "SEO" = Posting Content (search engines)
- "Door knocking" = Cold Outreach (1-to-1, in person)
- "Direct mail" = Cold Outreach (1-to-1, asynchronous)

Once you internalize the 4, you stop chasing the latest tactic. You pick which quadrant you're working in and optimize.

---

## The Order Hormozi Recommends

The book teaches the Core Four in this order, for a reason:

1. **Warm Outreach first** — smallest skill investment, fastest first lead, builds reps
2. **Post Content next** — compounding asset that makes everything else easier
3. **Cold Outreach third** — once you have warm conversations to draw from
4. **Paid Ads last** — costs money, learns most from prior 3

> *"If you start on your own, you tend to get your first referrals before you start building a big team."*

The progression matches the natural growth of a bootstrapped business:
- Level 1: You do warm outreach yourself
- Level 2: You add content
- Level 3: You hire help
- Level 4-7: Lead getters and executives scale all four

See [[seven-levels-of-advertisers]] for the full roadmap.

## How The Four Compound

> *"All the advertising methods compound together. The money, systems, and experience you earned from the prior method will help you master the next."*

> *"A business that posts free content and runs paid ads will get more out of their ads and their content than a business that does only one or the other. A business that does cold outreach and makes content will get more from their cold outreach and work their warm leads better than one that does only one. Every combination of the core four advertising activities boosts each other in some way."*

This is critical: **the methods are multiplicative, not additive.** Free content makes cold outreach more credible (the prospect can find you). Cold outreach generates content material (recent past stories). Paid ads to existing content get higher conversion than paid ads to a landing page. Warm outreach to content viewers converts better than to strangers.

## Hormozi's Personal Track Record By Method

> *"As a personal note, I've done 'em all. I built my first business off posting content and warm outreach. I built my gyms off free content and paid ads. I built Gym Launch off paid ads and cold outreach. I built Prestige Labs off affiliates. I built ALAN off paid ads and affiliates. I built Acquisition.com off posting content."*

Every business he's built has been on at least 2 of the Core Four. None has been on all 4 at the start — but all eventually scale to use all 4.

---

## The Picking-A-Method Heuristic

> *"Whenever I build a business I think about it this way — after I do warm outreach to get my pool of customers going — if I have more time than money, I move to posting content. If I have more money than time, I go with cold outreach or running ads."*

- **More time than money** → Posting Content (long-term, compounding)
- **More money than time** → Cold Outreach OR Paid Ads (faster scale)

Warm Outreach is always the starting point regardless of resources.

---

## The Final Mantra

> *"But remember, you only need to do one to get engaged leads. So, just pick one. Then, max it out. Do more. Do better. Do new."*

Don't try to do all four at the start. Pick one. Get it working. Then layer the next.
