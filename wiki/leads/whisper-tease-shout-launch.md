# Framework: The Whisper-Tease-Shout Launch
tags: [leads, Hormozi, launch, framework, affiliates, marketing]

## Summary / Definition

Hormozi's universal launch framework. Three escalating phases of pre-launch communication:

1. **Whisper** — Build curiosity. Hint without revealing.
2. **Tease** — Reveal product details. Establish value.
3. **Shout** — Hit them with CTAs. Urgency. Scarcity. Bonuses.

> *"I can't remember where I first heard this, but the name stuck."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Leads*

The framework was introduced in the [[affiliates-army-system|Affiliates chapter]] because it's the best activation mechanism for affiliates — but Hormozi notes it works for any launch.

---

## The Underlying Logic — Curiosity → Value → Action

Each phase maps to a different psychological state in the audience:

| Phase | Audience State | What You Communicate |
|---|---|---|
| Whisper | Curiosity | "Something is coming. You want to know what." |
| Tease | Evaluation | "Here's why it matters and what it does." |
| Shout | Decision | "Take action now. Here's how." |

This mirrors the [[paid-ads-callout-value-cta|Callout + Value + CTA]] structure of an ad — but stretched over weeks or months instead of seconds.

---

## Foundational Principle: Do The Work Ahead Of Time

> *"Good launches have the work done ahead of time. So do all the work for them. Then, they can plug and play."*

This applies most directly to affiliate launches but also general launches: pre-create assets, scripts, swipe copy, ad creatives, social posts, etc. — so anyone helping you launch has zero friction.

---

## Phase 1: Whisper

> *"Think 'Call Outs.' Like an ad, the key to the whisper phase is curiosity."*

Keep the product mysterious. Hint at how big a deal it is. Show behind-the-scenes.

### When To Start

> *"If you have something in the works, you can start the whisper phase a few years out."*

> *"The further out you start whispering, the bigger deal it becomes to your audience."*

The principle: **the longer something appears to take, the more an audience values it.** All else equal, a product taking 10 years to make is valued more than one taking 10 days.

> *"Show your work."*

### Why Curiosity Works

> *"Curiosity comes from wanting to know what happens next. So embed questions about the product in their minds. We need to tell them about something they want to know more about, then say . . . not yet."*

This is the same retention mechanic as the [[content-unit-framework|Content Unit's Retain step]] — unresolved questions create wanting.

### Whisper Tactics — Hormozi's Book Launch Example

During the whisper phase of his $100M Leads launch:
- Posted content
- Reached out to friends
- Emailed his list
- Told potential affiliates about major updates
- Showed which draft he was on
- Took photos behind the scenes of printing drafts
- Showed the many versions of frameworks he drew
- Shared videos of editing the book early morning and late at night

All of it made people who want leads get curious and pay attention.

### Whisper Cadence

| Time From Launch | Frequency |
|---|---|
| 2+ years out → 60 days out | Whisper every 4-6 weeks |
| 60 days out → 30 days out | Whisper every 2-3 weeks |
| 30 days out | Switch to Tease |

---

## Phase 2: Tease

> *"Think 'Elements Of Value.' It's time to start satisfying all the curiosity you created during the whisper phase."*

Now reveal:
- The product
- The launch date
- The value elements (use [[what-who-when-framework]])

### What To Show

> *"Use the What-Who-When Framework from the paid ads chapter."*

The full value equation, applied:
- **What**: dream outcome, perceived likelihood, time delay, effort & sacrifice
- **Who**: how others will perceive them after using it
- **When**: past pain → present problem → future relief

### Tease Tactics — Hormozi's Book Launch Example

> *"I was more specific and revealed more 'hard' information about the book. I started advertising how the book satisfied the dream outcome of limitless leads. Of doing less work, and getting it done faster than they could imagine. I also showed dozens of examples using the book to its potential."*

### Tease Cadence

| Time From Launch | Frequency |
|---|---|
| 30 days out → 14 days out | Tease once per week |
| 14 days out → 3 days out | Tease twice per week |
| 3 days out | Switch to Shout |

---

## Phase 3: Shout

> *"Think 'Call to Action.' Give specific actions for the audience to take when the product launches."*

Hit them with the four enhancement levers (from [[grand-slam-offer]]):
- **Bonuses** — being one of the first
- **Scarcity** — limited slots
- **Urgency** — time-bound offer
- **Guarantees** — risk reversal

### Shout Tactics — Hormozi's Book Launch Example

> *"I gave specific calls to action. Short, sweet, clear reminders to register for the book launch. I reminded everyone of the exclusive bonuses only for people who bought during the launch."*

### Shout Cadence

| Time From Launch | Frequency |
|---|---|
| 3 days out | At least 2× per day |
| Day of, leading to launch | Every few hours |
| 2 hours out | Every 30 minutes |
| Launch | Continuous |

---

## The Full Cadence Summary

| Phase | Window | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Whisper (early) | 2+ years → 60 days | Every 4-6 weeks |
| Whisper (late) | 60 → 30 days | Every 2-3 weeks |
| Tease (early) | 30 → 14 days | Once per week |
| Tease (late) | 14 → 3 days | Twice per week |
| Shout (early) | 3 days → launch day | Twice per day |
| Shout (mid) | Launch day | Every few hours |
| Shout (late) | 2 hours pre-launch | Every 30 minutes |

The frequency rises **exponentially** as the launch approaches. By launch hour you're essentially live-broadcasting.

---

## Why This Works For Affiliates

> *"I put it in the affiliates section because I haven't found a better way to activate affiliates than launches."*

Affiliates are independent businesses. They have their own customers, their own ad fatigue. To get them to push your launch hard, you need:

1. **A clear timeline** — they can build campaigns around dates
2. **Pre-built assets** — they don't have to create everything
3. **Escalating momentum** — they ride the rising wave
4. **A clear payoff** — a defined launch day where conversions concentrate

Whisper-Tease-Shout provides all four.

## Hormozi's Personal Universal Application

> *"Note: this is how you launch anything, not just affiliates."*

The framework applies to:
- Product launches
- Service launches
- Event launches
- Content launches (book, podcast season, video series)
- Affiliate program launches
- Internal launches (a new initiative for a team)

## Application For Caret/Colandrum

A V2 launch with affiliate involvement:

### Whisper (Months 6-1 Before Launch)
- Behind-the-scenes posts of new feature development
- Hint at "the next thing" without naming it
- Email list with "something big coming"
- Affiliate teaser: "early access for partners only"

### Tease (Days 30-3 Before Launch)
- Reveal product name + key feature
- Case studies of beta users
- Demos showing value elements
- Affiliate-specific commission preview
- Build the affiliate cohort that will push the launch

### Shout (Days 3 → Launch Hour)
- Bonuses for launch-week customers
- Scarcity ("first 50 spots")
- Urgency (price increase after launch week)
- Guarantee (30-day satisfaction)
- Affiliates pushing in parallel
