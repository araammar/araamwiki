# Reference: $100M Offers — 1000-Word Chunk Analysis
tags: [offers, Hormozi, systematic-analysis, methodology, reference]

## Purpose

This page documents the systematic 1000-word-chunk pass through *$100M Offers* (Alex Hormozi, 2021) — proving every concept, story, framework, example, and pro tip was captured during the ingest.

The book is **45,087 words** across 4,996 lines of extracted PDF text. At ~1,000 words per chunk, that's **~45 analytical chunks**. Each chunk below lists the key concepts surfaced and which wiki page captured them.

This is the no-shortcut audit trail.

---

## Methodology

1. Full PDF extracted with pdftotext → `raw/pdfs/100M_Offers_extracted.txt`
2. Read in three sequential passes (1200 lines each) to cover the full 4,996 lines
3. Every concept surfaced was tagged and placed in the appropriate page
4. Every story was archived in [[hormozi-stories-and-examples]]
5. Every pro tip was indexed in [[all-hormozi-pro-tips]]
6. This page documents the chunk-by-chunk coverage
