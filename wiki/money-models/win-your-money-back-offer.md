# Attraction Offer #1: Win Your Money Back
tags: [money-models, Hormozi, attraction-offer, gym, Win-Your-Money-Back]

## Summary / Definition

> *"You set a goal for the customer and tell them how to reach it. If they reach it, then they qualify to get their money back or get it back as store credit."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Money Models*

The Attraction Offer that built Hormozi's gym chain and Gym Launch's licensing model. Generated **$1+ billion in industry-wide sales** as the first Grand Slam Offer Gym Launch taught to gym owners.

---

## The Danny Origin Story (June 2013)

In a room of experienced gym owners, Danny shared his breakthrough:

> *"I had this pain-in-the-butt guy who wouldn't buy anything. He knew he needed it but he also said he needed more accountability. So we were going back and forth, and finally he came up with this idea. He said, 'How about this: I give you $500. You train me for eight weeks. And if I hit my goal, I get my money back. But in return, you can use my results to market your business.'"*

Result: The guy hit the goal. **And then used his refund to buy more training.** Plus his before-and-after pictures brought 13 referrals.

> *"I offer this to everyone now. The results are way better and people love the offer."*

Hormozi adopted the model:
- For private + group training
- For private + group nutrition coaching
- Then ran it in ads for new customers

> *"My cost of getting customers went way down and my leads exploded!"*

## The 3 Qualification Options

Customers can win their money back through one of three structures:

### Option 1: Results
- Customer must achieve the goal (lose X lbs, make $Y, get Z customers, etc.)
- Bet on their own outcome

### Option 2: Actions
- Customer must perform specified actions (attend all sessions, log data, post progress, etc.)
- Bet on their ability to follow directions

### Option 3: Actions AND Results
- Customer must do both
- Often the most realistic for first-timers in any field
- Sets them up to actually succeed

> *"Often, people wanting to achieve a goal have too few skills to do it. Even if they did bet on themselves, they'd fail. By setting a good goal for them and showing how they reach it, they have a fighting chance."*

## Examples Across Industries

### B2C: Free 28-Day Blueprint
Deposit $X, get it all back if you:
- Attend all consulting calls
- Post progress in the group weekly
- Journal daily in the app
- Attend feedback + transformation sessions

(Hint: meetings = upsell opportunities)

### B2B: 5 Customers In 5 Days Free Challenge
Deposit $X, get it back if you:
- Send 100 messages/day
- Report stats on messages sent
- Attend daily training
- Post finished homework in the group
- Attend the day-5 consulting call

### Physical Products: "Put 1,000,000 Miles On Your Car, Get A Free Car" (Actual Offer)
Get a free car if you:
- Buy a new car
- Drive 1,000,000 miles
- Turn it in
- Be in a press release
- We credit your original purchase toward your next car

## How To Create Good Win-Your-Money-Back Criteria

Three characteristics of strong criteria:

### 1. Easy To Track
> *"Train them on exactly what they need to do (or they will mess up). Bonus points if people already do it. Ex: Phones already track steps. Word processors already track word count. Cameras automatically date photos."*

### 2. Gets Customers Results
> *"Make criteria likely to get them their desired results. Realistic criteria do just fine. If you think the criteria look too easy, you've probably gotten close to realistic."*

Whatever the best customers already do → make everyone do it.

### 3. Advertises The Business
> *"Make advertising the business part of your criteria. For example: posting about their participation, tagging in social media, referring, or leaving reviews and testimonials."*

This is the secret. The "money back" is partly paid for by free marketing.

## Why The Offer Makes Money (Even With Refunds)

> *"From the data we've collected from thousands of gyms, about 10% of all customers will ask for their money back."*

It makes money because:

1. **Many won't qualify** even with realistic conditions
2. **Those who do qualify often stay as customers** — they have credit to spend → upsell opportunity
3. **The other 90% pay full price** and many refer additional customers

> *"Don't Worry. This Offer Makes Money. If you did give all the money back, this offer wouldn't make money — but it does."*

Critical rule:
> *"Only Offer 'Win Your Money Back' If You Feel Ok With Giving Money Back!"*

---

## How To Apply Store Credit [IMPORTANT — Per Hormozi]

When customers win, **don't give them all the time/value up front.** Spread it.

Example:
- You have a $200/mo product
- Customer wins $600 of credit
- Avoid giving them 3 free months up front
- Instead apply the $600 over 12 months ($50/mo discount)
- They now pay $150/mo for 12 months

> *"They can use credit however they want. But, I recommend you present this first. If they ask to use it up front, you can share my experience — people fall off if they don't pay something."*

This is the [[rollover-upsell|Rollover Upsell]] mechanic — see that page for the full play.

## The Mandatory Meeting Insight

> *"All Meetings And Calls Provide Opportunities To Make More Offers. Make check-in meetings part of your money-back criteria whenever you can. And make all meetings required to win their money back."*

Hormozi's gym version:
- **Nutrition Orientation** ("Before pictures") → make a supplement offer
- **Progress Check-in** → make a membership offer
- **Transformation Feedback** ("After pictures") → make the membership offer again, or upsell to annual prepay

Each meeting becomes a sales opportunity, framed as a win-condition. Customer doesn't see this as selling; they see it as commitment.

---

## "Make Everyone A Winner" In Private

> *"Promote and sell the program as though they will only get it back if they meet the criteria. But, about halfway through, make your next offer as if they already won."*

Script:
> *"I know you're trying to hit this short-term goal, but what's your long-term goal? . . . okay that's great to hear. You get that it's not about this program but about your long-term results. Tell ya what, to show you how much I want you to hit that long-term goal, I'll credit this program toward the next one whether you hit the short-term goal or not — how's that sound?"*

This kills the anxiety of "what if I fail?" and keeps them longer.

---

## At The End, Let The "Losers" Win Too

> *"If someone refuses your first upsell and fails the challenge, you can still upsell them again."*

Script:
> *"Don't worry about it. You started. That's the biggest victory of all. And even though you didn't hit your short-term goal, you met ours — which was finishing what you started. To show you that we're in this for the long haul, we'll credit your entire deposit towards staying with us long term."*

> *"You'll turn that frown upside down and they'll love you for it."*

The Hormozi mantra at the close of this section:
> *"We don't get customers to make a sale, we make sales to get customers."*

---

## Don't Take Blood Money

> *"If someone doesn't want me to have their money, I want it less than they do. As a personal rule, if a customer asks for a refund — entitled or not — I give it to them. Just focus on getting the next customer."*

This is Rule #3 from [[four-offer-types-framework|the 7 ground rules]].

---

## Hormozi's Summary Checklist

> *"Win Your Money Back is magical for businesses that require their customers to put in continuous efforts to get their ideal outcome."*

The offer rocks because:
- Loads of up-front cash
- More yeses (lower risk for customer)
- Massive customer results
- More long-term customers
- They advertise your offer for you

Operating rules:
- Make refund criteria easy to track + aligned with customer goals + helpful for the business
- Only use if your refund rate is **below 5%** (otherwise fix the product first)
- Put store credit toward another preferably more expensive offer
- Make everyone a winner in private

## Application For Caret/Colandrum

For a B2B SaaS where customers need to actually use the product:

> *"Pay $X for 3-month Colandrum trial. Win 100% of your money back if you:*
> *• Run at least 3 pitch campaigns through the platform*
> *• Attend all 3 onboarding calls*
> *• Submit 3 monthly results posts to your account*
> *• Leave a video testimonial at month 3"*

Then upsell the rollover credit toward an annual subscription with bonus features.
