# Upsell #1: The Classic Upsell
tags: [money-models, Hormozi, upsell, classic, BAMFAM]

## Summary / Definition

> *"The Classic Upsell offers a solution to the customer's next problem the moment they become aware of it."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Money Models*

The simplest, most profitable, easiest-for-anyone-to-do upsell.

> *"Current customers always have a higher chance of buying your stuff than strangers. And, when timed right, customers upsell themselves."*

---

## The Fur Coat Dealer Story (Summer 2016)

Hormozi's childhood mentor — fourth-generation fur coat dealer — sat across from him at a swanky restaurant. He smiled and revealed the play his son (the future fifth-generation owner) invented:

> *"We advertise free earmuffs with coat storage. And get this. When customers come to get their muffs and store their coats he says, 'Great. And we'll store those as well for $30. You don't want to store anything else do you?' And of course, they say no."*

Hormozi's reaction:
> *"Wait a second, so you get them to pay for additional storage for the free ear muffs by getting them to say no? You guys are legends."*

The "say no to say yes" mechanic is the key — the question is engineered so the natural "no" actually opts them in.

---

## The "You Can't Have X Without Y" Structure

The Classic Upsell relies on knowing more about the customer's problem than they do.

> *"Your core offer solves one problem and creates another. Your upsell immediately solves that next problem. This gives the classic upsell its 'You can't have X without Y' structure."*

Like the rental car story from [[money-models-master-hub]]:
- You can't have a car without insurance
- You can't have a car without gas  
- You can't have a good trip without a late checkout

> *"Bottom Line: If a problem appears, and you can solve it immediately — in exchange for money — do it!"*

## Important Notes

### Actually Do It

> *"You'd be amazed how many businesses come to me and only sell one thing. I usually just tell them 'You barely have a business — you have a front end. Figure out what you're gonna offer next.' Months later, I hear they actually 5x'd their business because they actually offered an upsell."*

Many businesses leave 5× revenue on the table just by skipping the next-offer.

### Offer More Profitable Upsells First

> *"If I offer two products and one has a higher profit than the other, I offer the higher profit option first."*

### "Say No To Say Yes"

The fur coat dealer trick:
> *"You don't want anything else do you?"*

This translates to: *"You don't want anything else [besides what I just offered] do you?"*

People are trained to say "no" to this question — and that "no" becomes a "yes" to the upsell.

### Surprise And Delight Bonuses

Have 4 bonuses saved for fence-sitters. Add one at a time. If they say yes before you've added all 4 — **still give them all 4**.

> *"It will surprise and delight them. And, it guarantees you still sell the same thing to everyone so no one feels left out later."*

### Hyper Buying Cycle

> *"Most buyers enter a 'hyper buying' cycle when they decide to do something new. It's a short window of time when they are the most excited about a new thing they're gonna do. This is when they spend a huge chunk of money in a short period of time. Think weddings, starting new hobbies, having babies, moving to new places, and so on."*

> *"If you have a business that caters to these sorts of problems, don't shy away from Upsell Offers. Embrace it . . . and keep making offers."*

### Use Free Bonuses To Create Upsell Opportunities

> *"Bonuses solve problems. That's what makes them valuable. And because of the problem-solution cycle, they can also reveal them. Upsells can solve that new problem."*

The fur coat dealer gave free ear muffs (low cost) → ear muffs need storage → $30 storage upsell. **Materials/labor on ear muffs paid for by storage fee.**

### Speed Of Access = Perceived Value

> *"The faster people get access to stuff, the more they'll value it. A $10,000 thing you get later is worth less than a $10,000 thing you get now."*

> *"Bonus points if you put it in their hands before they've said yes. It's way harder to give something back than it is to say no."*

### Bundle Upsells With Names

> *"It's easier to sell someone one thing than nine things. By bundling items together, you can make one 'ask' and get nine sales. I name the packages based on customer type and/or result. For example, 'Fastest results' bundle or 'Transformation package' or 'Minimum package.'"*

You can also "peel" components for a [[feature-downsell]] later.

### Integrate Upsells Into Other Offers

> *"My meal plans included optional supplement suggestions. So when I went over nutrition, people asked about supplements. Gym Launch sales and marketing training suggested optional softwares. This led gym owners to buy them. Integrate the next thing you wanna sell into the first thing they buy."*

### BAMFAM — Critical For Upselling

> *"The more times you can upsell, the more people you will upsell. If you upsell more people, you make more money. Since you want that . . . end every appointment by scheduling the next appointment. Don't let them leave without booking!"*

Sharran's mantra (referenced in $100M Leads):
> *"A customer should know the next time they see you — and why — before they leave."*

See [[bamfam-book-meeting-from-meeting]].

### Upsell As Many Times As It Makes Sense

> *"The rental car agency had lots of upsells. The burger place had lots of upsells. My gyms had lots of upsells. Gym Launch had lots of upsells. Offer as many solutions as there are problems you can solve. Don't be shy. If you can solve it, offer to."*

> *"The second worst thing that happens is they say no. The worst thing is if they would've said yes but you never asked."*

## The Movie Theater A-B-C Pricing Trick

How to nudge upsells via decoy pricing.

Standard pricing pushes Medium:
- Small: $5
- Medium: $8 (rather than the rational $7)
- Large: $9

Result: more people take Large.

If you want more Medium:
- Small: $6 (rather than rational $5)
- Medium: $7
- Large: $9

Result: people who'd take Small now take Medium.

> *"If you have a lot of customers buying small, you can bump them to mediums. If you have a lot of customers buying the medium, bump them to large. If you have a lot of customers buying large, raise all your prices."*

## Upsell Guarantees, Warranties, Insurance

> *"Many businesses offer guarantees on products. Many businesses offer warranties on products. Many businesses offer insurance on products. You can upsell all of them. So instead of doing it for free, just add 5-50% onto the price in exchange for a guarantee."*

Real example: An art studio used to replace damaged portraits for free. Hormozi told them to ask customers if they'd pay an extra 10% for the guarantee. **Now 30% of customers buy what the studio used to give away.**

> *"Pure profit baby."*

---

## Summary Points

- Use Classic Upsell for immediate problems revealed by your previous offer
- "You don't want anything else do you?" → people say "no" = yes
- Access speed = value. Get stuff in their hands fast
- Use free bonuses to create upsell opportunities
- BAMFAM ([[bamfam-book-meeting-from-meeting]]) is mandatory — never leave without booking the next
- You can have as many upsell offers as problems you can solve
- Sell guarantees / warranties / insurance for 5-50% upcharge
- Movie theater A-B-C pricing lets you nudge specific tier choices
