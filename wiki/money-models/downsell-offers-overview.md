# Section: Downsell Offers — What To Offer When They Say No
tags: [money-models, Hormozi, downsell-offers, overview]

## Summary / Definition

The third offer type. When customers say no to your upsell, you don't lose them — you downsell.

> *"Downselling tweaks the original offer to find the highest value solution for the customer's budget. So any offer you make after someone says 'no' is a downsell."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Money Models*

---

## Two Levers For Downselling

> *"I downsell in two ways. I change how they pay or what they get."*

## The 4 Rules Of Downselling

### Rule 1: They Said No To THIS Offer, Not All Offers

> *"Sometimes (a lotta times) people say no . . . and that's OK. Just because they rejected this offer does not mean they rejected you."*

> *"It hurts when someone rejects you. I get it. But see it for what it is — an opportunity to find out what they really want, and profit from it. Instead of hiding your head in the sand, stand your ground and make another offer."*

### Rule 2: Downsells Are Trades

> *"When downselling, you work with the customer to find combinations of giving and getting until you get a match. If you're gonna give something, get something."*

This is critical. You don't drop the price for nothing — you exchange terms.

### Rule 3: Personalize, Don't Pressure

> *"Figure out what they like and don't like. Then, offer more of what they like and less of what they don't — with a price to match."*

If someone refuses a large soda upsell, offer alternatives — small, juice, coffee.

> *"Am I being offensive by asking? Absolutely not. In fact, if I can better serve them, it would be offensive not to."*

### Rule 4: Offer The Same Things In New Ways

> *"In a perfect world, you've got tons of different things to sell so everyone buys something. In the real world, you limit downsells to what you've got."*

> *"Just think of downselling more like a hundred ways to offer the stuff you already have."*

---

## DO NOT Drop Your Price Just To Get A Sale

> *"First off, dropping your price is not really downselling, it's discounting. If someone wants what you have, and just doesn't want to pay the price — tough cookies."*

You can:
- Change *how* they pay (payment plan)
- Change *what* they get (feature downsell)

You cannot:
- Drop price for the same thing

---

## Customers Talk About Price

> *"By all means, test prices. Plan to offer your thing at a specific price, to a specific number of people, ahead of time. That's way different than charging somebody less in the moment just because you felt scared of losing the sale in the moment."*

> *"Customers talk. If they find out someone got the same thing for less 'just because' — you'll upset people. And it also becomes an ethical problem, at least to me."*

---

## How NOT To Downsell — The Insurance Story

> *"I was buying a car and the salesman tried to upsell car insurance. The cost of the insurance when he first started was $5,000. I said no. But then, he lowered the price. And I said no again. He kept lowering the price until the same insurance he first offered for $5,000 was now only $400! I still said no. At first I said no because it was too much money, but by the end I said no because I didn't trust the guy."*

> *"The entire experience felt dirty. Then I wondered, was he ripping me off on the car too?"*

> *"Now, I didn't want to buy the car from him either!"*

The lesson:

> *"People lower the price to close a sale. But even if you close this one sale, the customer will question every price you offer from that point going forward . . . and whoever they tell. You trade trust for a buck. Not worth it."*

Note: *"You can offer something different for less. You just can't offer the same thing for less."*

## Section Conclusion (From The Book)

> *"Downsells give you another shot at getting a customer by turning nos into yeses. For that reason, it's less about having a hundred different products with the same offer, and more about having a hundred different offers for the same product."*

> *"But, no matter what, the offer is never the same stuff for cheaper. We just keep tweaking the offer until we make it the best deal for them. The extra cash explodes our 30-day profits and blows us past our goals."*

---

## How To Pick Which Downsell To Use

The book's hierarchy:

1. **Start with Payment Plan Downsells** — Same product, same total price, different payment timing. Highest preserved revenue.
2. **If they still say no on the recurring service** → Trial With Penalty
3. **If they want to pay less overall** → Feature Downsells (different product, lower price)
4. **You can alternate between Payment Plan + Feature Downsells in the same sale** — very effective

---

## Application For Caret/Colandrum

When a prospect rejects the annual subscription:

1. **Payment Plan**: 4 quarterly payments instead of annual prepay
2. **Trial With Penalty**: Free 30-day trial with mandatory onboarding/usage criteria, billed if missed
3. **Feature Downsell**: Drop dedicated CSM → group support; drop white-label → standard branding; drop unlimited integrations → 3 max

Use the temperature check at any point: *"On a scale 1-10 how badly do you want this?"* If 8+, push payment plan. If 7 or below, push feature downsell.
