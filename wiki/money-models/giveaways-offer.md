# Attraction Offer #2: Giveaways
tags: [money-models, Hormozi, attraction-offer, giveaway, sweepstakes, scholarship]

## Summary / Definition

> *"Giveaway Offers advertise a chance to win a big prize in exchange for contact information and whatever else you want. Then, after picking a winner, you offer everyone else the big prize at a discounted price."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Money Models*

Goes by many names: scholarship, sweepstakes, raffle, contest. All mean "enter for a chance to win." Powerful enough that it's **heavily regulated** — Hormozi opens the chapter with a legal disclaimer.

---

## The Fitness Certification Origin Story (August 2020)

Phone call with an owner of a fitness certification business:

> *"How do you get leads?"*
> *"We advertise a full-ride scholarship to our entire program. People apply with their contact information and answer a few questions. The best answer gets a full-ride. But we also do something more . . . we give out partial scholarships."*

The mechanic:
- One person wins the full-ride
- Everyone else gets a "partial scholarship" → discounted core offer
- They don't know the actual price when they hop on the call
- They know the value of the full-ride
- Discounted price feels like a big deal

> *"Most of them join our program on the spot."*

Hormozi's realization:
> *"So not only do you get tons of engaged leads, but you also get more customers with your 'surprise discount?' Genius."*

The certification owner taught this exact play to the trainers he certifies — *"It works just as well to get fitness customers — sometimes even better."*

## The 6-Step Process

### Step 1: Pick A Grand Prize
- The thing you want everyone to buy
- **Assign a monetary value** to serve as price anchor
- *"If you sell $5,000 worth of value for $2,000, then advertise the $5,000 value!"*

### Step 2: Pick Your Promotional Offer
- The "partial scholarship" for everyone who didn't win
- Create by enhancing your core offer with a discount, bonus, or minor change
- The Grand Prize is the anchor; the promotional offer is what most people buy
- Names that work: "scholarship," "gift card," "dollars off," "store credit," "voucher"

> *"Remember, leads entered the Giveaway because they found the Grand Prize interesting. It gets you qualified leads because you offer what they already showed interest in at a discount."*

### Step 3: Ask For Contact Information

Two layers of qualification:

**Eligibility questions**: Are you a fit? (*"Do you own a vet clinic?"* or *"Why should you be selected?"*)

**Qualifying actions**: Stuff entrants do to qualify and promote the giveaway (attend a call, make a post, enter a group, etc.)

### Step 4: Put The Giveaway On A Deadline (Urgency)
- Hormozi prefers 3-7 days from promotion start
- Daily countdown updates to entrants
- Mix value + countdown ("here are 3 benefits of the grand prize, by the way you have 4 days left")
- One update per day across all platforms

> *"Pro Tip: Whisper-Tease-Shout. Once people enter the Free Giveaway, it may help to think of the countdown like a mini-product launch."*

(See [[whisper-tease-shout-launch]] from $100M Leads.)

### Step 5: Announce The Grand Prize Winner
- Public announcement for the winner
- Private messages to everyone else: "you also qualified for something"
- Frame the partial scholarship as a "participation trophy" or "we found your answer so compelling"
- Get them on a call

### Step 6: Add A Claim Deadline
- The promotional offer expires in 7 days
- Show benefits, social proof, value over the second countdown
- Book the call inside that window

## The Cost-To-Value Discount Rule

> *"My rule of thumb: make your core offer discount equal to 10%-30% of your gross margins."*

Worked example:
- Grand Prize: $5,000 value, $2,000 retail
- Everyone else: $1,800 (10% off retail)
- When framed correctly: $5,000 of value for $1,800 = **64% difference in cost-to-value**

So a tiny actual discount becomes a huge perceived discount because the price anchor is the Grand Prize value, not the retail price.

## Important Notes

### Legal: Consult Counsel

Hormozi explicitly says he's not a lawyer but considers these no-brainers:
- Somebody actually has to win the Grand Prize
- Make qualifications clear in the rules
- Make clear that more than one person can win a prize

### Eligibility Questions Drive Conversion Later

> *"More people will take your core offer if you can make the value specific to them. I ask questions like: Why should we pick you? Why this program? Why now? Why does this matter to you? What's your goal?"*

Use these answers in your sales calls. They become ammo.

### If The Giveaway Fails, The Grand Prize Wasn't Grand Enough

> *"One of my portfolio companies ran a giveaway. They barely got interest. Their grand prize? Tickets to their event. Not compelling."*

Re-ran with $50,000 bundle of equipment from a well-known supplier + their core product for a year. **Crushed.**

> *"When you have an awesome thing to give away and you advertise it properly, the leads flood in."*

### Give Two Prizes For Twice The Leads (Referral Hack)

> *"If you give away one prize, that's fine. But if you give away two grand prizes, you can get twice the leads (or more)."*

Mechanic: tell everyone if someone they refer wins, they win the other prize. Endless entries via referrals. Referrers stay invested in their referrals' success, keeping quality high.

(Hormozi did this for Skool.com, which he co-owns.)

### Scarcity + Urgency

Limit by time, entries, or both:
- "Up to 5,000 entries" cap
- Or 7-day window
- Match maximum entries to your sales team's call-capacity

### Have Downsells Ready

If the promotional offer doesn't land, offer same percentage discount on any other relevant product. Pivot to [[feature-downsell]] mechanics.

### For Recurring Revenue Businesses
- Apply the discount over the longest period they'll agree to
- Set up monthly subscription to auto-bill at full rate after the discounted period

## Application For Caret/Colandrum

**Free 1-Year Colandrum + Strategy Sprint Giveaway**
- Grand Prize: $50,000 value (Colandrum subscription + done-for-you pitch redesign + monthly strategy calls with founder)
- Promotional Offer: $5,000 voucher off annual subscription + onboarding waived
- Entry questions: Agency size? Top pitch challenges? Why now? Why you?
- 7-day entry window
- Multi-channel updates
- Public winner announcement → private outreach to all qualified runners-up
- Book 5 days of demos for the runners-up

Expected math: 200 entries → 80 qualified → 60 book calls → 25 close at average $5K-$10K ACV = $125K-$250K from one promotion.

---

## Summary Checklist

- Giveaways ask audience to apply to win a high-value thing
- Many enter — one wins. The rest qualify for discounts on your core offer
- Pick a Grand Prize people want
- Two prizes if you want referrals
- Get information via the entry process (ammo for sales)
- 7-day advertising window OR until you cap appointment capacity
- Book appointments with everyone else
- Expiration date on claiming the prize
- Have downsell offers ready
