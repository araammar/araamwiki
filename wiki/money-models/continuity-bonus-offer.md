# Continuity #1: Continuity Bonus Offer
tags: [money-models, Hormozi, continuity, bonus, membership]

## Summary / Definition

> *"With Continuity Bonuses you give the customer an awesome thing if they sign up today. Typically, the bonus itself has more value than the first continuity payment."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Money Models*

The Continuity Offer that combines a high-value upfront bonus with the recurring commitment.

---

## The Gym That Tripled Membership Sales (Fall 2019)

Hormozi was teaching gym owners how to sell six-week challenges. Some struggled to convert challengers into continuity. Then one previously-struggling gym posted numbers higher than the best performers.

> *"Dude — your numbers are insane. How do you convert so many members?"*
> *"I'm not really doing the six-week challenge."*

The mechanic:
- Run normal pitch on the challenge, explain the price
- As soon as they're interested, ask: *"Want to get it for free?"*
- Yes (of course).
- *"If you become a member, we'll make it free."*
- Plus member-exclusive bonuses: better class times, tanning booth, VIP events

> *"It converts like crazy. Last, we upsell a discounted prepaid membership."*

The upsell:
> *"Wanna save even more money?"*

Then offer prepaid discount + bonuses for 6 months of membership.

### The Numbers

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| 34/100 sign up for challenge | 15/100 sign up for challenge |
| 17/100 convert to stay (half of 34) | **40/100 go straight into continuity** |
| | + 8/40 take 6-month prepayment upsell |

> *"So let me get this straight . . . you tripled membership sales . . . you still get up front cash from challenges . . . and you stack even more up front cash from prepaid memberships?"*

> *"He could barely contain his grin. And for good reason."*

## The Mechanic

Continuity Bonuses use two levers:

### Bonus (Adding Value)
For products: many small things or one big complementary product. For services: defined program, onboarding, setup, or feature that adds value.

### Discount (Lowering Costs)
> *"Remember, anything you offer for free you can also offer as a discount."*

> *"When making Continuity Offers, I get more people to start if I add more good stuff (bonuses) and take away bad stuff (discounts)."*

> *"And of course, it all works better with a dash of urgency — if they join now."*

---

## How Hormozi Uses Continuity (After Other Offers)

> *"On their own, Continuity Offers get less cash now, and that makes it tough for getting customers profitably. But the way I use them, we can still hit our 30-day profit goals."*

Sequence:
1. Big-cash Attraction, Upsell, Downsell offers first
2. Continuity Offer = first month's small cash
3. Upsell to bulk prepay → more cash
4. This boosts 30-day profit AND builds recurring revenue

## Examples

### Physical Product: Pet Food Continuity
- **One-Time Bonus**: Every dog toy we've ever made for free ($800 value) when you sign up for monthly dog food ($59/mo)
- **Monthly Bonuses**: New dog toy every month as a member

### Service: Short-Term Accelerator
- **One-Time Bonus**: Short-Term Accelerator (normally $1,000) FREE when you become a member ($100/mo)
- **Bonus Package**: VIP community members get first-in-line event access, longer support hours, better support reps

### Digital Product: Newsletter
- **One-Time Bonus**: All past 40 newsletters ($15,880 value) FREE for becoming a member ($399/mo after 30-day free trial)
- **Lifetime Bonuses**: Pay today → lock in $299/mo lifetime + early digital access + monthly physical copy

## Important Notes

### Focus On The Bonus, Not The Membership

> *"'Join my membership program' isn't nearly as compelling as 'get this free valuable thing.' So advertise that. Then, explain the rest after they show interest."*

### Bonuses Work Like Upsells (3 Types)

- **More of the same**: "2 years of past newsletters free for becoming a member"
- **Complementary**: "Free nutrition services when you sign up for fitness membership"
- **Upgrade**: "Free gold membership when you buy bronze (limited availability)"

### Keep Bonuses Related To Core Offer

> *"If the bonus is too different you will attract the wrong customers. For instance, don't advertise a free t-shirt to upsell tech services."*

### Make Bonuses Things You Already Have And Do

> *"The two past years of newsletters cost no extra time but are super high value. And onboarding is something you have to do with the client anyways, so you might as well slap a price on it and give to 'em as a bonus."*

### Physical Bonuses For Digital Products (And Vice Versa)
- Digital membership → offer hat, shirt, tool related to the offer
- Physical membership → offer livestream classes

> *"This strategy often lowers the cost of getting a customer more than the cost of the bonus."*

### Use Realistic Pricing

> *"The bigger the value-anchor on your bonus, the more compelling the offer. But, you also have to make that anchor believable. Some business owners make up ridiculous values. Don't do this. It won't anchor the customer and you'll lose trust."*

Best practice: anchor against products you've actually sold before — real prices, real value.

### Titles As Bonuses

> *"Consider giving customers titles after they stay three, six, or twelve months and beyond. Titles like silver, gold, diamond, double diamond, etc."*

A friend of Hormozi's found customers cared more about the title than any other bonus. They introduced themselves to her using the title.

### Anchor The Bonus When Presenting

First, sell the benefits of the bonus. Not the membership — **the bonus**. Use it as the price anchor.

> *"It may shock them — and that's okay. Because then you ask, 'Do you want to know how you can get this for free?'"*

If they say yes, explain how:
> *"Become a VIP member today and you'll get it all as a free gift for joining. Or, you can just buy it for $XXX — which would you prefer?"*

### Stack Bonuses For More Conversion

> *"On top of that . . . when you become a member you'll get amazing thing 1, amazing thing 2, amazing thing 3."*

Mention individual dollar values to anchor.

### Force Continuity-Only Bonuses

> *"If you want to force everyone into continuity, then offer continuity as the only option. In other words, make the bonuses only available if they join the membership."*

## Hormozi's Pricing Data — Standalone vs Continuity

> *"For whatever reason, some people pick one-time payments over continuity . . . even with higher one-time payments. So offer a higher one-time payment option."*

The data Hormozi has tested:

| Target % Choosing Continuity | Standalone Multiplier | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 50% | 1.33× | $399 standalone ($266/mo) or $199/mo membership |
| 60% | 1.66× | $499 standalone ($333/mo) or $199/mo |
| 70% | 2× | $599 standalone ($399/mo) or $199/mo |
| 80% | 2.33× | $699 standalone ($466/mo) or $199/mo |
| 90% | 2.66× | $799 standalone ($532/mo) or $199/mo |

> *"The exact numbers matter less than the principle. The smaller the standalone price compared to the continuity price, the more people buy the standalone. The larger the standalone price compared to the continuity price, the more people choose continuity."*

### If You Want More Up-Front Cash

> *"Make bonuses and continuity+bonuses separate offers. Make the bonus-only offer a single payment that's 1.33x to 2.66x more expensive than the first month of the continuity+bonus offer."*

> *"Even if you charge 33% more for a one-time purchase, half will buy it!"*

### Offer Bulk Prepaid Discounts

> *"Bulk continuity upsells boost 30-day profits by a lot. Let's say you offer 'buy five months get one free.' Only one out of every eight people has to take the upsell to raise 30-day profits by 50%!"*

### If You Want Commitments — Trade

> *"For example, only allow customers to get the bonus if they commit to 3-6-12+ months."*

> *"You'll lose people who would've signed up month-to-month just to get the bonus. This nets fewer sales, but more committed customers. This is the trade you make."*

---

## Summary Points

- Sign up to continuity → get a high-value bonus
- Typically the bonus is worth more than the first month's payment
- If used as attraction: advertise what you give away, not what you sell
- Bonus must be related to core offer
- Use stuff you already have and do
- Sell the bonus value BEFORE telling them how to get it free
- Standalone offer = 33-166% above continuity, captures half who want one-time payment
- Bulk prepaid discounts boost 30-day profits significantly
- Commitment-only bonuses trade volume for retention

## Application For Caret/Colandrum

### Annual Subscription Bonus Stack
Sign up for annual prepay and get:
- The Pitch Vault library (all past playbooks, $10K value)
- Founder office hours quarterly ($5K value)
- Private agency Slack ($3K value)
- Onboarding fee waived ($5K value)

Total bonus value: **$23K** anchored against $12K annual subscription.

Tested per Hormozi's data — pricing the standalone monthly equivalent at $1,800-2,400/mo (~1.5×) would shift ~60% of customers to annual.
