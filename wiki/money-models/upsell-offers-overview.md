# Section: Upsell Offers — Whatever You Offer Next
tags: [money-models, Hormozi, upsell-offers, overview]

## Summary / Definition

The second offer type. Once Attraction Offers have got you customers, Upsell Offers make more money from them.

> *"With an Attraction Offer in place, you've got customers and cash. If we did a good job, we've turned a profit too. Nice! Now we want to maximize 30-day profits. So what do we do? Answer: make more money. To do that, we make Upsell Offers. And when it comes down to it, Upsells just mean whatever we offer next."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Money Models*

---

## The Underlying Mechanic — Problem-Solution Cycle

> *"When an offer solves a problem, another appears. You upsell the solution to the problem your offer reveals. So, every offer opens the door to an upsell . . . even upsells! Often, upsells make the majority of the profit. They make or break a Money Model."*

This is the same Problem-Solution Cycle from [[lead-magnets-framework]]. The Money Model is the cycle operationalized as cash extraction.

---

## The Burger Shop Math (Why Upsells Make Or Break)

Hormozi's worked example:

| What Customer Buys | Profit |
|---|---|
| $2 burger | $0.25 |
| + $0.75 fries upsell | **$1.00 (4×)** |
| + drink (make it a meal) | **$2.00 (8×)** |
| + supersize for $1 | **$3.00 (12×)** |

> *"Your first offer doesn't always make the profit. In other words, the thing you sell the most isn't always the thing you make the most profit on. You make it on the second, third, and in the case of the burger business, fourth offers and beyond."*

> *"If McDonald's didn't upsell fries and soda, there wouldn't be a McDonald's."*

The lesson: **figure out your version of "Do you want fries with that?" If you don't, others will.**

---

## When Upsells Fail

> *"Upsells fail when:*
> *• You offer something they don't want (too different or doesn't solve their problem).*
> *• You offer it at the wrong time (before they've experienced the problem).*
> *• You offer it the wrong way (they don't believe you).*
> *• Or, a combination of the above."*

Right thing. Right time. Right way. Miss any of these and the upsell dies.

---

## The 3 Categories Of Upsell Content

> *"In summary, Upsells tend to offer:*
> *• More of what they just got (think quantity) — Why have one burger when you can have two?*
> *• Better versions of it (think quality) — Why have mystery meat when you can have sirloin?*
> *• New or complementary stuff (think different) — Do you want fries and a soda with that burger?"*

Each play covered in this section uses one or more of these mechanics.

## Section Conclusion (From The Book)

> *"Anytime you offer something next, you upsell. Upsells play a key role in Money Models by getting more cash from customers faster than you otherwise would have. And if your Attraction Offer already covers the costs of getting customers and delivering — more money ain't a bad thing."*

> *"They are core to my business success. Upsells change everything. Many businesses go from burning cash to printing it — overnight."*

---

## How To Pick Which Upsell To Use

| Situation | Try |
|---|---|
| First purchase reveals a clear next need | [[classic-upsell]] |
| Multiple products available, customer has options | [[menu-upsell-system]] |
| Wide price range (premium → basic) | [[anchor-upsell]] |
| Want to convert short-term buyers to long-term | [[rollover-upsell]] |

Most businesses use multiple upsells in sequence. The 4 plays are tools, not alternatives.

---

## Application For Caret/Colandrum

If Colandrum sold a base SaaS subscription:

1. **Classic Upsell**: "You can't measure pitch performance without our pitch coach add-on" → +$X/mo
2. **Menu Upsell**: When account manager onboarding ends, present 3 tier choices
3. **Anchor Upsell**: "$50K white-glove transformation" → if gasp → "$10K self-serve + coaching" → if gasp → "$5K base"
4. **Rollover Upsell**: 3 months in → "$2K of credit toward annual prepay"
