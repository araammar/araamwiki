# Downsell #3: Feature Downsell
tags: [money-models, Hormozi, downsell, feature, DIY, guarantee]

## Summary / Definition

> *"Feature Downsells lower prices by changing what customers get. I do them by offering lesser quantity, lower quality, lower price alternatives, or cutting optional components."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Money Models*

Hormozi prefers Feature Downsell when the customer wants to pay less *overall* (not just less right now).

---

## The 75% Close Rate Story (2019)

> *"This new downsell tripled my close rate from 25% to 75% last quarter. And even crazier, more people bought the main thing."*

> *"You start offering a payment plan or a discount?"*
> *"Neither. Payment plans take too long. And discounts devalue my product."*

> *"What are you doing?"*
> *"I lower the price, but I justify it by cutting a feature. That way, I'm not discounting."*

The friend's secret: **he downsold by removing his money-back guarantee.**

> *"When we get a price objection we ask 'If you don't want the option to get your money back, you can pay less. Or, you can keep your money-back guarantee — which would you prefer?' Once they understand what they'd give up, they often say 'Screw it, I'd rather keep the guarantee and get my money back.'"*

The numbers:
- Before: 100 leads → 25 buyers (1 full-price option)
- After: 100 leads → **35 buy the main thing + 40 take the downsell = 75 total**

> *"So it upped your full-price buyers, total close rate, and up front cash."*

---

## How The Mechanism Works

> *"All features have a price and a value. If you remove something the price goes down, sure. But, the value goes down too."*

The trick: **remove from highest to lowest value.** People weigh how much they save vs. how much they lose.

- Remove high-value features → big perceived loss → they reconsider the main offer
- Remove low-value features → small perceived loss → they take the downsell

> *"Clever Feature Downselling gets customers to 're-upsell' themselves on the more expensive offers."*

---

## The Formula

> *"Feature Downsells have a simple formula: take something away, lower the price, and in so many words ask 'how about now?'"*

---

## Feature Downsell Categories

### Product / Service Quantity
- **Product**: "Instead of a three-month supply, how about we start with just one?"
- **Service**: "Instead of four sessions per month, why don't we start you at two?"

### Product Quality
"Instead of the leather seats we can do vinyl, how does that sound?"

### Service Quality
"Instead of 5-minute response times, why don't we just start you at overnight response times?"

### Service Quality Features (Hormozi's Full Menu)
- **Time Availability**: Specific times vs anytime
- **Days**: Mon/Wed/Fri vs all days
- **Hours**: 9-5 vs 24/7
- **Length**: 15-min vs 60-min support calls
- **Location**: One location vs all locations
- **Cancellations**: Reschedule fees vs free
- **Speed of response**: Minutes vs hours vs days
- **Speed of delivery**: Wait in line vs priority; same-day vs next-week
- **Service ratio**: 1:1 vs 1:many vs many:1
- **Communication**: Text vs chat vs video
- **Provider qualifications**: Owner vs senior employee vs new employee
- **Live vs recorded**
- **In-person vs remote**
- **DIY / DWY / DFY**
- **Expirations**: Forever vs X time vs specific times
- **Personalization**: Generic vs custom
- **Insurance / Guarantee**:
  - Length: 1 year vs lifetime
  - Coverage: specific bad thing vs any bad thing
  - Terms: unconditional vs conditional

### Removing Entire Features
"Instead of priority chat support, email support, and calls, why don't we just keep chat and email but drop the calls?"

### DFY → DWY → DIY
- Chiropractor: "Instead of adjustments, let's start you with at-home massage tools, foam rollers, mats"
- Painter: "If you can't afford me painting, why don't I give you the paint and lease one of our spray machines?"
- Hormozi himself: "Instead of me and my team buying your company, why don't you just attend a workshop?"

## Important Notes

### Never Negotiate The Price

> *"People who demand to pay less for the same thing are business terrorists. I don't negotiate with terrorists."*

- Pay less now → payment plan
- Pay less overall → feature downsell
- Pay less just because → no

### Maintain The Position Of A Helpful Guide

> *"Feature Downselling means trying to find the best deal for them. This keeps the conversation collaborative rather than competitive. If you act pushy, your offers will exhaust customers faster. If you stay a helpful guide, you can downsell as many offers as necessary without exhausting the customer."*

### Standardize Your Downsell Process

> *"First, I cut something valuable and lower the price a little. I do this to get them to reconsider the original offer/price. If that fails, I continue removing features and lowering prices until they buy. I'd rather people get something rather than nothing."*

### Name Your Combinations

- Most expensive: "The Whale Package," "Total Transformation," "High Roller"
- Cheapest: **"The Minimum"** — implies they need at least that

Hormozi:
> *"I like ['The Minimum'] because it implies they have to get at least that thing. If someone rejects all other packages, I just say 'so nothing more than the minimum package then?' to get them to say no to say yes."*

(Same mechanic as [[classic-upsell]].)

### Temperature Check After Two Downsells

> *"If you make two changes in a row and they still refuse, make sure they really want the thing."*

> *"Got it. Real quick. I want to make sure. On a scale from 1-10 how bad do you want this?"*

- 8+: Switch to [[payment-plan-downsell|Payment Plan Downsell]]
- 7 or below: Ask "What would a 10 look like?" → recombine features

> *"This means you can alternate between payment plan and feature downsells. When you use both, you become very difficult to refuse."*

### Ask "Deal?" Or "Fair Enough?"

> *"This works astonishingly well. Fewer people will see you change the offer for them and then say 'No that's not fair.'"*

### Free Orientations Boost DIY Downsells

After they refuse all done-for-you offers:

> *"Even though we're not gonna work together on X, I still want to help. How about you just come to a free orientation on X tomorrow?"*

Hormozi did this for fitness — half showed up, **almost all of them bought supplements.**

> *"Free money for little extra work."*

### Feature Downsell Your Guarantees

> *"If you already have a guarantee, make removing it part of your Feature Downsell process. People value security, so removing it gets many people to realize its value. This often flips an initial 'no' back to a 'yes.'"*

This is the friend's trick from the origin story.

### Feature Downsell Current Customers

> *"Customers who use all the features they pay for keep paying longer than people who don't. So once you see a customer isn't using a feature, offer a lower price — only paying for the features they use."*

> *"Customers who we've downsold into a lower package just for them have the second highest value of all my customers."*

### Barter With Reviews, Testimonials, Referrals

> *"I'll knock $100 off if you: 1) Leave me a review on all review sites 2) Leave me a video testimonial 3) Make a public social post at the beginning, middle, and end of our program showing your progress 4) Introduce me to two friends who you would want to do this with. Deal?"*

> *"To me, the advertising is worth more than the $100 discount. To them, the $100 is worth less than the advertising. Win-win."*

## Summary Points

- Lower prices by removing stuff
- "Take something away, lower price, how about now?"
- Lesser quantity / quality / different / cut features
- People see value in what you removed AFTER seeing price difference
- Remove valuable things first to get reconsideration; remove cheap things to get the downsell
- First downsell = get reconsideration of original offer
- Subsequent downsells = find best deal for them
- 1-10 check after 2 refusals (8+ payment plan, less feature downsell)
- Alternate between feature and payment plan downsells
- Free orientations boost DIY downsells (half show, most buy supplements)
- Feature downsell your guarantees — biggest re-upsell mechanism

## Application For Caret/Colandrum

When agency rejects $12K annual:

1. *"Wanna drop the white-label add-on? Saves $2K."* → $10K
2. If still no: *"Drop the dedicated CSM, use group support?"* → $7K
3. If still no: *"Drop the API integration and use manual upload?"* → $5K
4. If still no: temperature check
5. If 8+: payment plan it
6. If 7 or less: free orientation → DIY toolkit upsell
