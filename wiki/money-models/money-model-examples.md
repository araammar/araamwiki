## Example 1: Gym Launch (Services)

### Stage I — Attraction Offer
[[decoy-offer|Decoy Offer]]:
- Free Do-It-Yourself (courses, books, video training)
- **vs Premium $16,000 Done-With-You Licensing** (16 weeks)

### Stage II — Upsell Offer
[[classic-upsell|Classic Upsell]]:
- *"Once you know how to get 'em, you gotta know how to keep 'em."*
- **$42,000/year ($36,000 Prepaid)** for advanced business services

### Stage II — Downsell Offer
[[payment-plan-downsell|Payment Plan Downsell]]:
- Seesaw Downsell: $10,000 down + rest spread over 52 weeks
- Final Payment Plan: $800/week × 52 weeks

### Stage III — Continuity Offer
[[menu-upsell-system|Menu Upsell]] + [[feature-downsell|Feature Downsell]]:

| Package | Price/Week |
|---|---|
| **Full Package** | $800 |
| Done-For-You Advertising | $300 |
| Gym Sales Daily Training | $200 |
| Monthly New Releases | $500 |
| Original Licensing + Tech Support | $100 |
| **Minimum Package** | **$100** |

### What This Money Model Achieved
- $476,000/mo in 3 months (Stage I alone)
- $1.5M/mo (with Stage II)
- $2.3M/mo (with Menu + Feature Downsells, month 14)
- $4.4M/mo (with Prestige Labs integration, month 20)
- Over 5 years: **$43M in owner distributions**
- Sold 66% at **$46.2M valuation** → $100M+ net worth at age 31

See [[make-your-money-model]] for the full evolution narrative.

## Patterns Across All 4 Examples

### Pattern 1: Every Money Model Uses All 4 Types
None of the examples are missing Attraction / Upsell / Downsell / Continuity. The book argues this is the minimum required for a *$100M* Money Model.

### Pattern 2: Many Offers Wear Multiple Hats
The Newsletter example is the most extreme — one offer serves all three stages. But every example has at least one play that does double duty.

### Pattern 3: Continuity Comes Last (And Compounds)
In every example, Continuity is the long-tail. The first 30 days' cash comes from Stages I-II. Stage III's cash compounds forever after.

### Pattern 4: Downsells Are Often Combinatorial
Look at Gym Launch's downsell: "Seesaw Downsell" — start at $10K down, work down to $800/wk. Not a single play; a process. And the Stage III "Minimum Package" is the [[feature-downsell|Feature Downsell]] endpoint.

## Application For Caret/Colandrum

A B2B SaaS Money Model for Colandrum:

### Stage I — Attraction Offer
[[decoy-offer|Decoy]]: Free Pitch Audit Playbook (DIY) vs $12K/yr Colandrum Pro Subscription

### Stage II — Upsell Offer
[[anchor-upsell|Anchor Upsell]]:
- White-Glove $50K/yr (dedicated CSM + custom integrations + monthly founder office hours)
- **Main**: $12K/yr Pro tier

[[classic-upsell|Classic Upsell]] add-on: Pitch Coach @ $500/mo (you can't win without coaching)

### Stage II — Downsell Offer
[[payment-plan-downsell|Payment Plan Downsell]]: Quarterly payments instead of annual prepay
[[feature-downsell|Feature Downsell]]: Drop dedicated CSM, drop white-label, drop integrations to find a fit

### Stage III — Continuity Offer
[[continuity-bonus-offer|Continuity Bonus]]: Annual prepay = Pitch Vault library + founder office hours + private agency Slack ($23K perceived bonus value)
**Weekly billing** ([[continuity-discount-offer|Continuity Discount tip]]) = 8.3% extra annual revenue
