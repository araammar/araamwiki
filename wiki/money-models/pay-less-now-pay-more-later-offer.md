# Attraction Offer #5: Pay Less Now or Pay More Later
tags: [money-models, Hormozi, attraction-offer, free-trial, guarantee]

## Summary / Definition

> *"You give people a choice to pay full-price later OR pay a discounted price now. This play works so well because we remove all risk from the customer. They pay later and only if they like it. So it combines the benefits of a delayed payment and a satisfaction guarantee."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Money Models*

The "free with the card on file" mechanic, but engineered to convert. Combines a satisfaction guarantee with a discount-for-immediate-payment incentive.

---

## The Reading Guru Origin Story (June 2016)

A headline caught Hormozi's attention:

> *"Double your reading speed in 3 hours, or it's free."*

Registration page offered two options:

**Option 1 (Pay Later):**
> *"You can put your credit card down for $0, and get billed $297 tomorrow. And if your reading speed doesn't double, just email us before then and we'll cancel the charge. But you must attend in order to be eligible."*

**Option 2 (Pay Now):**
> *"You can just pay $97 right now, and as a free bonus, get the recordings, which won't be for sale anywhere else."*

Hormozi took Option 1. The training delivered. He stayed.

> *"After two hours, using his tactics, my reading speed doubled. Impressive. The training had been true to the promise. He earned his $297."*

He learned **a brand new Attraction Offer** in the process.

## How It Works

The structure has two layers:

### Layer 1: Pay Later Option (Free Trial With Card)
- Customer puts card down for $0
- Bills after the trial period unless they cancel
- Eligibility for cancel = they must attend / use the product
- Frames the whole thing as "free" since they can cancel

> *"This gets lots of leads engaged. But this free offer has an added benefit — we get their card on file. If they choose this option and hate the product, then they can cancel any time before the charge goes through."*

### Layer 2: Pay Now Option (Discount + Bonus)
- 20-50% discount off the pay-later price
- Plus valuable bonuses
- For people who want to lock in the better deal immediately

### The Choice
- Whether they pick Pay Later or Pay Now, **you've got a customer** (or about to)
- Whether they ultimately pay or cancel, you've moved them down the funnel

## Examples

### Education: Find Your First Real Estate Deal — Free 3-Day Workshop
- **Pay Later**: $0 for workshop. Billed $500 at end unless they cancel.
- **Pay Now**: $299 for workshop + recordings + 1:1 with certified expert + printed materials
- **Upsell**: $30,000 take-you-through-every-step coaching for 6 months

### Local Service: Trim Your Hedges For Free
- **Pay Later**: $0 lawn cut + hedges then $599 after
- **Pay Now**: $369 lawn cut + hedges + lawn treatment
- **Upsell**: $199/mo ongoing lawncare

### Physical Products: 14-Day Clothing Trial
- **Pay Later**: $0 now. Billed $149 in 14 days.
- **Pay Now**: $97 for clothing + accessory
- **Upsell**: Monthly subscription
- *Must return product in like-new condition before billing to qualify for guarantee*

## Important Notes

### Promise A Clear Yes/No Result

> *"First, make your promise a clear 'yes or no' result. Second, make sure you can deliver on it within your time frame."*

For shoulder pain: rate 1-10 before, then after. Tracked. Verifiable.

> *"Keep the promise simple, clear, and measurable. This avoids unnecessary cancellations."*

### Make A Conditional Satisfaction Guarantee

Customers can only cancel if they qualify. The reading guru required attendance. *"They can't say you suck if they never try it."*

> *"Make the criteria what people do to get the most value out of the product. Win-Win."*

### Bonuses For Your Pay-Now Option

> *"I hate when people repeat content and call it new. So I didn't want to be like that. I dedicated an entire chapter to bonuses in my offers book: $100M Offers."*

See [[bonus-stacking-system]] for the bonus mechanic.

### Optimizing The Pay-Now vs Pay-Later Mix

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| Too many take Pay Later | Discount Pay Now more, better bonuses, or both |
| Too many take Pay Now | Opposite — increase Pay Later attractiveness |
| >10% of Pay Later people cancel | You promised too much, conditions too easy, or price too high |

> *"No matter how well you deliver, some people will cancel their payment. That's okay. Factor it in your costs of doing business."*

### Works For Recurring Revenue Too

> *"Give them the option to pay a higher ongoing rate 30 days later or they pay less today and keep the lower rate for good. Plus, add in some bonuses."*

### Pro Tip: Hint At Your Next Offer Early

The reading guru could have said: *"Everyone wants to know when my next reading intensive starts because they sell out so fast. I'll get to it at the end."*

This pre-suggests the upsell early, **getting more people to buy it later**.

Hormozi accidentally discovered this with nutrition consultations:

> *"One tired day I spouted 'everyone wants to know what supplements to buy. We'll get there — I promise. But please pay attention to the nutrition section — it matters more.' By accident, I implied everyone bought supplements without offering them."*

The implication created the upsell. **All the head nods showed they wanted more products.** A happy mistake he repeated.

## Summary Points

- **Pay Later** = delayed payment + conditional guarantee
  - Clear criteria, easy measurement
  - Aligns with what gets customer the most value
- **Pay Now** = 20-50% discount + bonuses
  - Offer Pay Now AFTER they accept Pay Later
  - They get discount + bonuses instead of guarantee
- Promise must be easy to track, hard to refute, clear yes/no
- If >10% cancel: promised too much, criteria too easy, or price too high
- For events/workshops: hint at next offer early

---

## Hormozi's Distinction: Pay Less Now vs Trial With Penalty

> *"I use Pay Less Now or Pay More Later as a downsell for physical products or one-time services. And I use Trial With Penalty as a downsell for recurring products or services."*

But this chapter is in the *Attraction* section because Hormozi uses this play to **attract** customers, while [[trial-with-penalty-downsell]] is a different chapter used as a **downsell** for recurring businesses.

The mechanics overlap, but the placement differs.
