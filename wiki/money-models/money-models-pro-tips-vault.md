## Anchoring

### **The Worst Mistake In Business Selling**
> *"The Only Thing Worse Than Making A $1,000 Offer To A Person With A $100 Budget . . . is Making A $100 Offer To Someone With A $1,000 Budget."*

> *"In the first situation you lose $100. In the second, you lose $900."*

Always have a premium offer ready, even if most won't buy.
→ [[anchor-upsell]]

### "The Gasp"
The customer gasping at the premium price is the *signal that it's working*. The bigger the gasp, the more they buy.
→ [[anchor-upsell]]

### Primary vs Secondary Features
Keep primary features same in main offer; change secondary features in premium.
→ [[anchor-upsell]]

### The Economist Play
3 options where the third (bundle) is priced the same as the most expensive standalone → people pick the bundle.
→ [[menu-upsell-system]]

## Pricing & Money Mechanics

### **The Highest-Value Tip In The Entire Book**
> *"Bill weekly (weekly, every 2 weeks, 4 weeks, 12 weeks etc)."*

> *"There are 12 months in a year, but the year has 13 four-week cycles. That's an 8.3% difference."*

> *"If I offer my thing at '$100 every four weeks' (versus $100 a month) the same number of people buy. But, I make 8.3% more annually."*

> *"To put this into perspective, if your business has 20% margins, this skyrockets annual profit by 41%."*

→ [[continuity-discount-offer]]

### 3% Processing Fee
> *"Get 3% More Revenue For Five Extra Words. 'Yea, it's $X plus a 3% processing fee.'"*

For a 10% margin business → 30% profit increase. **Pure profit for five words.**
→ [[continuity-discount-offer]]

### Get Two Forms Of Payment (Via Processing-Fee-Waiver)
Customers gladly give a second card to save 3%. Reduces declined-payment losses.
→ [[continuity-discount-offer]]

### Get ACH If You Can
Cheapest non-cash transaction. Direct bank link.
→ [[continuity-discount-offer]]

### Align Payment Schedules With Paychecks
Higher chance of successful payments. Hormozi recoups ~1/3 of declined payments by running cards multiple times on paycheck days.
→ [[payment-plan-downsell]]

### ProfitWell Churn Data (5× Lower With Annual)
| Billing | Monthly Cancellation |
|---|---|
| Monthly | 10.7% |
| Quarterly | 5% |
| Annual | 2% |

→ [[payment-plan-downsell]]

### Make Bonuses Anchor Real
> *"Some business owners make up ridiculous values. Don't do this. It won't anchor the customer and you'll lose trust."*

Anchor bonus values against products you've actually sold at that price.
→ [[continuity-bonus-offer]]

## Discounting & Free

### Four Ways To Advertise A Discount
For year-long $100/mo → $900 prepay:
1. Percentage Off: "25% off"
2. Absolute Amount: "$300 off"
3. Free Portion: "3 Months Free"
4. The Total Package: "One Year For $900 ($1,200)"

Test which converts best in your market.
→ [[decoy-offer]]

### Discount Offers Have Higher Show-Up Rates Than Free Offers
Free → more leads, lower show rate
Discount → fewer leads, higher show rate
Especially important for high-cost-of-no-show businesses (doctors, dentists, lawyers).
→ [[decoy-offer]]

### Buy X Get Y Free > Discount Framing
> *"Free drives more interest than a discount. More people know the value of free than the value of one shirt."*

→ [[buy-x-get-y-free-offer]]

### More Free Than Paid Wins
> *"Buy ten get two free is not as strong as buy two get ten free."*

→ [[buy-x-get-y-free-offer]]

### More Cheaper Free Things > Fewer Expensive Free Things
For the same cost, test "Buy 1 shirt get 3 socks free" vs "Buy 1 shirt get 1 shirt free."
→ [[buy-x-get-y-free-offer]]

### Discounts Get Cards On File
$1 month works better than free month if you want to confirm the card works for auto-bill.
→ [[trial-with-penalty-downsell]]

## Downsell Specific

### "Personalize, Don't Pressure"
The downsell rule. Find what they like / don't like → adjust offer.
→ [[downsell-offers-overview]]

### "Don't Drop Your Price Just To Get Somebody To Buy"
That's discounting, not downselling. Change *what* they get, not price for the same thing.
→ [[downsell-offers-overview]]

### "You Can Offer Something Different For Less. You Just Can't Offer The Same Thing For Less."
The trust-preservation rule.
→ [[downsell-offers-overview]]

### Customers Talk About Price
If they find out someone else got the same thing cheaper, you lose them all.
→ [[downsell-offers-overview]]

### "Never Negotiate The Price"
> *"People who demand to pay less for the same thing are business terrorists. I don't negotiate with terrorists."*

→ [[feature-downsell]]

### The 1-10 Temperature Check
After 2 refusals, ask "On a scale 1-10 how bad do you want this?"
- 8+: Payment plan
- 7 or less: Feature downsell
→ [[payment-plan-downsell]] + [[feature-downsell]]

### "Ask Deal? Or Fair Enough?"
After each downsell concession. *"This works astonishingly well."*
→ [[feature-downsell]]

### "Just Call It A Trial"
For Trial With Penalty — calling it anything more complicated scares customers.
→ [[trial-with-penalty-downsell]]

### Free Orientations Boost DIY Downsells
After refusing all DFY offers, invite to a free orientation. Half show. Most buy supplements/DIY products.
→ [[feature-downsell]]

### Feature Downsell Your Guarantees
Most powerful re-upsell mechanic — people see the guarantee's value only after losing it.
→ [[feature-downsell]]

### Barter With Reviews, Testimonials, Referrals
$100 off in exchange for reviews on all sites + video testimonial + 3 referrals.
→ [[feature-downsell]]
