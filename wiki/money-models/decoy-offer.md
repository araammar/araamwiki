# Attraction Offer #3: Decoy Offer
tags: [money-models, Hormozi, attraction-offer, decoy, premium]

## Summary / Definition

> *"Decoy Offers advertise something free or discounted. Then, when leads ask to learn more, you also present a more valuable premium offer. The premium offer provides more features, benefits, bonuses, guarantees, and so on."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Money Models*

The play that **saved Hormozi's gym** when leads got too expensive. Saved Gym Launch from stagnation. Eight out of ten people took the $399 premium over the free option.

---

## The John "Turkey Talk" Origin Story (June 2014)

John, an early mentor, retired early. Drove Hormozi to his lake house and taught him the **$5 5-Day VIP Tanning Pass**:

> *"You see, the beauty of the 5-Day Pass is everyone thinks they can get tan in five days. And they can. But, it's never as tan as they want to get. And if they try to 'speed things up' . . . they'll burn. So, when someone comes in with a pass, we ask them how tan they wanna get. As soon as they say they want to get a few shades darker, we give them 'the turkey talk.'"*

**The Turkey Talk:**

> *"Say a Thanksgiving turkey takes three hours to cook. We all know what happens if you double the temperature to cook it in half the time — you burn it! It takes at least five to ten sessions to get the color they want without burning."*

Then the pivot:

> *"Let's just credit your VIP pass toward your first month. Why buy so many $25 day passes when members get unlimited access for just $19.99?"*

> *"They immediately see the value and take the membership. Easy as that."*

## Five Years Later — The Save (2019)

Hormozi's gym fitness leads got too expensive. Most reps were barely breaking even.

The team recalled the $5 VIP Tanning play:

> *"Why don't we offer something super cheap to get the leads, but when they come in, make them a crazy offer that costs more but is 100x better. They can still take the cheap thing, but we'll just explain that they'll get way better results with extra accountability, nutrition, etc."*

Two weeks later:
- **Free option**: 1 session per week
- **$399 Premium option**: Unlimited sessions, 1-on-1 coaching, personalization, results guarantee

> *"We got about eight out of ten people to take the $399 option. We're crushing it."*

> *"Awesome — let's scale it!"*

## The Underlying Principle

> *"You have to know what gets results for customers better than they do. This makes our premium offer the clear solution."*

The decoy doesn't disqualify the lead — it qualifies the *premium* offer as the clear winner. The free option is real but obviously inferior.

---

## The 2-Step Process

### Step 1
Advertise a lesser, smaller, or simpler version of your premium offer as a **decoy**.

### Step 2
When leads engage, offer both options, but emphasize the premium one.

---

## Examples Across Industries

### Lemonade Stand (Physical Products)
- **Attraction Offer**: "Free Week of Lemonade" OR "$1 Week of Lemonade"
- **Decoy Option**: Water + powdered lemon + corn syrup
- **Premium Option**: Organic, all-natural, vegan, gluten-free, imported Italian lemons, cold-processed, shipped to your door, includes sparkling rosewater flavor

### Float Tank Center (Service)
- **Attraction Offer**: "Free 6-Week Stress Release" OR "$6 6-Week Stress Release"
- **Decoy**: One float per month + at-home DIY stress exercises
- **Premium**: 2× floats per week × 6 weeks + 1:1 consulting + journal + sleep routine + satisfaction guarantee

### Gym (Local Business)
- **Attraction Offer**: "Free 21-Day Transformation" OR "$21 21-Day Transformation"
- **Decoy**: Workouts in a Skool.com group + general nutrition plan + no support + no guarantee
- **Premium**: Unlimited workouts + personalized nutrition plan + 1:1 accountability + results guaranteed (or another 21 days free)

## Important Notes

### How To Make Your Decoy

> *"Offer fewer components, older models, or fewer personalized versions of your premium offer. Also, remove any guarantees."*

The Attraction Offer only needs to get them to engage. Nothing more.

### Advertise Benefits, Not Features

> *"We want to sell them on the dream outcome. We advertise a transformation in 21 days, not workouts and meal plans. Leads get specific product details in the sales presentation, not in the advertising!"*

> *"Private jets and rowboats can both get you to an exotic island, but the premium option is certainly more enjoyable."*

### Four Ways To Advertise A Discount

For a year-long $100/mo thing → $900 prepay option:

1. **Percentage Off**: "25% off"
2. **Absolute Amount**: "$300 off"
3. **Free Portion**: "3 Months Free"
4. **The Total Package**: "One Year For $900 ($1,200)"

Same math. Test which converts best.

### Make The Contrast Huge

> *"The value of the premium option comes from huge differences with the decoy option. So make the decoy option as basic as reasonable. Then make the premium option as awesome as possible. The bigger the contrast, the better the deal, and the more customers will take it."*

### Discount Offers Have Higher Show-Up Rates Than Free Offers

> *"If you run a Free Attraction offer, you'll get more leads. If you run a Discount Offer, you'll get fewer leads, but a higher percentage will show up."*

Important for high-cost-of-no-show businesses (doctors, lawyers, dentists).

### Present The Premium First (If Possible)

> *"In a perfect world, they take the premium offer immediately. The decoy offer stays in your back pocket. If they come in specifically asking for the decoy option up front..."*

**Get them to give you permission to sell them**:

> *"Are you here for free stuff or lasting results?"*

If they say "results," skip to premium.

If they say "free stuff," present decoy → contrast with premium → ask:

> *"Which do you think will get you to your goal faster?"*
>
> Or: *"Which would you prefer: XXX less valuable benefit or YYY more valuable benefit 1, 2, 3...?"*

### The Assumed Close

> *"You want to talk to the lead as if you already know they will accept your offer. Many salespeople refer to this as an 'assumed close.' You operate from a position of: This is what everyone does. This is just a formality. Let me get your ID and credit card so you can get your value. No hype. Just friendly disposition. Almost bored over how regularly people buy."*

### Surprise Benefit (Optional)

> *"If someone takes the decoy option, you can choose to surprise them with a few low-/zero-cost features from your premium offer. Just say something like 'hey, I'm gonna throw this in, even though it's part of our premium offer just because I want you to get great results.'"*

Builds goodwill, overdelivers, increases chance they take future upsells.

---

## Summary Points

- Decoy Offers advertise free or discounted, then offer a premium upgrade when leads engage
- Make the premium far more valuable than the decoy (features, benefits, bonuses, guarantees)
- Strip the decoy down as much as reasonable
- When leads ask about the decoy, present the premium right next to it
- Ask "are you here for free stuff or lasting results?" to skip to premium
- You still make money from decoy-takers (they convert later)
- Expect to make money fast — if you don't, make the contrast bigger
