# Framework: The Four Offer Types
tags: [money-models, Hormozi, framework, offers, structure]

## Summary / Definition

The structural framework of every Money Model. Four offer types in deliberate sequence:

| # | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Attraction Offers** | Turn strangers into customers |
| 2 | **Upsell Offers** | Get people to spend more cash |
| 3 | **Downsell Offers** | Get people to say yes when they would have said no |
| 4 | **Continuity Offers** | Keep people buying |

> *"All improve our Money Model, but they all do it differently. They work great on their own, but together they make your business unstoppable."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Money Models*

---

## The Sequence Logic

Hormozi explains why all four matter, in sequence:

> *"If you don't have an offer for getting customers, you won't get as many. But let's say you do. If you only have that one thing to offer, you won't make nearly as much money as you could. So if you have something to offer next, an upsell, you'll finally get some cash. But, you still won't make as much as you could because lots of people will still say 'no.' So, we turn those 'nos' into 'yeses' with downsells. And that works fine. But it would be even better if you had that extra cash guaranteed to come in month after month. So, you make a continuity offer to top it off. That's how I like to do it."*

Each layer compensates for what the previous one leaves on the table.

---

## Why Most Businesses Are Stuck

Most businesses run on **one** of these four. Sometimes two. Almost never all four.

- Retail typically has only Attraction (sales, discounts)
- Service businesses typically have only Continuity (monthly retainer)
- Consulting typically has only Upsell (one-time engagement)

The book's claim: businesses that combine **all four** in deliberate sequence achieve 30-day profit per customer that bootstraps unlimited acquisition. Businesses that miss any one of them either run out of cash or cap out at a plateau.

## The 7 Ground Rules

Before diving into specific plays, Hormozi lays out 7 rules:

### Rule 1: All Businesses Have Money Models. It Makes A Business A Business.

> *"Switch the poor person mantra 'this won't work for my business' to the rich person mantra 'how will I make this work for my business?' They all work. Be creative."*

### Rule 2: Some Money Models Work Better In Some Businesses Than Others

The plays are different tools, not universal templates. You must design your own.

### Rule 3: If A Customer Asks For Their Money Back — Give It Back

> *"Avoid the headache. And if you made a goof — fix the goof. Don't be a silly goose. Treat customers well. Next time, spend the time and resources to get better customers."*

This is also the doctrine baked into [[win-your-money-back-offer]]: don't take "blood money."

### Rule 4: Hard Selling Is For Weak Products

> *"If someone doesn't want something, that's OK. Don't convince someone against their will. Make offers available at the time your customer has a problem and you'll be ahead of the competition."*

Don't argue. Make the offer at the right moment. Move on if they decline.

### Rule 5: Obey The Law

Advertising laws change. Especially around "free" claims. Check with legal counsel.

### Rule 6: Be Transparent

> *"State the facts. And if the facts aren't compelling, change reality to make them compelling or learn to frame them in a way that is. Don't lie. You'll short-change yourself long-term. And unlike credit card debt, you can't file bankruptcy to erase a bad reputation."*

### Rule 7: Any Offer Can Be Used On Its Own, At Any Time, In Any Order

> *"A business works as long as it makes a profit. Most offers in this book could meet that minimum requirement on their own. When used in the right sequence, and at the right time, they make a $100M Money Model."*

Treat the four-type framework as a default — but recombine freely as needed.

## How Each Type Fits The Cash-Flow Story

Per [[money-models-master-hub]], the goal is recovering CAC + delivery in 30 days.

| Type | When It Cashes In | What It Does To 30-Day Profit |
|---|---|---|
| Attraction Offer | Day 0 (entry) | Gets the customer in the door, covers initial costs |
| Upsell Offer | Days 0-30 (immediate to early) | Adds margin per customer |
| Downsell Offer | Day 0 (when they say no to upsell) | Converts otherwise-lost sales |
| Continuity Offer | Day 30+ (and forever after) | Compounds lifetime value |

Stages I + II + III (Attraction, Upsell, Downsell) all aim at the 30-day window. Stage IV (Continuity) extends past it.

## The Underlying Mental Model

> *"When an offer solves a problem, another appears. You upsell the solution to the problem your offer reveals. So, every offer opens the door to an upsell . . . even upsells!"*

The Money Model is the operational expression of the **Problem-Solution Cycle** (also referenced in [[lead-magnets-framework]]):

1. Customer has Problem A → Offer 1 solves Problem A but reveals Problem B
2. Offer 2 solves Problem B but reveals Problem C
3. Offer 3 solves Problem C but reveals Problem D
4. ... infinite cascade until the customer's full need is met

Each Money Model offer is a "next step" in this cascade. The art is sequencing them so the cascade compounds cash.

## The Burger Shop Math (Why Upsells Matter)

To make the upsell-power tangible:

| Customer Buys | Profit |
|---|---|
| Just the burger ($2) | $0.25 |
| + Fries upsell | $1.00 (4× the original) |
| + Make it a meal (drink) | $2.00 (8×) |
| + Supersize the meal | **$3.00 (12×)** |

> *"This little burger place actually has a chance at succeeding."*

The implication: **your first offer doesn't always make the profit.** The thing you sell the most isn't always the thing you make the most profit on.

> *"If you want to win, you have to figure out your version of 'Do you want fries with that?' If you don't, others will."*

## When You Don't Need All Four

Hormozi notes you can run a profitable business on 1, 2, or 3 of these — but you cap.

| Stack | Result |
|---|---|
| Attraction only | Get customers, cap on margin |
| Attraction + Upsell | Get customers, decent margin, cap on conversion |
| Attraction + Upsell + Downsell | Get customers, decent margin, max conversion, cap on LTV |
| All four | The $100M Money Model |

The book assumes you want all four eventually — but explicitly recommends building one at a time. See [[make-your-money-model]].
