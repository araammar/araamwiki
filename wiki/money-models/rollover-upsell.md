# Upsell #4: The Rollover Upsell
tags: [money-models, Hormozi, upsell, rollover, winback, gift-card]

## Summary / Definition

> *"Rollover Upsells credit some or all of a customer's previous purchases toward your next offer."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Money Models*

The Upsell that "**changed Hormozi's life, thousands of gym owners' lives, and the lives of customers.**" The missing link in his Money Model.

---

## The Justin Story (June 2014)

Hormozi had been running a Win Your Money Back Offer at his gym. $600 fitness program — members win their money back if they hit a goal. Sold tons. But:

> *"Most winners put their $600 toward three months of membership. Fine. But then they churned out before their first out-of-pocket payment. So I essentially sold 'buy six weeks get three months free.' Then, they'd leave. Not fine."*

He saw Justin posting numbers way higher than his best performers. He flew over to spy.

> *"Do you get lots of people winning their money back?"*
> *"Yeah."*
> *"Then how do you deal with all the free time you have to give away?"*
> *"Free time? Ha! I just roll over their winnings into a year long membership."*

Hormozi's face:
> *"What?"*

Justin's mechanic:
> *"We just give them fifty bucks off per month for a year."*
> *"So even though they won their money back, they start paying immediately?"*
> *"Of course. I don't want people not paying. What sort of business doesn't have paying customers?? They still get their money back . . . it just takes a year."*

> *"Boom. This was it. The missing link in my Money Model."*

The Rollover Upsell rescued Gym Launch's continuity revenue.

---

## How It Works

> *"Instead of hoping customers spend money again, I roll over the cost of what they just bought toward the next thing. And when paired with more expensive offers, it skyrockets 30-day profits."*

> *"You can Rollover Upsell anyone anything. (Even stuff people bought at other businesses . . . mwahaha)."*

Three questions to answer:

### Who To Upsell (4 Targets)

1. **Re-engage customers who left a while ago** (winback)
2. **Rescue your own upset customers** (refund alternative)
3. **Rescue other people's upset customers** (steal competitors' customers)
4. **Upsell regular customers**

### What To Upsell

- More of what they got
- Better version
- Something new
- **Make sure you profit after applying credit**

### How To Roll Over Credit

- Up front (front-loaded discount)
- At the end (earn it)
- Spread over time (monthly discount)
- Mix of the above

## Examples

### Chiropractor — Winback Campaign
- **Who**: Customers with 6+ months since last purchase
- **What**: New treatment plan
- **How**: Up front credit

Script:
> *"Hi Mrs. Banks, I wanted to give you your money back, do you have a minute? Great, yea. I wanted to see how your back pain is going? Oh, I'm sorry to hear that. Well, I have some good news. As a way of saying thank you, I want to give you $500 of your money back as credit towards staying pain-free for good. Is that of any interest?"*

### Dentist — Save Your Own Upset Customer
- **Who**: Upset customer
- **What**: Teeth whitening package
- **How**: Front-load $200 credit

Customer paid $200 for teeth cleaning but thinks teeth aren't whiter. Explain they need more → upsell into whitening package + apply the $200 already paid.

### Software — Rescue (*Cough* Steal) Competitor Customers
- **Who**: Competitors' customers
- **What**: Service agreement
- **How**: Roll over cost to break old agreement

Script:
> *"Hi John, I saw your negative review on their product and it really upset me. To make it up to you, I'll credit whatever payments you have left with them to switch to ours. This way, you don't lose a thing and you start getting the benefits now. Fair enough?"*

### Membership — Spread First Purchase Over A Term
- **Who**: Current customers
- **What**: 12-month membership
- **How**: Spread out first purchase

$600 first purchase → $50/mo discount for 12 months on a 12-month membership.

## Important Notes

### Use Rollover Offers To Attract New Customers

> *"Roll over some or all of what customers paid somebody else towards your thing."*

> *"You can find leads for this by scraping contact information from negative product reviews where available. Voila — a hot new list of leads who need what you have."*

Bonus: Create a way for people to complain about products in your industry (any media with comments). Then Rollover Upsell all of them.

> *"Nasty."*

### Do Rollover Upsells Before Refunding

> *"This has saved me tons of customers and cash. If you did a bad job (hey, it happens), roll over for a 'do over.'"*

### Reach Out To Old Customers

> *"I called these 'winback campaigns.' I made personalized videos for 200 past customers offering them $4,000 of credit to return. We got about 20% to take the offer. One day of recording videos got us an extra ~$1,900,000 of annual revenue. Worth It."*

**$1.9M in ARR from one day of recording videos.**

### Add Urgency

> *"If you're spicy, make the moment you present the offer the time to take it. A once-in-a-customer-lifetime offer. They don't get to sleep on it."*

> *"That's the point! You want to surprise and delight. So if they want the credit, they've gotta take it now."*

### How To Price

> *"To make money on a discounted offer, you must have profit left after you discount it. Since I prefer to make a profit, I try to make the upsell offer at least four times more than their rollover credit. So even if I apply the whole amount of the first purchase, it discounts 25% at most."*

### You Don't Need To Credit The Entire First Purchase

Roll over whatever amount incentivizes them. Test to find the sweet spot.

## Hormozi's "Famous" Gift Card Play

> *"You can use the Rollover Upsell as an Attraction Offer for new and current customers by advertising gift cards for 90%+ off."*

Mechanic:
- Sell $200 gift cards for $20
- Limit to 2 per customer
- Specify they can only use them on **other people** (referrals/gifts)
- Becomes a great holiday/lead-magnet offer

When customers buy the gift card, ask:
- Who to make it out to
- Will they make an introduction

When the friend comes in, **roll the gift card over** — make the gift card value 20% of the price of whatever you want to sell next.

Example math: $200 gift card / $1,000 next offer = 20% discount.

> *"People pay you to refer their friends. It's pretty great. Plus, you get some pocket change from unused gift cards."*

---

## Summary Points

- Rollover Upsells credit previous purchases toward next offer
- 4 targets: old customers / your upset customers / competitors' upset customers / current customers
- 3 credit-application methods: up front / at end / spread out
- Price next offer at least 4× higher than the credit (25% max discount)
- Add urgency: one-time only
- Use to attract new customers from competitors via negative reviews
- Gift card play: sell 90%+ off → use gift card value as 20% rollover credit on next offer

---

## Why This Was Gym Launch's Magic Bullet

The Rollover Upsell solved Gym Launch's biggest cash-flow problem. Before: customers won money back, redeemed for months of membership, then churned before paying anything. After: their winnings became a $50/mo discount on a 12-month contract — **customers started paying immediately while still getting their money back.**

This single mechanic stabilized recurring revenue across the entire Gym Launch licensee network.

## Application For Caret/Colandrum

### Winback Campaign
Identify churned Colandrum subscribers. Reach out:

> *"Hi [name], I wanted to give you $1,500 in Colandrum credit back. Now that we've launched [new feature], it solves the exact problem you mentioned when you cancelled. Wanna apply the $1,500 toward your next year? Just an extra discount on top of our 20% renewal offer."*

### Competitor Steal
Find agencies who left a competitor pitch platform. Reach out:

> *"Hi [name], saw your post about leaving [competitor]. To make the switch painless, we'll credit your remaining months on their platform as Colandrum credit. Sound fair?"*

### Save Customer
A 3-month customer wants to cancel. Roll over their 3 months ($X) as credit toward an annual prepay (4× more expensive than monthly).
