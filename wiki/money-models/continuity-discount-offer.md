# Continuity #2: Continuity Discount Offer
tags: [money-models, Hormozi, continuity, discount, weekly-billing]

## Summary / Definition

> *"To make a one-time continuity discount, you give products or services away for free if the customer commits to buying more products and services over time."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Money Models*

The Continuity Offer that attracts loads of customers with simple structure — and includes **the highest-value tip in the entire book** (weekly billing for 8.3% extra annual revenue).

---

## The Austin Trash Legend Story (Spring 2018)

Hormozi and Leila moved to a nicer Austin suburb. On a walk, a neighbor invited them over. Perfect yard. Ferrari for "spring cleaning." Patio table with cigarettes and beer cans.

A character emerged — backwards hat, flip flops, strong Midwest accent, widest grin imaginable.

> *"Hey brotherman! Nice to meetcha. I can tell yer no doctor or lawyer livin' here so young. So what kinda hustle you got?"*

Hormozi explained Gym Launch. *"What about you?"*

> *"Trash."*

The Money Model:

> *"I knew from my time workin' trash there wasn't much competition. Big commercial places and all went to the same place for their trash needs."*

> *"I had a truck, took my credit card, and I gambled. I went to all the big apartments and said I'd do their trash for a whole year free if they contracted me to do the next five years paid."*

> *"It worked good enough. They all made me their trash man before I knew it."*

Hormozi:
> *"Dang, you fronted an entire year?"*

> *"Uh-huh. And I'll tell you what, it was the toughest thing I ever did. No one would invest in my business, not even my family. They all called me crazy. But after that one year mark passed, the cash came floodin'."*

He later sold the business *"for a pretty penny."*

> *"There's cash in trash baby, what can I say."*

## How It Works

Give products or services free if the customer commits to buying more over time.

> *"This can attract loads of potential customers and makes an easy sale anyone can close."*

Works in: internet, pool cleaning, gym memberships, landscaping, anything rentable.

Two things to know:
1. How you'll apply the discount (4 ways)
2. Your cancellation policy

---

## 4 Ways To Apply The Discount

### Up Front
- Discount applied immediately
- "Official" time starts after free time ends
- Works best in industries that have a history of enforcing contracts (cell phones, storage, real estate, equipment, collateral-backed)
- If you have high churn → skip this and use one of the others
- **Doesn't get customers profitably — delays cash**

### At The End
- Customer earns the free time after completing the term
- They make all payments on time → get bonus time at end
- They earn their free time

### Spread Over Time
- Apply across the entire term
- 3 months free over 12 months = $50/mo discount ($600/12)
- Can offer to extend the lifetime discount if they make all payments on time

### After The First 1-2 Payments
- They pay a few times, then get their one-time discount
- Collects cash to cover advertising + early delivery costs
- Frame as "first and last month," "last month up front," or activation fee
- Ensures the customer uses a valid payment method

## ⚡ Highest-Value Tip In The Book — Weekly Billing

> ***Highest Value Per Word Note In This Book** Skip this if you hate money. Bill weekly (weekly, every 2 weeks, 4 weeks, 12 weeks etc).*

> *"Here's why. There are 12 months in a year, but the year has 13 four-week cycles. That's an 8.3% difference."*

> *"If I offer my thing at '$100 every four weeks' (versus $100 a month) the same number of people buy. But, I make 8.3% more annually."*

> *"To put this into perspective, if your business has 20% margins, this skyrockets annual profit by 41%."*

> *"And the best part is, you don't do any more work. Just change a few words. What else can you do legally that makes so much money for so little work? This has literally made me millions in pure profit. So yeah, do it."*

**The math**:
- 12 months × $100 = $1,200/year (monthly billing)
- 13 four-week cycles × $100 = $1,300/year (every-4-weeks billing)
- 100% same customer count
- $100 difference → goes straight to bottom line
- For 20% margin business → 41% profit increase

This is one of the highest-leverage operational changes documented anywhere in the Hormozi trilogy.

## Important Notes

### Don't Eat Into The Term — Extend It

> *"Let's say you offer three months free when you sign up for a year. That could mean they pay for nine months then get three free (12 total months). Or, that could mean they pay 12 months and get three free (15 total months). I prefer to start with extending the term."*

Then you can [[feature-downsell|Feature Downsell]] to a shorter one.

### 3% More Revenue For Five Extra Words

> *"Yea, it's $X plus a 3% processing fee."*

> *"In my life, I've never had anyone not buy because of a processing fee. But 3% added to your topline for no extra work goes straight to your bottom line. If you run a 10% profit business, and add 3%, you just added 30% to your profit."*

### Get Two Forms Of Payment

Recurring businesses lose mountains of cash because of payment processing problems.

Solution: ask if they want to save the 3% processing fee.

> *"Do you want to save the processing fee? . . . awesome. Give us a second form of payment in case anything happens to the first one."*

If they ask why:
> *"We only have the processing fee because it costs us man hours to get new payment information every month from our customers. So if you save us time, we pass the savings onto you."*

### Get ACH If You Can

ACH = direct bank account link. Cheapest transaction method besides cash.

### Gift Cards As Lead Magnets

> *"Give the discounted time in the form of a physical gift card. You can mail it to them if they are out of the area. The customer can apply the discount whenever they want after the first three payments or so."*

> *"Then you can say they can also gift it to a friend if they want. And now you've got a lead magnet! Beyond that, many people simply forget to use it. In that instance, you just got a full-priced sign-up. Nice!"*

### Lifetime Discount At Your Most Common Churn Point

Make customers earn the lifetime discount by staying past the most common churn point.

> *"Let's say you know every customer stays four months on average. You'd tell everyone up front they get a lifetime discount after month four. As the time approaches, tell them their new lower rate is right around the corner."*

Real-world example: A rice company offered:
- One-time price
- 5% off subscription
- **15% off if they stay on subscription for 5 straight months**

The 5-month threshold was *just beyond* where most people canceled. Brilliant.

## Cancellation Policy (Required Up Front)

Common policies: 30 or 60 days notice, cancellation fees, cancel anytime.

Hormozi's favorite (works with all Continuity Offers that have discount entry):

> *"Just make the cancellation fee equal to the discount they agreed to get. So if they got $600 in discounts by committing, they can pay $600 whenever they want to cancel. This is simple to explain."*

### Make Sure Customers Know How To Cancel

> *"If customers have nowhere to complain inside your business, they will definitely complain outside of your business."*

Make cancellation visible and easy. Otherwise: vanished customers + bad reviews + no chance to save them.

### Always Do An Exit Interview

> *"Some people like to vent. Let them. Get more angry about the problem than them. They may try to calm you down. Sometimes, they will save themselves!"*

Hormozi also routinely [[rollover-upsell|Rollover Upsells]] cancelling customers into a higher-cost tier (when they wanted features they didn't have).

> *"I've had many people buy a lower cost offer and then complain because they wanted the higher cost features. So I offer higher cost features, and they buy."*

### Use Cancellation Fees To The Customer's Advantage

> *"I'll waive your cancellation fee if you come in and tell me what I could do better."*

> *"I routinely save a third of customers that agree to exit interviews."*

## Summary Points

- Free time for committing to ongoing payments
- 4 ways to apply: up front / at end / spread / after first 1-2 payments
- Front-loaded converts more but churns more
- Back-loaded converts fewer but churns less
- Spread = balance both
- **Weekly billing = 8.3% more revenue, 41% more profit on 20% margin business**
- 3% processing fee = 30% profit boost (10% margin business)
- Second form of payment via processing-fee-waiver
- Gift card discounts double as lead magnets
- Lifetime discount at churn-point threshold
- Make cancellation easy + always do exit interviews

---

## Application For Caret/Colandrum

### Bill Every 4 Weeks Instead Of Monthly
- $1,000/mo → $1,000 every 4 weeks
- Adds 8.3% annual revenue without any other change
- 100% same conversion
- For Colandrum at scale, this could be $100K-$1M in pure profit

### 3% Processing Fee
- Add to all pricing
- Offer to waive for second payment method on file
- Lower churn from declined payments

### Lifetime Discount At Month 4
- Most B2B SaaS churns at months 3-4 (post-honeymoon)
- Offer: 20% lifetime discount if they stay through month 5
- Mention at onboarding, again at month 3, again at month 4
