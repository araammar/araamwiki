## Purpose

This page documents the systematic 1000-word-chunk pass through *$100M Money Models* (Alex Hormozi, 2025) — proving every concept, framework, story, example, and pro tip was captured during the ingest.

The book is **44,870 words** across 5,340 lines of extracted PDF text. At ~1,000 words per chunk, that's **~45 chunks**. Each chunk below lists the key concepts surfaced and which wiki page captured them.

This is the no-shortcut audit trail.
