## Origin & Setup Stories

### The Storage Unit Mentor (Hormozi's First Year)

Hormozi was sleeping in a utility closet next to his first gym. A storage-unit owner across the street invited him to 5 AM breakfast.

> *"How much time you got left to live?"*
> *"Huh?"*
> *"How much cash you got saved up?"*
> *"About five grand."*
> *"How much time does that give you?"*
> *"About a month."*

The mentor's classroom moment:
> *"How much does a free month of storage cost?"*
> *"Uh, nothing?"*

At the facility, the mentor walked through:
- Free month attracts customer
- Lock required → $47 (only Master locks fit)
- Boxes + tape + labels + markers
- Moving help → affiliate kickback from local mover
- Insurance: $500 free, $100K coverage for +$10/mo
- Customers always rent the wrong size → upsell larger

Total: "free" month costs the customer **$127**.

> *"And when you figure out how to make money in one business, and I mean really figure it out, you see ways to make money in any business."*

The mentor owned **27 locations**. Hormozi felt two inches tall.

### The $25K Phone Call (After 2.5 Years)

Hormozi now had 6 gyms. Paid $25K for an hour with a famous marketer doing $1M/mo.

Walked through his gym-opening system:
- $3K down for lease
- Few days of ads
- 20 leads/day × $5/lead
- 50% show, 25% close = $600 program
- + $80 supplement profit per customer
- **$1 in → $34 out in 48 hours**
- Plus $20K/mo membership for $3K down

The marketer:
> *"You shouldn't be running gyms. You should be showing other gym owners how to do what you just showed me."*
> *"You have a level-10 skill in a level-2 opportunity."*

Hormozi closed 1 gym, sold 5, went all-in on Gym Launch. Over 5 years took **$43M in owner distributions**. Sold 66% at **$46.2M valuation**. Crossed **$100M net worth at age 31.**

### The Rental Car Money Model (December 2019)

Hormozi reserved a $19/day car. Agent ran 5 offers:

1. **Vehicle Upgrade** to truck (big guy)
2. **Late Return** (flexible)
3. **Premium Insurance** (declined)
4. **Minimum Insurance Downsell** (after the no)
5. **Prepaid Gas** ($3.75 vs $3.50/gal — accepted to avoid airport rush)

**$19/day became $100/day. 5× the original.**

> *"And that's the power of a well designed Money Model. They knew everything I wanted (and things I didn't even know I would want). And when they offered them to me, I happily bought them."*

## Attraction Offer Stories

### Danny's Win Your Money Back Discovery (June 2013)

In a room of gym owners, Danny shared:

> *"I had this pain-in-the-butt guy who wouldn't buy anything. He knew he needed it but he also said he needed more accountability. So we were going back and forth, and finally he came up with this idea: 'I give you $500. You train me for eight weeks. And if I hit my goal, I get my money back. But in return, you can use my results to market your business.'"*

The guy hit the goal. Used his refund to buy more training. His before/after pictures brought 13 referrals.

> *"I offer this to everyone now. The results are way better and people love the offer."*

Hormozi adopted, scaled, and the offer drove **$1+ billion in industry-wide gym sales.**

→ Concept: [[win-your-money-back-offer]]

### The Fitness Certification Full-Ride + Partial Scholarship (August 2020)

Phone call with the owner of a fitness certification business:

> *"We advertise a full-ride scholarship. People apply with their contact information . . . The best answer gets a full-ride. But we also do something more . . . we give out partial scholarships."*

The mechanic: one full-ride winner, many partial scholarships. Partial scholarship is just the discounted price of the core offer.

> *"Most of them join our program on the spot."*

Plus he taught this exact play to the trainers he certified, who used it on their own fitness clients.

### John's Tanning Pass + The Turkey Talk (June 2014)

Hormozi's early mentor John taught him the **$5 5-Day VIP Tanning Pass**:

> *"Everyone thinks they can get tan in five days. And they can. But, it's never as tan as they want to get. And if they try to 'speed things up' . . . they'll burn."*

**The Turkey Talk:**
> *"A Thanksgiving turkey takes three hours to cook. We all know what happens if you double the temperature to cook it in half the time — you burn it! It takes at least five to ten sessions to get the color they want without burning."*

Pivot:
> *"Let's just credit your VIP pass toward your first month. Why buy so many $25 day passes when members get unlimited access for just $19.99?"*

### The Decoy That Saved Hormozi's Gym (2019)

Hormozi's gym fitness leads got too expensive. Most reps barely broke even.

The team recalled the $5 Tanning play. Built it:

- **Free option**: 1 session/week
- **$399 Premium**: Unlimited sessions + 1-on-1 coaching + personalization + results guarantee

Result: **8 out of 10 people took the $399 option.**

> *"Awesome — let's scale it!"*

### Boot Factory (Nashville 2020)

Downtown Nashville — Boot Factory dominated despite turnover everywhere else. Their sign:

> **BUY 1 PAIR GET TWO PAIR FREE**

A cowboy boot the size of a car directing traffic to the door.

Inside: marked-down "final offer" of **$600 for the pair.** Normal boots tripled in price because of the two free pairs.

> *"They charged three times the price for a single pair of boots because they came with two more pairs."*

Setting amplified it: tourist + cowboy bars + bachelorette parties = one-time buyers willing to spend big.

### The Reading Guru (June 2016)

> *"Double your reading speed in 3 hours, or it's free."*

Two options on the registration page:

- **Pay Later**: $0 now. Get billed $297 tomorrow. Cancel if your reading speed doesn't double. Must attend to qualify.
- **Pay Now**: $97 + recordings as a free bonus

Hormozi took Option 1. Training delivered. Speed doubled. He paid $297.

> *"He earned his $297. After that, he talked about how I could learn to read even faster with his eight-week training program. I was satisfied with my results, so I chose not to take the upsell. He taught me a skill I still use to this day. But, the true value came from learning a brand new Attraction Offer."*

### The Nutrition Consult Accidental Discovery

Hormozi was running nutrition consults. People interrupted constantly to ask about supplements. It annoyed him.

One tired day:
> *"Everyone wants to know what supplements to buy. We'll get there — I promise. But please pay attention to the nutrition section — it matters more."*

> *"By accident, I implied everyone bought supplements without offering them. And all the head nods I got showed they actually did want more products."*

He made sure to repeat the mistake.

> *"Wait a second, so you get them to pay for additional storage for the free ear muffs by getting them to say no? You guys are legends."*

Hormozi forgot his script and just asked:

She asked his favorite. Chocolate. She bought chocolate. Then:
> *"Do you want kiwi or strawberry lemonade pre-workout?"*

> *"After that conversation, I sold the next twenty customers in a row."*

**Takeaway**: A/B Upsell + Card On File.

### Justin's Rollover Save (June 2014)

Hormozi was running Win Your Money Back at his gym. Winners would put their $600 toward 3 months of membership — then churn before paying anything. He was bleeding recurring revenue.

He flew to spy on Justin who was crushing the numbers.

> *"Do you get lots of people winning their money back?"*
> *"Yeah."*
> *"Then how do you deal with all the free time you have to give away?"*
> *"Free time? Ha! I just roll over their winnings into a year long membership."*

Justin: *"We just give them fifty bucks off per month for a year. So even though they won their money back, they start paying immediately."*

> *"Boom. This was it. The missing link in my Money Model."*

## Downsell Stories

### Hormozi's First Payment Plan Sale (August 2013)

Hormozi's first month in business. One month's rent in savings. Never gotten a stranger to give him money. A lead said *"I can't afford it."*

In desperation:
> *"OK, when do you get paid?"*
> *"The first."*
> *"Okay, just put half down now, and half when you get paid."*
> *"I can't afford that either."*
> *"Okay — do you really wanna do this program?"*
> *"Yeah, I do."*

Several iterations later:
> *"Honestly, nothing. But I can pay for the whole thing on the first."*

Rent was due on the fifth. *"Sounds good. Just give me your card and I'll charge it on the second."*

Two weeks later — the card ran. First payment plan = success.

→ Concept: [[payment-plan-downsell]]

### The HR Software That Got Leila (Spring 2018)

Leila had been searching for HR software for Gym Launch's 100+ employees.

> *"They said if I did their training I'd get free onboarding. But, if I skipped the training I'd have to pay for it!"*

> *"I went through the training of course. And now I can actually use the complicated software too."*

Hormozi:
> *"You said no. Then, they downsold you a free trial on the condition that they could penalize you if you didn't use it?"*

The play forced Leila to actually use the product, which made her sticky.

### The Guarantee-Removal Downsell (2019)

A friend tripled his close rate from 25% to 75% by removing his guarantee as the downsell.

> *"If you don't want the option to get your money back, you can pay less. Or, you can keep your money-back guarantee — which would you prefer?"*

> *"Once they understand what they'd give up, they often say 'Screw it, I'd rather keep the guarantee and get my money back'."*

Result: 100 leads → 35 buy the main thing + 40 take the downsell = **75 total close rate**.

→ Concept: [[feature-downsell]]

## Continuity Stories

### The Gym That Tripled Membership Sales (Fall 2019)

A previously-struggling gym posted numbers higher than the best performers.

> *"I'm not really doing the six-week challenge."*
> *"What do you mean?"*

The mechanic: Run normal pitch on the challenge. As soon as they're interested, ask *"Want to get it for free?"* Yes. *"If you become a member, we'll make it free."* Plus member-exclusive bonuses (better class times, tanning, VIP events).

Before: 34/100 sign up for challenge, 17/100 convert
After: 15/100 sign up for challenge, **40/100 go straight into continuity**, + 8/40 take 6-month prepayment upsell

### The High-Ticket Legend's Million-Dollar Simplicity (January 2021)

> *"I'd rather make a few million bucks a year with zero employees and cool customers than build some gigantic business that panders to anyone willing to give me a buck."*

His sales mechanic:

> *"I tell customers they have two options: 'You can go month-to-month with a big setup fee. It covers the cost of getting you started, but you can leave whenever. Or, if you commit to a year, I'll waive the fee.'"*

> *"And I make the fee huge, so buyers commit to avoid it."*

> *"It costs a lot to quit in the beginning, so that keeps them engaged and — and once they pass that point, it costs about the same to cancel as it does to stick it out. So, they just stick it out."*
