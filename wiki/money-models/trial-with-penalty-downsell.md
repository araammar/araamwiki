# Downsell #2: Trial With Penalty
tags: [money-models, Hormozi, downsell, trial, penalty, fee]

## Summary / Definition

> *"In a Trial With Penalty offer, customers can try your product or service for free so long as they meet your terms. If they don't meet the terms, they pay the fee."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Money Models*

The inverse of [[win-your-money-back-offer]]:
- **Win Your Money Back** = pay up front, get money back if you do the work
- **Trial With Penalty** = start free, pay only if you don't do the work

---

## The HR Software Story (Spring 2018)

Gym Launch had 100+ employees. Leila needed better HR software.

> *"They got me."*
> *"Seriously? How did they manage that?"*
> *"They had a trial offer with a weird spin. It was pretty smart."*
> *"What did they offer?"*
> *"They said if I did their training I'd get free onboarding. But, if I skipped the training I'd have to pay for it!"*

> *"So you said no. Then, they downsold you a free trial on the condition that they could penalize you if you didn't use it?"*

> *"Basically. I mean, it makes sense. It forced me to learn, and now I don't want to learn anyone else's complicated software . . . so we're sticking with them!"*

The play forced Leila to actually use the product, which made her stickier.

---

## The Core Distinction

> *"So Trial With Penalty isn't 'here's my thing — see if you like it.' It's 'here's my thing, you get it for free so long as you do this stuff . . . which makes you a perfect fit for my next offer. And if you don't, then you have to pay for it.'"*

The criteria mirror what makes the best customers — usage, attendance, milestones.

---

## How It Increases Sales

> *"Normally, you get one chunk of people to buy your main offer. So, offer that first. And the rest, you'll get on this downsell."*

Worked example:
- Normally close 3 of 10 on Attraction Offer
- Now downsell another 4 on Trial With Penalty
- After trial ends, upsell 3 of them
- Go from 3 sales to 6 sales — **doubling customer count**

> *"I'm still irritated at the thousands of customers I've lost on free trials over the years before learning this."*

## Examples

### B2C: 28-Day Kick-That-Habit Blueprint
Get the trial free (avoid fee) by:
- Attending all consulting calls
- Posting progress in the group weekly
- Journaling daily in the app
- Attending feedback + transformation sessions (upsell opportunities)

### B2B: 5-Day Get-Your-First-5-Customers Challenge
Get the trial free (avoid fee) by:
- Sending 100 outbound messages per day
- Reporting message stats
- Attending daily training
- Posting homework completion in the group
- Attending the graduation call (upsell opportunity)

### Software: $500 Onboarding Waived
Get the $500 onboarding free if you:
- Attend onboarding (3 × 60-min Zoom calls = upsell opportunities)
- Do the homework
- Activate your employer profile
- Get employees set up by end of 3rd call

Otherwise: pay the $500.

## Important Notes

### What They Get + What They Have To Do

The valuable parts can be:
- Your bare-bones offer (like [[decoy-offer|Decoy Offer]])
- Your [[win-your-money-back-offer|Win Your Money Back]] offer

Recommendation: give more rather than less.

> *"The criteria should activate and retain customers. You can swipe these directly from Win Your Money Back."*

### Breaking Up Fees vs One Lump Fee

> *"Say you have a $500 product with ten things to do. I'd rather bill $50 for each mess up than one $500 fee on their first mess up."*

Different mechanic for different businesses. Both work.

### Offer The Trial Last (After They Say No to Everything Else)

> *"If someone makes it clear they don't want your first offer, then downsell the Trial With Penalty."*

Script:
> *"Hmmm . . . that sure is a pickle. I'll tell ya what. How about we just get ya started for free, would you be okay with that? We can just help you out, and if you like it, you can stay. Let me get your ID and we can get the process started — fair enough?"*

### Always Get A Credit Card

> *"What card do you wanna use?"*

If they balk: *"That's just how we've always done it."*

If they still refuse: wish them a lovely day. Show them out.

> *"Pro Tip: If someone doesn't agree to put their card down and do the work, I won't sell them. They complain more and they convert less. Not worth the hassle."*

### Always Sell Staying And Paying

> *"If this program got you the result, will you stay long-term?"*

You want them to commit to staying long-term if you get them results.

> *"I don't want you to try it. I want you to get results. And out of integrity, I want to set realistic goals. You are not going to hit your long-term goals during this trial. But, you will establish habits that will help you get there. And we're gonna help you do that for free. But if you wanna get to your long-term results, you're gonna have to stay on after."*

### Explain Fees AFTER Getting The Card

> *"If you explain the fees before you get the card, you will get more resistance. So explain after with a 'this is just how we've always done it' attitude."*

Script:
> *"We will do our part so long as you do yours. That's fair right? So now I just ask that you bet on yourself — if you miss or skip any stuff, your results will suffer. We charge to keep you on track. If you miss, no big deal. You'll get dinged a little fee but it'll get you back on track. If you follow through, you get all this for free."*

Hormozi has customers initial separately next to fee clauses to force salespeople to explain them.

## How To Upsell From A Trial (3 Scenarios)

### Scenario 1: They Like It
> *"This is the easy one. You already have them set up for automatic billing. Great! Meet with them anyway. You can still offer a longer term or higher value version of your service."*

### Scenario 2: They Hate It
> *"Turn that frown upside down. Ask them what they would have liked to be different. Tell them they're totally right, and that you're angry at yourself for missing this. Do not blame them. Only one person can be angry — and it needs to be you."*

> *"Ask if they'll give you a chance to make it up because of how outraged you are at their experience. And now, since you better understand their needs, that they're a better fit for your higher level thing."*

> *"I can get about half of these people to buy."*

### Scenario 3: They Didn't Use It
> *"Reach out to people multiple times before you get to this point. Explain that you need to meet with them. Offer to waive the fee if they do. Now, you can try to get them back on track or offer something better for them."*

> *"I don't like billing non-starters. A small fee isn't worth a 1-star review."*

## Important Operating Notes

### Tweak Your Trial To Get The Most Customers
- No one takes the trial → lower the requirements or penalties
- They take but don't follow through → emphasize how fees help them + make sales meetings mandatory
- They don't stay on the back end → emphasize value of staying, deliver better, ensure back-end fits front-end

### Let People Make Up For Goofs
> *"People often get discouraged after getting billed. But, you can offer an opportunity to 'make it up.' This does a great job of getting people back on track and converting."*

### Just Call It A Trial
> *"Even though the Trial With Penalty has some 'special features,' you should just call it a Free Trial. Otherwise, people may get scared and confused."*

If asked why: *"This is just how we've always done it"* or *"People get the best results this way."*

### Discounts Get Cards On File
For low-friction businesses, offer *"first month for $1"* instead of free. The small charge confirms the card works.

## Hormozi's Distinction: Pay Less Now vs Trial With Penalty

> *"I use Pay Less Now or Pay More Later as a downsell for physical products or one-time services. And I use Trial With Penalty as a downsell for recurring products or services."*

This is in the Downsell section because Hormozi uses it as a downsell — but the HR software company used it as an attraction offer. Either works.

> *"I have only made this work in businesses where the customer has to do work to get results. If you find other types of businesses these work for . . . let me know!"*

---

## Summary Points

- Free trial with conditions; pay penalty if you don't meet them
- Gets yeses from people who've said no
- Process: get the card → get the commitment → explain what to do + meetings required → explain consequences
- More paying customers than normal free trials because they actually use the product
- Use same criteria from Win Your Money Back
- Can break fees by criteria or lump fee
- Money comes from results-driven conversion, not fee-nickel-and-diming
- Mid-trial check-ins = more offers

## Application For Caret/Colandrum

For an agency that rejected the annual subscription:

> *"Tell ya what. Let me get you started on a 30-day free trial. Here's the deal: it's free if you:*
> *• Submit 5 pitches through the platform*
> *• Attend the kickoff + mid-trial + closeout call*
> *• Complete the integration setup in week 1*
>
> *Otherwise, you pay our standard $499 onboarding fee. Fair?"*

Then at the closeout call, upsell to annual subscription.
