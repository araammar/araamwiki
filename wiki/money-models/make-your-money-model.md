## The Gym Launch $100M Money Model Evolution

The case study at the heart of the chapter. Hormozi walks through the actual progression of Gym Launch — from a single Decoy Offer to a 4-quadrant Money Model.

### Phase 1: Decoy Offer Only → $476,000/mo (3 months)

> *"I attracted new customers with lots of free courses, books, video training, live training, and so on. All stuff on growing a gym. Each free product came with its own free call to help gym owners use it."*

The offer on the call:

**[[decoy-offer|Decoy Offer]]:**
- **Free DIY**: "Now that you've got the plan, you do it on your own for free."
- **Premium**: "We can help you implement all this stuff for $16,000 over 16 weeks."

Result: **$476,000/mo in three months.**

But:
> *"I had a problem. Since I only had one thing to sell, I knew my revenue would plateau fast. I needed an upsell to raise profits or Gym Launch would stagnate."*

### Phase 2: Adding Upsell + Downsell → $1.5M/mo

**[[classic-upsell|Classic Upsell]]:** "Gym Lords" at $42,000/year (advanced playbooks + community)
- $6,000 discount for prepay (= $36,000 prepaid)
- Used [[continuity-bonus-offer|Continuity Bonus]] (community access as the bonus)

**[[payment-plan-downsell|Payment Plan Downsell]]:** Seesaw downselling
- Start: $10,000 down with rest spread over time
- Then: $800/week for 52 weeks
- Then: Start free → [[continuity-discount-offer|Continuity Discount]] front-loads free time → roll into paid

Result: **~$1,500,000/mo**

### Phase 3: Menu + Feature Downsells → $2.3M/mo

The Upsell + Downsell process worked but some gym owners still said no. Built more options.

**[[menu-upsell-system|Menu Upsell]] with different service levels:**
- Done-for-you advertising
- Sales team training
- Turnkey campaigns
- **Minimum Package** (cheap monthly access to original materials + tech support)

**[[feature-downsell|Feature Downsell]]** to find the right fit for each gym

Result: **$2,300,000/mo within 14 months**

### Phase 4: Prestige Labs Integration → $4.4M/mo (Month 20)

Started Prestige Labs supplements + integrated with Gym Launch.

Result: **$4,400,000/mo by month 20**

> *"It was life-changing. And it *only* took a few darn good products and a $100M Money Model to do it."*

## The 3 Stages

> *"It rarely looks clean, but I break $100M Money Models into three stages:"*

| Stage | Goal | Offer Types |
|---|---|---|
| **Stage I: Get Cash** | Attract customers for less than you make | Attraction Offers |
| **Stage II: Get More Cash** | Upsells + Downsells to maximize 30-day profit | Upsell + Downsell Offers |
| **Stage III: Get The Most Cash** | Maximize customer's total money spent | Continuity Offers |

> *"I break my $100M Money Model down into these stages because Money Model growth happens alongside the growth of the business."*

> *"If you try to start a bootstrapped business, from zero, on your own, with a 'finished' Money Model . . . it will collapse on top of you."*

---

## The Build Sequence

> *"None of my businesses started with a fully forged Money Model. They all start at Stage I. Even Acquisition.com!"*

The actual evolution:

1. **Get customers reliably** (Attraction works)
2. **Make sure they pay for themselves reliably** (Attraction covers CAC + delivery)
3. **Make sure they pay for OTHER customers reliably** (Attraction generates excess cash for next CAC)
4. **Maximize each customer's long-term value** (Add Upsells + Downsells + Continuity)
5. **Spend as many advertising dollars as possible** (Print money via Stage IV CFA)

> *"My Money Models develop this way because I make sure each stage pays for the next. We keep improving each stage until it gets reliable."*

Warning:
> *"When your Money Model starts working, your business starts breaking. Part of the game. So I suggest you find someone who can build and lead the team that makes your vision a reality. When I did, I married her."*

## The 4-Step Build Process

### Step 1: Start With An Attraction Offer

> *"The goal is to turn strangers into customers and cover our costs."*

Pick one of:
- [[win-your-money-back-offer]]
- [[giveaways-offer]]
- [[decoy-offer]]
- [[buy-x-get-y-free-offer]]
- [[pay-less-now-pay-more-later-offer]]

Advertise it. If you get leads converting → you're on your way.

> *"Figuring out what works best may take up to a year."*

### Step 2: Pick An Upsell Offer

> *"The goal is to get 30-day profits well above our costs of getting a new customer and delivering what you offer to them."*

Pick one of:
- [[classic-upsell]]
- [[menu-upsell-system]]
- [[anchor-upsell]]
- [[rollover-upsell]]

Make the offer at customer's time of greatest need.

### Step 3: Pick A Downsell Offer

> *"The goal is to get customers who said no to your last offer to say yes to another offer."*

If keeping price the same → [[payment-plan-downsell]] or [[trial-with-penalty-downsell]]
If charging less → [[feature-downsell]]
Best: alternate between them.

### Step 4: Pick A Continuity Offer

> *"The goal here is to get one last sale in our 30-day window and stack recurring cash."*

Pick one of:
- [[continuity-bonus-offer]]
- [[continuity-discount-offer]]
- [[waived-fee-offer]]

> *"Sometimes the best timing for Continuity Offers happens after the first thirty days, and that's OK. It's better to make the offer at the right time than to try and force it at the wrong time."*

## Bootstrapped vs Funded — The Profit-First Mandate

> *"Unless you get outside investors . . . start with a fortune . . . or, have an endless source of free customers . . . achieving a Money Model is the only way you can profitably scale. Otherwise, you run out of cash and go out of business before you even have a chance."*

If you're bootstrapped, **the Money Model is the only way.**

## Important Notes

### Perfect One Offer At A Time

> *"It's tempting to implement a whole Money Model at once. Don't. Stick to your stage. Pick one offer. Try it. Keep doing it until it works reliably. Then, after it's reliable, do it so many times it gets automatic. Then, go to the next stage."*

> *"Patience is still the fastest way to get to your goal. So you'll need to measure in quarters, not weeks. You either build it right or you build it again. And again. And again."*

### Raise Price In Stages

> *"Make new offers cheap at first. Then, as you get yeses, raise the price. Lots of early yeses get customer feedback and make the product better."*

> *"Keep raising the price until you cannot make up for the nos with the extra cash you make from the yeses. In other words, keep raising the price until you make less money."*

### Simple Scales. Fancy Fails.

> *"Get as much as you can out of what you have. Remember, it's less about having 100 products to offer, and more about having 100 ways to offer your product."*

> *"If I offer personal training, I can offer one, two, three, four etc. sessions per week. This turns one product into many offers."*

### Affiliate Products Can Fill Money Model Gaps [IMPORTANT]

> *"An affiliate relationship just means you sell other peoples' stuff for a commission."*

Use cases:
- If you have nothing to sell yet → start as an affiliate
- If you have one offer and need more → offer affiliate products
- If you have a $100M business → add affiliate products with no operational headache

Examples:
- **Service**: Dental agency sends dentist clients to braces manufacturer → commission per referral
- **Local Business**: Massage therapist sells home massage tools, exercise bands → customer pays through therapist, drop-shipped
- **Digital Product**: Educator recommends specific CRM → commission per signup

> *"In short, you can always offer somebody else's stuff in your Money Model."*

### Turn Attraction Offers Into Continuity With Automatic Renewal

> *"If you do a Buy 6 Months Get 6 Months Free Offer, they can roll automatically into a month-to-month subscription at the end of 12 months. This gets the benefits of Attraction and Continuity Offers."*

A two-for-one mechanic. Most businesses miss this.

### Mix And Match Offers However You Want

> *"I present offers this way because that's how I use them. But if you recall, I learned many of them from people who used them differently than me!"*

You can:
- Use Upsell tactics in your Attraction Offer
- Install a Downsell process with every offer
- Use a Continuity Offer to attract new customers
- *"There are no rules. You can do whatever you want."*

> *"Start with the way I suggest it. Then, as you get better, experiment. It's how I learned this stuff. And it's how you'll learn it too."*

## Summary

- A Money Model is a deliberate sequence of offers
- 3 Stages: Get Cash → More Cash → Most Cash
- Start with Attraction Offer → add Upsell → add Downsell → add Continuity
- Don't implement full Money Model at once — break your business
- Less about 100 products, more about 100 ways to offer the same product
- Affiliate relationships fill gaps with no operational overhead
- Price new offers low → raise until you stop making more money
- A $100M Money Model eliminates cash as a growth bottleneck
