# Section: Continuity Offers — Keep Them Buying For Good
tags: [money-models, Hormozi, continuity, recurring]

## Summary / Definition

The fourth and final offer type.

> *"You can shear a sheep for a lifetime, but you can only skin it once."*
>
> — John, an early mentor (chapter epigraph)

> *"Continuity Offers provide ongoing value that customers make ongoing payments for — until they cancel. They boost the profit from every customer and give you one last thing to sell. Continuity Offers are awesome because you sell once, but get paid again and again."*

— Alex Hormozi, *$100M Money Models*

---

## The Continuity Math

Worked example from the book:

| Approach | Now | Over Time | Total Per Customer |
|---|---|---|---|
| $1,000 one-time (10 of 100 buy) | $10,000 | $0 | $10,000 |
| $50/mo continuity (40 of 100 buy, 20 months avg) | $2,000 | $40,000 | $42,000 from 40 customers |

> *"You go from making $10,000 now and $0 over time to $2,000 now and $40,000 over time."*

Plus: 40 customers to upsell later vs. 10.

But:
> *"This illustrates the pros and cons of continuity. You can attract more customers compared to something more expensive, but you make way less money now. That makes it tough to use as an Attraction Offer on its own."*

---

## Hormozi's Trade-Off Solution

> *"By making continuity offers last, we get the best of all worlds. We get cash today from Attraction Offers, Upsell Offers, and Downsell Offers. We get a little cash today and tons of cash tomorrow from Continuity Offers."*

This is **Stage III** in the Money Model build sequence. See [[make-your-money-model]].

---

## When Continuity Makes Sense

> *"Only some stuff makes sense for a Continuity Offer. It's silly for someone to pay for a one-day workshop . . . forever. It makes sense for them to pay until they cover the cost — and that makes it a payment plan."*

> *"At the same time, you probably make a mistake to offer a single price (even a big price) to provide a service forever. If your customers get ongoing value, it probably makes sense for them to make ongoing payments."*

The test: does the customer get ongoing value? If yes, continuity. If one-time, payment plan.

---

## Hormozi Is A Continuity Guy

> *"I've been a continuity guy my entire life: personal fitness, then gyms, then gym licensing, then supplements, then software, and now with Acquisition.com . . . lots of stuff. Needless to say, I'm a fan."*

> *"Main reason: when you do continuity right, you get more customers and make more money from them."*

## The 3 Plays

| Play | Mechanism | Page |
|---|---|---|
| **Continuity Bonus** | Add bonuses for signing up to continuity | [[continuity-bonus-offer]] |
| **Continuity Discount** | Discount the continuity if they commit | [[continuity-discount-offer]] |
| **Waived Fee Offer** | Big startup fee, waived if they commit | [[waived-fee-offer]] |

All three depend on getting customers to start AND keep buying.

> *"All offers depend on getting customers to buy. But, Continuity Offers depend on getting customers to keep buying. I get them to do both by combining bonuses, discounts, and fees."*

---

## The Author Note (Critical)

> *"No successful continuity businesses I've seen has a standalone membership offer. They all have other bells and whistles to upsell."*

> *"Main reason: Continuity Offers are tough to advertise profitably. Nobody wants to make a recurring commitment to something they haven't tried."*

The fix:
- Attract customers with trials
- Once they join, upsell other features and longer-term prepay
- This generates cash for ads while building recurring revenue

---

## Section Conclusion

> *"Continuity Offers provide ongoing value that customers make ongoing payments for until they cancel. Many businesses use Continuity Offers to attract customers for less. But, it crashes 30-day profits. This makes profitable advertising difficult."*

> *"I use Continuity Offers differently. I make them last. I start with profitable Attraction Offers. Then make my Upsell and Downsell Offers. Then, I offer Continuity. And if they accept, I upsell a bulk amount of time or product at a discount. Then, they automatically enter continuity after they've used up their bulk purchase."*

> *"This way, I make even more cash and I get the recurring-cash benefits of the other continuity customers."*

---

## Reward vs Punishment

> *"Continuity Offers work with rewards or punishment. I prefer rewards. And two of the three Continuity Offers I explained use them. But, there will always be times when a more traditional contract makes sense. In those situations, I like Waived Fee Offers."*

| Play | Mechanism | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Continuity Bonus | Reward | Positive |
| Continuity Discount | Reward | Positive |
| Waived Fee | Punishment | Negative |

## Application For Caret/Colandrum

For an annual SaaS subscription:

1. **Continuity Bonus**: Sign up annually → get the agency-only playbook library + monthly office hours + private Slack channel access (all bonuses worth $5K)
2. **Continuity Discount**: 20% off if you commit to annual prepay
3. **Waived Fee**: $5K onboarding fee, waived if you sign 12-month commitment

Combine all three for maximum conversion.
