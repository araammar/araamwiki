# Strategy: AI SEO Strategy (Brand Radar Playbook)

## Summary
Product and brand discovery is migrating from traditional Google search to AI-powered interfaces: ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google's Gemini AI summaries at the top of search results. The ranking logic is different. AI doesn't reward backlinks and keyword density the same way. It surfaces Reddit threads and YouTube videos heavily — in ChatGPT's own words, Reddit and YouTube build AI results, not traditional PR or backlinks. Brands that build presence in those two channels and write descriptive, information-dense product copy will gain AI visibility. Brands that relied on programmatic spend and SEO tricks will lose ground.

## Framework / Steps

### Step 1: Establish a Baseline with AI Share of Voice Tracking
Before optimizing, measure where you are. Use a tool like **Ahrefs Brand Radar** (purpose-built for this) to:
- Track your brand's AI share of voice across ChatGPT and Google AI
- See where your brand appears in Reddit discussions and YouTube content
- Benchmark against competitors to see who is gaining and losing AI attention
- Build a baseline so progress is measurable

Brand Radar is built on millions of real search queries — it shows what people actually ask and what AI answers back. It can research brands, products, competitors, geographic regions, and individual people.

### Step 2: Rewrite Product Copy for AI Agents
AI agents don't read clever naming or vibe-driven copy. They read descriptive, informational text and match it to query intent. Rewrite every:
- **Product page:** What is this, what does it do, who is it for, what makes it different
- **Category page:** What are the products in this category, how do they differ, what should a buyer know
- **Headers and titles:** Descriptive over clever. An AI looking for "best cashmere sweaters" needs to see "cashmere sweater" and quality markers in your text, not your brand's poetic tagline

The principle: **agents look for solutions to questions, not vibes.** Treat your product pages as answers to the questions your buyer would type into ChatGPT.

### Step 3: Build In-Depth Knowledge Bases
Create thorough informational content that explains your product category, your materials, your methods, and your quality standards. This serves three purposes:
1. AI surfaces it as authoritative content when users ask related questions
2. Consumers who research before buying find it and build trust
3. It seals the "why buy from you" question that the discovery query was trying to answer

Format: long-form knowledge base articles, FAQ pages, material education guides, production explainers. Text on product pages and category pages counts. The goal is thorough, accurate, descriptive information that explains what you sell and why someone would want it.

### Step 4: Enter Reddit Organically
Reddit is heavily weighted in AI search results — it is one of the primary sources AI LLMs draw from when building recommendations. Priority actions:
- Identify the subreddits where your category lives (r/malefashionadvice, r/coffee, r/homeimprovement — whatever applies)
- Engage genuinely as brand or as founders/team, not as fake accounts
- Seed authentic product discussions with partners and customers who can post honest experiences
- Commission or encourage customers to share detailed reviews in relevant threads

Critical constraint: do not pay for Reddit posts. Reddit is simultaneously the most gamed and most AI-weighted network. Being caught paying for posts destroys the trust you were trying to build, and the AI visibility benefit depends on authentic social proof — not planted content.

### Step 5: Enter YouTube Organically
YouTube is the other primary source AI draws from. The owned content goal:
- Create videos that exist at the intersection of your product category and genuine consumer questions
- "How cashmere is graded," "what to look for in a leather bag," "how to evaluate [your category]" — educational, searchable, useful
- Show your manufacturing context (the Show It Principle — see [[show-it-principle]])
- Interview suppliers, show materials sourcing, demonstrate quality differences

The seeded content goal: work with YouTubers who review products in your category authentically. Not paid-placement-without-disclosure, but genuine product reviews from creators with relevant audiences.

### Step 6: Monitor Progress and Iterate
- Run Brand Radar (or equivalent) monthly to track AI share of voice movement
- Identify which queries your brand shows up for and which your competitors dominate
- Double down on content that is already surfacing you in AI results
- Build content specifically targeting the queries where competitors have AI visibility and you don't

## When to Use
This is the primary SEO strategy for any brand operating in 2026 and beyond. Traditional Google SEO (backlinks, keyword stuffing, programmatic traffic) is becoming less relevant as:
- AI summaries (Google Gemini) absorb more of the organic traffic that used to go to ranked pages
- More product research queries are moving to ChatGPT and Perplexity directly
- Users trust AI-synthesized recommendations over a scrollable list of paid-SEO results

This is not a replacement for the full SEO toolkit — it's the layer on top that is now the highest-leverage investment.

## The AI Discovery Principle
AI agents are, at their core, answer machines. When a consumer asks "what's the best wool sweater brand under $300," the AI is matching that query to content that answers it. The brand that wins is the one whose content says, clearly and accurately, "here is a wool sweater under $300, here is what makes it worth buying, here is what other people have said about it on Reddit and YouTube." That is the algorithm now.
