# Strategy: Intentional Scroll — Platform Intelligence System

## Summary

Scrolling is competitive intelligence when done right. Orin John's 3-layer system turns passive consumption into an active research operation: engineered accounts that mirror target customer feeds, time-blocked sessions per niche, and a weekly processing ritual that extracts saved content into a queryable Notion database. The output is platform knowledge most creators don't have — what content formats are dominating, which creators your audience already trusts, what's getting shared.

---

## The 3-Layer System

### Layer 1: Account Architecture

**The problem with one account:** Your personal feed is a mix of everything you're interested in. It tells you what you like, not what your target audience sees.

**The fix:** Separate accounts per niche.

- 2 phones, multiple accounts per phone (or just multiple accounts on one device)
- Each account follows only creators and brands in one specific niche: beauty, fashion, personal brand, B2B outbound, etc.
- Never cross-contaminate: no personal follows, no algorithm drift
- One account per niche — the feed becomes a pure signal for that niche

**What you're building:** A feed that mirrors what your target customer's feed actually looks like. What influencers appear? What brand content breaks through? What formats dominate?

### Layer 2: Time-Blocked Sessions

**The problem with passive scrolling:** It's unstructured, runs long, bleeds into personal consumption time, and produces no useful output.

**The fix:** 30-minute blocks, scheduled, per niche account.

- 2-3 sessions per niche account per week
- One additional session per week reserved for personal consumption (different niche, inspiration, personal library)
- Timer on. When it ends, stop.

**Scroll speed variation (algorithm shaping):**
- Faster scrolling → trains algorithm toward shorter content
- Slower, more complete viewing → pulls longer content
- Use scroll speed deliberately based on what you need to see that session

### Layer 3: Weekly Processing Ritual (Sunday)

Everything saved during the week goes into the Notion creative toolkit database on Sunday.

Fields per entry:
- **Category:** campaign / launch / tool / organic / ads / strategy
- **Notes:** Why you saved it. What's interesting. What it's for.
- **Link:** Direct reference.
- **Tried / Not Tried:** Binary. Proven winners vs untested references.

The database compounds over time. Filtering by "launch content" surfaces a library of real working examples instantly.

## What This Produces

**Platform fluency:** You know what's actually working before it becomes common knowledge. You're watching trends at the niche level, not the surface level.

**A tested reference library:** When a client needs a launch strategy, you pull 40 references and tell them which 12 you've personally run. That's a different conversation than "here are some examples I found."

**Relevancy maintenance:** This is the operational counterpart to avoiding [[relevancy-decay-problem]]. You stay in the mix without needing to be in a full-time role.

## Setup Checklist

- [ ] Create at least one dedicated niche research account on Instagram
- [ ] Create at least one dedicated niche research account on TikTok
- [ ] Follow nothing personal on these accounts — niche only
- [ ] Block 30 min twice per week for each niche account
- [ ] Create Notion bookmark doc with four columns: Category, Notes, Link, Tried/Not Tried
- [ ] Set Sunday as processing day — every save from the week goes in
