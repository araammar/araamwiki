# Strategy: The Executive Schedule for Creators

## Summary

Orin John's time-blocking system for running a content operation like an executive. Every task gets a named 30-minute block. Nothing is left to "when I feel like it." The system creates reliable output without requiring motivation — the schedule is the decision-making layer, so you never waste time deciding what to work on.

---

## The Core Principle

Treat content creation as an executive function, not a creative mood. Executives don't decide each morning what to prioritize — they have blocked time for each responsibility. Apply the same structure to creator work.

30-minute increments are the unit. Not hours, not "half the day." A 30-minute block is long enough to complete a meaningful unit of work and short enough to stack without fatigue.

---

## The Block Architecture

### Production Blocks
- Script writing: 30 min per script
- Filming: batched into 2-3 hour blocks once per week
- Editing: 30-60 min per piece
- Thumbnail/cover design: 30 min per asset

### Research Blocks
- Platform scroll sessions: 30 min per niche account (see [[intentional-scroll-platform-intelligence]])
- Competitor analysis: 30 min per session
- Trend monitoring: 15-30 min daily

### Distribution Blocks
- Posting + first-30-min engagement window: 30 min per platform
- Comment replies: 30 min daily

### Client/Operations Blocks (for creator-consultants)
- Client calls: blocked, never scheduled ad hoc
- Strategy work: 60-90 min deep blocks, never split

---

## Protecting Research Blocks

Research blocks are the first thing creators cut when they're behind. This is the mistake. Research blocks are what maintain relevancy — without them, content drifts toward past-tense expertise (see [[relevancy-decay-problem]]).

Rule: Research blocks are non-negotiable. Production blocks can be compressed. Research blocks cannot.

---

## Filling Freed Slots with Leverage Work

When a scheduled block finishes early or a call cancels: the freed slot goes to leverage work (the thing with highest long-term return), not to catching up on email or admin.

Leverage work hierarchy:
1. Creating an asset that earns repeatedly (evergreen content, templates, systems docs)
2. Building a relationship that unlocks future opportunities
3. Learning something that makes every future block more effective

---

## The Weekly Template

| Time Slot | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AM Deep | Script x2 | Script x2 | Film batch | Edit x3 | Strategy |
| Mid AM | Research (niche 1) | Research (niche 2) | Film batch | Edit x3 | Research |
| Midday | Post + engage | Post + engage | Post + engage | Post + engage | Post + engage |
| PM | Client/ops | Client/ops | Client/ops | Client/ops | Review + plan |

Saturday: Personal scroll session (non-niche, inspiration)
Sunday: Bookmark processing (see [[creative-toolkit-bookmark-system]])

---

## What to Steal

Block your next week before Sunday ends. Every recurring content task gets a named slot. Put research blocks on the calendar like client calls — immovable. The first week will feel constraining. By week three it will feel like freedom.
