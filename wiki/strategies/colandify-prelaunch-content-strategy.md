# Strategy: Colandify Pre-Launch Content Strategy

## Summary

Build a following before selling. The media-company model applied to a B2B SaaS pre-launch: create content that serves the ICP's exact problems (deliverability, lead data quality, tool overwhelm) without mentioning the product until the community is warm. The reveal becomes news, not a pitch.

## ICP

B2B outbound freelancers and agencies. They run cold email infrastructure. They care about deliverability, lead data quality, and not paying for 14 tools that half-overlap. They are skeptical of software that promises the world and delivers another dashboard.

---

## 3-Phase Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–2)

**Goal:** Establish credibility in the niche before any product mention.

- Post daily on Instagram about outbound pain points — deliverability failures, bad data, inbox hell
- Post 3x/week on LinkedIn about B2B prospecting strategy
- Do not mention Colandify. Do not hint at a product.
- Engage in every comment. Reply to every DM.
- Identify the 20-30 most active voices in the outbound niche and engage their posts daily.

**Content types:** Pain amplification reels, "how I fixed X" threads, contrarian takes on common outbound advice.

### Phase 2 — Traction (Weeks 3–4)

**Goal:** Build a waitlist-ready audience through education and authority.

- Introduce the problem framing: "Why every cold email tool solves the wrong problem"
- Launch a Reddit presence in r/sales, r/entrepreneur, r/freelance — provide value, no product mention
- Post "behind the scenes" of building — the problem you're solving, the research, the failures
- ManyChat trigger: post "Comment COLANDIFY for early access" — do not run this until engagement is consistent

**Content types:** Tutorial receipts (screen recordings), contrarian positioning posts, build-in-public threads.

### Phase 3 — Reveal (Weeks 5–6)

**Goal:** Turn warm audience into waitlist and beta users.

- Announce the product. Frame it as: "You've been asking about X. This is what I built."
- Post the origin story reel — the specific pain point that created the idea
- Show the product solving an exact problem from Phase 1 content
- Push to waitlist. Make the ask once, clearly.

---

## Channel Strategy

| Platform | Frequency | Content Type | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 4-5x/week | Reels + carousels | Reach + brand |
| LinkedIn | 3x/week | Text posts + threads | ICP authority |
| Reddit | 3-5x/week | Value posts + comments | Trust in niche communities |

---

## Weekly KPIs

- Instagram: 3%+ save rate, 1%+ share rate
- LinkedIn: Meaningful comment rate (not emoji reactions)
- Waitlist: 500+ before launch announcement

---

## What Kills This Strategy

- Mentioning the product before Phase 3
- Explicit engagement asks (penalized by LinkedIn March 2026 Authenticity Update)
- Generic B2B content that could apply to any tool
- Posting without engaging in comments
