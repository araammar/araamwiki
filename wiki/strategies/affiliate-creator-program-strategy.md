# Strategy: Affiliate Creator Program — Build, Scale, and Feed Paid

## Summary

Orin John's operational playbook for building a creator affiliate program from zero to scale. The program is not "influencer marketing." It is a content production system where creators make content for brands, brands purchase rights to what performs, and the best organic content feeds directly into paid ad accounts. The affiliate program is the creative supply chain.

---

## The Business Model

### Affiliate vs. Retainer

| Structure | How It Works | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Affiliate (commission)** | Creator earns % of sales generated. No guaranteed monthly cost. | Scaling broadly; testing new creators with lower risk |
| **Retainer** | Creator paid $1,500–$2,500/month guaranteed. | Reliable, consistent output from proven creators |
| **Hybrid** | Retainer base + commission on sales over threshold | Top-performing creators who warrant both |

Affiliate structures reduce upfront costs but require cash reserves for monthly retainer payments to proven performers. At scale, the best creators get pulled into retainer arrangements.

### TikTok Shop Layer
TikTok Shop affiliate model: creators make content, earn commissions on actual sales. Top earners (20–25 years old) can generate $10,000–$25,000/month in commissions. For brands: affiliate model removes the CPM mindset and aligns creator incentives directly with sales.

## How to Build the Program

### Phase 1: Finding First Creators (0 → 10)

Source from the customer base first. People already using the product make the most credible first content.

**Methods:**
- Instagram Story DMs to engaged followers
- Email outreach to existing customers
- Tool: **Outer Signal** — identifies customers in your email list who have social followings; generates data-backed personas

**Criteria:** Genuine product users, even if small following. A customer with 2,000 engaged followers outperforms a 50K account with no product affinity.

**Brief philosophy:** Brief loosely. Give message and outcome guidance; let creators execute in their native style. Over-scripted creator content reads as brand content — defeats the purpose.

---

### Phase 2: Building Infrastructure (10 → 100)

**Community platforms:**
- Discord or WhatsApp communities with active creator groups
- Notify affiliates in real time about top-performing content and ad revenue potential
- Build a culture of information sharing — creators learning from each other scales the program faster than brand briefing alone

**Management tools:**
- **Social Snowball** — affiliate program management
- **SuperAffiliate** — scaling affiliate operations
- **Youka** — TikTok Shop creator sourcing
- **Creator Ads Manager** — managing creator content at scale

**Diversity requirement:** Across gender, age, body type, content style (ASMR vs. yap vs. get-ready-with-me), demographic segment. A program of 100 identical creators is not a program — it is a single creator scaled inefficiently.

---

### Phase 3: The Organic-to-Paid Pipeline (Scale)

The strategic advantage of the affiliate program is not just organic reach — it is the creative supply chain for paid.

**The flow:**
1. Affiliates post content organically
2. Monitor performance across all creator content
3. Identify outliers (top 5–10% of performers)
4. Purchase ad rights: $100–400 per video
5. Feed purchased content into paid ad account continuously
6. Do not batch-add — add assets as they arrive, keep ad account refreshed

**Why it works:** The organic performance is pre-validation. You're not betting on creative you think will work — you're amplifying content you know works.

**Tools for monitoring:** Video Database (Cut30 internal), Sandcastles (competitor monitoring), Perplexity AI (comment scraping from TikTok/Reddit for feedback signals).

## Team Structure for Running the Program

**One-person team:**
- Specialize initially in one of: briefs, creator sourcing, or video editing
- Hire contractors for the other functions (Contra, Dribbble for graphic designers)
- Upcharge client rate to cover contractor costs
- Build toward lean team model

**Lean team (standard):**
- 2 video editors
- Graphic designer
- Creator sourcing specialist
- Media buyer (separate from creator side)

**Monthly delivery cadence:**
1. Deliver monthly toolkit (12–13 creative briefs)
2. Creator/UGC manager chases deliverables from all creators
3. Assets added to ad account continuously as they arrive
4. Refresh cycle: repeat monthly

---

## Common Mistakes

- Starting with too few creators and treating it as an experiment rather than infrastructure
- Over-scripting briefs — destroys the organic credibility that makes creator content work
- Batch-adding to ad accounts instead of continuous refresh — ad fatigue accelerates
- Evaluating creator content by brand team's personal taste rather than performance data
- Staying at 2–3 creators indefinitely instead of building toward 100+
