# Caret Model — Product Overview

## What It Is

An evidence-first lead generation platform for marketing agencies and freelancers.

Instead of a contact list like Apollo or ZoomInfo, Caret finds local service businesses already spending on Google and Meta ads, audits their websites for conversion failures, and delivers a lead with a documented problem and a ready-made outreach hook.

**One-liner:** Stop pitching strangers. Start diagnosing problems.

---

## How It Works

A 6-layer pipeline that finds local service businesses already spending on Google and Meta ads, then audits their websites to find the gap between their ad spend and their conversion rate. Every lead comes with attached evidence.

### Example Output

> **OASIS DAY SPA — NEW YORK, NY**
> Running both Google + Meta ads. Mobile site scores 12/100 on PageSpeed. LCP: 8.4 seconds. No booking form on landing page. Score: 100/100.
> Hook: "You're paying for traffic that bounces before it books."

---

## Who It's For

**Primary buyer:** Owner-operated marketing agencies, $750K–$3M revenue, 6–20 FTE, vertical specialists, retainer-led pricing ($3K–$15K/mo per client), founder is still the primary salesperson.

**Secondary buyer:** Freelance marketers and solo operators actively prospecting for clients.

## Pricing

Subscription + credits model. Month-to-month. No annual contracts. Cancel anytime.

| Tier | Price | Credits | For |
|------|-------|---------|-----|
| Free | $0 | 50 credits/mo | Try it |
| Starter | $79/mo | 1,500 credits/mo | Solo freelancers |
| Pro | $249/mo | 6,000 credits/mo | Small agencies (Most Popular) |
| Agency | $599/mo | 18,000 credits/mo | 10+ FTE agencies |

### How Credits Work

- Discovery + audits = 1 credit
- Contact reveals = 5 credits (Hunter.io pass-through)
- AI sales briefs = 8 credits (GPT-4o generation)
- Rollover: up to 1x monthly cap, 60-day expiry
