# Caret Model — 30-Day Product Launch Plan

## North Star

**500 waitlist signups before hard launch. 50 paying customers by day 30.**

Soft launch days 1–14. Hard launch day 15. Scale what works days 15–30.

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Total budget | $1,500 |
| Channels | 5 (Reddit, X, LinkedIn, Meta, cold outreach) |
| Hard launch | Day 15 |
| Content pieces built before launch | 12 |

---

## Why Med Spas First

Med spas are the right launch vertical for three reasons:

1. **The math is undeniable.** They spend $1,500–$5,000/mo on Google and Meta ads. Their mobile sites are notoriously bad. The gap between spend and conversion is massive and visible.

2. **461 businesses already processed, 183 scored leads ready.** This is launch content, demo material, ad creative, and the Reddit post all in one.

3. **The pain is visual.** A 7-second page load with no booking form on a business spending $3,000/mo on Meta is something any agency owner immediately understands.

HVAC announced on day 28 to signal momentum and platform breadth.

---

## Phase 1 — Days 1 to 3: Pre-Launch, Build the Foundation

**Goal: All assets live before first outreach.**

**Set up brand accounts — all platforms, same day**
X/Twitter @CaretModel, Instagram @caretmodel, LinkedIn company page, TikTok @caretmodel. Same profile photo and bio everywhere: "We find local businesses wasting ad spend on broken funnels. Then we tell you." Link to waitlist on every profile.

**Landing page goes live with waitlist and sample lead card**
Hero: "Stop pitching strangers. Start delivering diagnoses." One CTA: Join the waitlist. Below the fold: one full sample lead card showing a real med spa audit. No pricing. No feature list. Let the output do the work.

**Write all 12 content pieces — ready to schedule**
Build the entire content library before launch week. 6 X threads, 3 LinkedIn posts, 2 Reddit posts, 1 TikTok script. All grounded in real audit data from the 183 med spas already processed.

**Budget: $0**

---

## Phase 2 — Days 4 to 14: Soft Launch, Build the Waitlist

**Goal: 500 waitlist signups.**

**Reddit "roast my idea" post — Day 4**
r/agency and r/sweatystartup. Title: "Built a tool that audits local businesses wasting ad spend on broken funnels — roast it." Paste the full sample lead card. Ask what's wrong with it. Do not pitch. Drop waitlist link only if asked. This single post can drive 100+ signups if the output is strong.

**X thread "We audited 183 med spas, here's what we found" — Day 5**
Data-first. Real numbers: average PageSpeed score, percentage with no booking form, percentage with LCP over 4 seconds, estimated ad spend being wasted. Last tweet: "If you sell to med spas or local service businesses, this is what your next 50 leads look like. Link in bio." Pin this tweet.

**LinkedIn "The problem with every lead gen tool" — Day 6**
"Apollo gives you 210 million contacts. We give you 50 businesses with a documented problem, an active ad budget, and the first line of your email already written." No link in post. Drop waitlist link in first comment.

**100 cold LinkedIn DMs to agency owners — Days 4 through 10**
10 DMs per day. Message: "Hey [name] — built a tool that finds local businesses already running ads on broken funnels, audits the problem, and writes the outreach hook for you. Every lead ships with a screenshot of what's broken. Want me to send you a real example?" No link. No pitch. Just the offer to show.

**TikTok / Reels screen recording — Day 7**
"I audited a med spa funnel and found this." 45 seconds. Show the Meta ad running, then show the mobile landing page with the 7-second load and no booking form. Last line: "We find these every day. Link in bio if you sell to businesses like this."

**First Meta ad — Days 8 through 14**
Visual: med spa Meta ad vs. same business mobile page with 7-second load timer and missing booking form circled. Copy: "This med spa is paying $40 a click to send people to a broken page. We found 1,847 more like them." CTA: Join the waitlist. $50/day. Kill if CPL exceeds $15.

**Product Hunt upcoming submission — Day 8**
Submit as an upcoming product launching Day 15. Start collecting followers who get notified on launch day. Can add 200–300 waitlist signups before the product is even live.

**Budget: $350 (Meta ads)**

---

## Phase 3 — Days 15 to 21: Hard Launch, Product Goes Live

**Goal: 25 paying customers.**

**Product Hunt launch day — Day 15**
Post goes live at 12:01am PST. Email the entire waitlist: "Caret is live. You're first." Post across all socials simultaneously. DM every person who engaged with any content in the prior 10 days. Ask everyone you know to upvote. Goal: top 5 of the day. Top 5 on PH = 500–1,000 additional organic signups.

**Founder pricing — 48 hours only — Days 15 and 16**
First 50 signups get 40% off forever. Call it the Founding Agency Rate. Creates urgency, rewards the waitlist, generates social proof screenshots for week 3.

**X launch thread — Day 15**
"Every lead gen tool gives you a list. We built a tool that gives you a diagnosis. Here's why that difference matters and what we found when we audited 183 med spas to prove it." Real data, real output, real reason.

**Scale Meta ad if CPL was under $15**
Increase to $100/day. Change CTA from "Join the waitlist" to "Start for free." If CPL was over $15, pause and test Leaky Funnel creative instead: carousel of 3 real audits with the worst problem circled on each slide.

**Budget: $700 (Meta ads)**

---

## Phase 4 — Days 22 to 30: Scale, Convert and Retain

**Goal: 50 paying customers.**

**Publish "State of Local Ad Waste" data report — Day 22**
One-page report: average PageSpeed scores by vertical, percentage of active advertisers with no booking form, estimated monthly ad spend being wasted across all 183 audited businesses. SEO anchor piece, PR hook, and the content that gets shared without any promotion.

**10-minute call with every paying customer — Days 22 through 28**
Every single one. Two questions: "What made you sign up?" and "What would make you tell another agency owner about this?" Answers become testimonials, next ad copy, and the product roadmap.

**Reddit transparent launch recap — Day 25**
"We launched, here's what happened." Real numbers: waitlist signups, paying customers, DM reply rate, Meta CPL. What worked, what didn't. r/entrepreneur and r/sweatystartup. Drop link in first comment.

**HVAC vertical announcement — Day 28**
Post across all channels: "Med spa was just the start. HVAC is live." One X thread, one LinkedIn post, one email to the full list.

**Budget: $450 (remaining Meta)**

## End of Month Decision Gate

| Result | Action |
|--------|--------|
| 50+ paying customers | Double Meta budget. Add Google Search ads on "lead gen for agencies." Hire one part-time content creator. |
| 25–49 paying customers | Hold ad spend. Run another 100 cold DMs. Double down on the content that drove the most signups. |
| Under 25 paying customers | Stop all paid spend. Get on calls with every customer you have. The positioning or the output needs fixing before more distribution. |

---

## The Most Important Thing

The most important asset built this month is the sample lead card — not the funnel, not the ads, not the content. Every tactic points to it. If the output makes an agency owner stop and say "I need that," the rest of the plan works. If it doesn't, nothing else matters.
