# Reference: Particle

## Overview
Particle is a competitive intelligence tool for e-commerce and luxury brands. It provides real-time and historical sales data by brand, broken down by product type, price tier, color distribution, and marketing activity. Used to sanity-check what brands actually sell (versus what they market), identify entry-level price points driving volume, and understand campaign patterns.

## What Particle Shows
- **Top-selling products by brand** — ranked by volume over selectable time windows (last 30 days, last 90 days, etc.)
- **Product detail breakdowns** — individual SKU-level data including units sold online
- **Color distribution** — which colorways are actually moving (e.g., Brunello Cucinelli's top colors are brown and blue, not black)
- **Price distribution** — "percent of product" at each price bracket vs. "percent of revenue" — reveals which tier is sneakily driving the business
- **Marketing assets** — homepage changes, email frequency (e.g., Brunello sends two emails per month), campaign breakdowns
- **Collaboration item performance** — can search by collaborator name to isolate how collab collections actually perform in units sold

## Key Use Cases

**1. Sanity-checking luxury brand claims**
Moncler's Genius campaigns (A$AP Rocky, Donald Glover, Rick Owens) generate massive cultural conversation. Particle shows the collaboration items moved 10-12 units. The actual business is winter down jackets. The campaigns are marketing for the core product, not commercial vehicles themselves.

**2. Identifying real entry-level volume drivers**
Brunello Cucinelli top seller by unit: the $1,100 entry-level sneaker — not the $3,000+ suits the brand is known for. Price distribution shows most buyers are in the sub-$368 range by count, but the $1,000+ sneaker tier is generating disproportionate revenue. Without this data, you'd assume the wrong product is driving the business.

**3. Competitive product research in any niche**
"Always worth it to sanity-check what people in these niches are doing and what they actually sell versus what they market." Use Particle before building or repositioning a brand in any category where established players exist.

**4. Email and marketing cadence intelligence**
Understand how frequently competitors communicate with their audience and through which assets. Useful for setting your own cadence benchmarks.
