# Reference: Reel Script Tool

## Overview

A tool that generates reel scripts in a defined voice, using a taxonomy of hook types, body structures, and CTAs. Voice DNA is extracted from 12 real scripts. Output is a ready-to-film script with hook, body, and CTA separated.

---

## Voice DNA (from 12 Scripts)

**Core signatures:**

- **Contrast pairs:** "This does X. That does Y. You want Y." Two lines. No connective.
- **Triple negative:** "Not X. Not Y. Not even Z. [Reveal]."
- **Gambling metaphors:** Stakes framed as bets, odds, or house edges
- **Blame reframe:** Shifts fault from the person to the system, environment, or bad information they were given

**Sentence rhythm:** Short. Punchy. No subordinate clauses in hook or close. Subordinate clauses allowed in body explanation only.

**Register:** Direct, slightly confrontational, never aggressive. The tone of a colleague who's figured something out and wants you to know.

---

## 10 Hook Types

1. **Pain diagnosis** — names the exact frustration without any warmup
2. **False belief reframe** — "You're not afraid of X. You're afraid of Y."
3. **Counterintuitive claim** — states the opposite of what the audience expects
4. **Specific data point** — one exact number that forces attention
5. **Comparison reveal** — "I tested X and Y on the same [thing]. Here's what happened."
6. **Identity declaration** — assigns a trait to the audience that makes them want to prove it true
7. **Stakes escalation** — opens small, elevates to life-defining consequence
8. **Permission declaration** — "I'll say it so you don't have to."
9. **Process reveal** — "Here's exactly how I [specific action]"
10. **Named framework** — introduces a proper noun for an idea; label first, explanation second

---

## 4 Energy Modes

| Mode | When to Use | Sentence Style |
|---|---|---|
| **Authority** | Teaching, positioning | Short declaratives. Periods only. |
| **Peer** | Sharing discovery, behind-the-scenes | Casual, first-person, "I found that..." |
| **Challenger** | Contrarian takes | Slight confrontation, rhetorical questions |
| **Storyteller** | Journey arcs, case studies | Two hyper-specific data points + time gap |

---

## Tutorial Formats

For how-to and walkthrough reels:

- **Step list:** Numbered. Each step one sentence. No explanation until after the list.
- **Before/after:** Start with the broken state. Show the fix. Show the result.
- **Decision tree:** "If X, do Y. If not X, do Z." Two branches max per step.

---

## CTA Taxonomy

| Type | Example | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Follow for more** | "Follow for weekly outbound tactics" | Education content |
| **Save this** | "Save this — you'll use it" | Tactical/recipe content |
| **Comment trigger** | "Comment [word] for the full breakdown" | ManyChat integration |
| **Question close** | "Which one are you making?" | Engagement driver |
| **No CTA** | — | Authority mode, named framework posts |

Never ask for likes. Never say "drop a comment below."

---

## Personal Voice Moves

Specific constructions to use sparingly for distinctiveness:

- "The math doesn't care about your feelings."
- "This is what [X] actually looks like."
- Parenthetical asides in em dashes — the whisper inside the sentence
- Ending on a single word sentence. One. Word. Full stop.

---

## Script Output Format

```
HOOK: [1-3 sentences, ~210 characters max]

BODY:
[Structured content — steps, contrast pairs, or story arc]

CTA: [One line, optional]
```
