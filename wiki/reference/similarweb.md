# Reference: SimilarWeb

## Overview
SimilarWeb is a competitive intelligence platform that provides data on competitor website traffic, sources, keywords, ad creative, and AI visibility. In 2026, it has added an AI brand visibility module that tracks how often a brand is mentioned by LLMs (ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Perplexity) — making it one of the first tools to quantify AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) performance.

**Primary use cases:**
- Competitive website traffic audits
- AI brand visibility / LLM mention share tracking
- Keyword gap analysis (branded and non-branded)
- Referral source monitoring (who links to competitors but not to you)
- Display ad and PPC creative research
- Landing page strategy reverse-engineering

## Key Modules

### AI Brand Visibility
Tracks how often a brand is recommended by AI chatbots in relevant topic categories.
- Outputs: brand mention share, key topics driving mentions, LLM comparison (which AI mentions you most), prompt examples showing competitor rankings, citation source data, sentiment analysis
- Use case: See whether your brand or competitors have an "AI share-of-voice" strategy working. This is a significant referral traffic source for many brands starting in 2026.

### Referral Traffic Analysis
- Input: a competitor's domain
- Output: which sites are driving referral traffic to them (and not to you)
- Use case: Identify partnership, guest post, and backlink gaps

### Search Traffic & Keywords
- Shows both branded and non-branded keyword traffic
- Reveals which landing pages are receiving organic and paid traffic
- Use case: Reverse-engineer competitor content strategy; find keyword gaps; see what's being A/B tested on their paid landing pages

### Ad & Campaign Performance
- Display ads currently running
- Google ads and YouTube ads
- PPC spend estimates
- Creative assets: the actual ad creative they're running
- Use case: Competitive creative intelligence; understand what messaging and visual approaches competitors are investing in

## How to Run a Competitive Web Audit (Checklist)
1. Enter competitor domain
2. Check AI Brand Visibility: do they have LLM mention share? What topics drive it?
3. Check referral traffic: what sites send them traffic? Who's sending traffic to them but not to you?
4. Check search: branded vs. non-branded split; top non-branded keywords; landing pages for organic and paid
5. Check ads: what creative are they running? What PPC messaging are they testing? What YouTube ads are active?
6. Identify gaps: where are they getting traffic/visibility that you're not?
7. Build action plan: which gap is easiest to close first? What does that require from the team?
