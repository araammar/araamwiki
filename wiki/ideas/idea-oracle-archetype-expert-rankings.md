# Idea: Oracle Archetype — Expert Rankings Series

## The Idea
Launch a recurring "expert rankings" short-form video series built on the Oracle archetype. A credible person from inside the business (founder, specialist, lead practitioner) rates, compares, or ranks items within the brand's product category on camera — answering the exact question every customer asks before spending money: "Is X or Y actually worth it?"

## Format
- **Length:** 45–90 seconds for TikTok/Instagram Reels
- **Structure:** Hook (state the comparison or ranking) → Brief credential signal (visual costume, environment, or one-line authority marker) → Ratings with reasoning → Clear verdict
- **Recurring set:** Film in the natural expert environment — dentist chair, kitchen, garage, design desk, workshop floor. The environment does credibility work before a word is spoken.
- **Carousel version:** Same content without on-camera presence — each slide is one rating, final slide is the verdict.

## Example Applications by Industry

| Industry | Expert | Rating Format |
|----------|--------|---------------|
| Skincare | Brand founder or formulator | Rate competitor serums vs. yours on one criterion |
| Fitness | Head coach or in-house trainer | Rate common gym supplements: do they work? |
| Food/Beverage | Head chef or R&D lead | Rate 5 versions of the ingredient category you use |
| Legal / Finance | Founding attorney or analyst | Rate common mistakes clients make — 1–10 severity |
| Manufacturing | Production specialist | Rate supplier materials on durability and value |
| Fragrance | Founder or perfumer | Rate common fragrance families for specific moods |

## Why It Works
- Viewers come to social with the "is X worth it?" question already in their head — expert rankings answer it in the exact format they're already consuming
- The ranking/rating format consistently outperforms other expert content templates for hook rate because the premise creates immediate information tension: "I want to know the verdict"
- Wearing the costume (being in the real expert environment) creates an authority signal that overrides follower count — a dentist in a dentist chair with 200 followers is more credible than a generic talking head with 50K
- Comments from viewers asking about adjacent products generate the next five video topics automatically

## Execution Checklist
- [ ] Identify who in the organization can be the credible on-camera expert (founder, specialist, or hireable spokesperson)
- [ ] Define 10 ranking comparisons customers already have opinions about in your category
- [ ] Set up the recurring filming location (the costume + environment)
- [ ] Batch-film 4–6 episodes before posting any
- [ ] Hook each video with the comparison stated directly: "Is [X] or [Y] actually worth [Z]?"
- [ ] Add no CTAs for the first 30 days — build traction first

## Connection to Full Funnel
Once traction exists (2K+ views consistently for narrow niches, 5K+ for broad categories), shift comments to DM capture: "Comment 'rankings' and I'll send you the full comparison." This moves warm leads off social into direct conversation without a landing page required.
