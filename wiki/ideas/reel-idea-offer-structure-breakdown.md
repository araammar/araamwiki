# Idea: "Here's the Exact Offer I Made That Got Me [Result]"

## The Hook
"This is the exact offer I built that replaced my salary in 6 months"
"Most freelancers sell services. I sell this instead — and charge 3× more"
"The offer formula that makes clients say yes without negotiating price"

## The Content Format
**Graphic yap with slide overlay** — you on camera walking through a written offer structure. Show the actual formula, the actual price, the actual deliverable list. 60–90 seconds.

Walk through:
1. The old way you sold your time (hourly or vague retainer)
2. The formula: "I do [specific transformation] for [specific client type] in [specific timeframe]"
3. Your current stacked offer (core deliverable → add-ons → premium tier)
4. The price at each level
5. What changed when you stopped selling time and started selling outcomes

## Why This Works
- Specific money numbers in hooks perform exceptionally well for creator/consultant audiences
- The offer structure is actionable — people can adapt it immediately
- The "I was doing it wrong" narrative arc is inherently watchable
- Positions you as someone who has solved a problem others still have

## Who Should Make This
- Freelancers in any creative discipline (design, video, copy, strategy)
- Consultants who've made the shift from hourly billing to packaged offers
- Coaches or educators helping other creatives understand monetization
- Agency founders explaining their service model

## The Formula to Show On-Screen
```
Core Offer: I do [X] for [Y] at [Z]
- X = specific deliverable or transformation
- Y = specific client type (don't say "anyone")
- Z = specific price (not "starting at" — a number)

Add-ons (offer stacking):
- [Add-on 1]: [price]
- [Add-on 2]: [price]

Premium option (done-with-you or accelerated):
- [Full service version]: [higher price]
```

## Variation Ideas
- "The price I used to charge vs. what I charge now — and what changed"
- "Three freelancers pitched me this week — here's why I hired the one who charged the most"
- "The thing stopping most creatives from charging what they're worth" (strong take format)
- "I removed 'hourly rate' from my vocab — here's what I say instead"
- Case study format: "Here's a real proposal I sent that closed" — walk through the document

## Production Requirements
- Slide or document showing the actual offer structure (you don't have to use real client names)
- Specific numbers — vague amounts kill credibility on this type of content
- Genuine before/after: what did you charge before, what do you charge now, what changed

## Pitfall to Avoid
Don't make this aspirational or theoretical. "Here's how you could structure an offer someday" is weak. The hook is "here's the exact thing I did." Show the actual formula, the actual price, the actual result. Generic advice about pricing is everywhere; specificity is rare and therefore more valuable.
