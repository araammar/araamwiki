# Idea: "I Watched 300 [Category] Videos So You Don't Have To"

## The Hook
"I had Perplexity watch 300 [product/category] TikToks and here's what it found"
"Here's what 300 TikTok reviews of [brand/product] actually say about the competition"
"I used AI to analyze [niche] content on TikTok — here are the 5 biggest findings"

## The Content Format
**Graphic yap** — you on screen with research output slides or screenshots overlaid. Run 60–90 seconds.

Walk through:
1. The methodology (you ran AI research on X videos, here's what you asked)
2. The most-mentioned brands and their reputation
3. Common consumer complaints in the category
4. The content hook formats that are actually getting engagement
5. Your takeaway / what this means for [your niche audience]

## Why This Works
- Positions you as someone who does the research others don't
- "So you don't have to" framing is a classic value hook
- Gives intellectual credibility — you're not just making content, you're analyzing an industry
- The methodology reveal (using AI) is itself content in 2026 — audiences want to see workflows

## Who Should Make This
- Marketing strategists and consultants
- Brand managers or brand-side creatives
- Anyone in a competitive consumer product category
- Social media managers explaining what's working in a niche

## Variation Ideas
- "I had AI watch [brand]'s comment section — here's every complaint they get"
- "Here are the 5 most-used hooks in [category] content right now"
- "The brand that shows up in 300 [product] reviews more than anyone else (it's not who you think)"
- Monthly series: "[Month] Creator Intelligence Report for [Niche]" — watch volume, rank, hook format winners

## Production Requirements
Minimal. This is a research-output video. You need:
- The Perplexity agent mode research output (takes 20+ minutes to run)
- Screenshots or a slide with the top-line findings
- CapCut or Edits for graphic overlay
- 60–90 seconds on camera

## Pitfall to Avoid
Don't make this academic. Lead with the surprising finding, not the methodology. "The top brand in creatine TikTok reviews gets destroyed in comments for taste — and no one's capitalizing on it" is a better hook than "Here's my research process."
