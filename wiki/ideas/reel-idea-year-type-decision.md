# Idea: "Which Type of Year Are You Having?" (Five-Type Framework)

## The Hook
"There are only 5 types of year you can have — here's how to pick the right one"
"The reason you're not making progress: you're trying to have 3 different years at once"
"I wasted years because I didn't know which kind of year to have"

## The Content Format
**Graphic yap / four-or-five-things format** — structured list delivery with each year type as a named point. 60–90 seconds or extended to 3 minutes if going deep.

Short version: name the five types, give one defining characteristic of each, end with the decision question.

Long version: for each type, explain who it's for, one tactic that defines it, and the failure mode if you try to do it simultaneously with others.

## Why This Works
- Named frameworks are inherently shareable — people tag others who are "in a money year"
- "Which one are you" content drives comment engagement
- Directly addresses a pain point: feeling stuck or spread thin without a clear reason why
- Reframes the problem (lack of focus) in a way that gives people agency

## Who Should Make This
- Creators and coaches targeting the "career change / inflection point" audience
- Anyone whose content covers productivity, career, personal development, or creative work
- Entrepreneurs and freelancers who serve people at transition moments

## The Five Types to Present

| Year Type | One-Line Summary | Defining Tactic |
|-----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Money Year** | Fix your relationship with money before making more | Consumables audit + subscription purge + invest savings passively |
| **Systems Year** | Build the infrastructure of your life | Calendar + task tracking + weekly updates, 52 consecutive weeks |
| **Rebrand Year** | Become the person you want to be seen as | Archetype exercise + the pause technique |
| **Mastery Year** | Build one skill to professional level | Make something every day, no exceptions, for 12 months |
| **Obsession Year** | Go all-in on one goal — with everything it costs | Say yes to everything aligned, say no to everything else |

## The Decision Framework to Show On-Screen
- Financial stress with decent income → **Money Year**
- Chaos, ideas that never execute, missed deadlines → **Systems Year**
- Want to be seen differently → **Rebrand Year**
- Want a new major skill → **Mastery Year**
- Already have momentum and a clear vision → **Obsession Year**

"Most common mistake: trying to do all five at once."

## Variation Ideas
- "I had a money year in [year] — here's what I gave up and what I got"
- "The obsession year I never told anyone about"
- "My systems year playlist" — what to listen to, read, track during a systems-focused year
- Poll content: "Which year are you having?" (comment-based or story poll)
- Case study: "Here's someone who had a mastery year and what it looked like 12 months later"

## Production Requirements
- Slide or graphic listing the five types for overlay
- Clear delivery of the decision framework at the end — this is the actionable part
- Personal example of which year you're in or which one changed your trajectory most

## Pitfall to Avoid
Don't present this as an academic taxonomy. The video only works if it feels immediately applicable. The hook needs to promise clarity, not information. "This framework helped me stop feeling stuck" is stronger than "Here are 5 things to consider."
