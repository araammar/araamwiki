# Idea: Already Extinct

## Concept

A reel about the 4-6 month expertise decay rate. The hook: a guy who sold his company for $100M+ is functionally out of touch with current marketing within a year of stepping away. The insight is counterintuitive — most people assume a $100M exit = maximum credibility. The reel reframes it: past achievement doesn't equal current relevance. Platform mechanics, content formats, and campaign standards change fast enough that even elite marketers go stale.

## Why It Works

- Counterintuitive hook: the person most people would trust (big exit, proven track record) can be giving outdated advice
- Resolves tension in the creator economy: why some "expert" content feels slightly off even when the person is clearly smart
- High comment potential — people will defend or attack the premise
- Strong visual proof available: contrast a creator who "went stale" vs one who stayed active

## Hook Angle

**Option 1 (False Belief Reframe):**
"A guy who sold his company for $100M doesn't know what's happening in marketing right now."

**Option 2 (Stakes Escalation):**
"Most marketing experts are already extinct. They just don't know it yet."

**Option 3 (Specific Data Point):**
"After 6 months out of the industry, even the best marketers can't speak to current platform mechanics with authority. Here's why."

## Hook Type

False belief reframe. Diagnoses the real problem underneath the apparent credential.

## Target Audience

- Creators who consume "expert" marketing content and occasionally feel it's slightly off
- Marketing professionals building their own content presence
- Founders evaluating marketing advisors or consultants

## Visual Proof Options

- Screen recording: compare two creators' content quality — one who stayed active, one who stepped back
- Timeline graphic: showing how platform mechanics changed in a 12-month window
- Talking head + text overlay listing specific things that changed since 2024

## Recommended Structure

1. Hook (counterintuitive claim — 3-5 seconds)
2. Validate: "This isn't about intelligence. It's about immersion." (5 seconds)
3. Explain the mechanism: texture and specificity come from lived current experience (15-20 seconds)
4. The 4-6 month rule: specific data point (5 seconds)
5. Flip: the fix is intentional re-immersion, not more credentials (10 seconds)
6. Close: "Past achievement tells you what someone was capable of. Current immersion tells you if they're still in the game."
