# Idea: "I Tracked Every Post for 3 Months — Here's What I Learned"

## The Hook
"I tracked every single piece of content I posted for 90 days and the data surprised me"
"The metric that actually predicts follower growth (it's not views)"
"I killed my most popular content format after seeing the data — here's why"

## The Content Format
**Graphic yap with data overlay** — you on camera with your actual spreadsheet data shown behind you (blurred or anonymized if needed). 60–90 seconds.

Walk through:
1. The four or five pillars you were tracking
2. The metric you thought mattered vs. the metric that actually correlated with growth
3. The surprising finding (shares/saves over hook rate, for example)
4. What you changed as a result
5. What the audience can take from this for their own strategy

## Why This Works
- "I ran the experiment so you don't have to" structure is inherently valuable
- Surprising findings drive shares — people forward counterintuitive data
- It positions you as someone who operates with rigor, not just vibes
- It generates follow-up question comments ("what tool did you use to track this?")

## Who Should Make This
- Content creators at any follower level
- Social media managers at brands who want to demonstrate strategic thinking
- Consultants showing how they evaluate account performance
- Anyone trying to position themselves as data-informed rather than trend-chasing

## The Data Setup (Required Before Making This)
Track monthly per content pillar:
- Average views
- Saves
- Shares
- Reach (new vs. existing audience)
- Hook rate (% watching past 3 seconds)

After 3 months minimum, run correlations: what metric is most associated with follower growth? Most accounts discover shares and saves outpredict hook rate for sustained growth.

## Variation Ideas
- "I ran the same hook on 10 different videos — here's what happened"
- "I changed one variable in this content format — views went up 4×"
- "This pillar I thought was working was actually dead — here's how I found out"
- A monthly recurring series: "[Month] Content Performance Report" — transparent, data-forward, builds trust

## Production Requirements
- 3 months of tracked data minimum (can be less if you have high posting frequency)
- Spreadsheet screenshot or slide for graphic overlay
- The more specific and real the numbers, the more credible the video

## Pitfall to Avoid
Don't make generic points about "engagement matters." The hook is the specific, non-obvious finding. "I discovered that shares, not views, predict my follower growth — and I changed my entire strategy because of it" is a video. "Track your metrics" is not.
