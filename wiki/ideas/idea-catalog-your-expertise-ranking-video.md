# Idea: Catalog Your Expertise — The Ranking Video as First Piece of Content

## The Idea
For any brand, service, or professional account starting from zero followers: film one rankings video in your expert environment and post it before anything else. The ranking/rating format is the highest-performing expert content template on short-form platforms right now — it answers the question every buyer has before spending money in your category, it creates immediate information tension in the hook, and it requires zero production budget beyond a camera and the expert environment you already have.

## Why This Is the Right First Move

Starting a brand social account with zero followers creates a false dilemma: do you post content for the algorithm or for the people who don't exist yet? The rankings format solves both.

- **For the algorithm:** Information tension in the first three seconds (the viewer wants to know the verdict) drives strong hook rate from the first view
- **For non-followers:** "Is X better than Y for Z?" is a search-intent question. The content surfaces through discovery because it directly answers a question people are actively asking
- **For conversion:** A viewer who watches an expert correctly diagnose something in their category arrives at the brand with trust already established — the content did the credentialing work

**The evidence:** A dentist with zero social following filmed herself rating common whitening products while sitting in the dentist chair. First video ever made. 1.6M views. A baker in Vermont did the same with premium baking ingredients. Equally viral. Same format. Different category. Same result.

## The Formula
"Is [X] or [Y] actually better for [Z]?"
or
"I tried/rated [5–10 items in your category] — here's my honest verdict"

## How to Execute It

**Step 1 — Choose the comparison or ranking set.**
Pick 3–10 items in your category that your most valuable customer already has opinions about or is actively researching. The items should be: well-known enough to have existing search interest, and genuinely differentiated enough that an expert verdict is useful.

**Step 2 — Film in the expert environment.**
The environment is the credential signal. A dentist chair, a professional kitchen, a workshop, a design studio, a medical office — these settings communicate authority before the expert speaks. iPhone quality is sufficient. The set does the work.

**Step 3 — Dress the part.**
Lab coat, chef's coat, work uniform, trade-specific gear. This is not about appearance — it's about reducing the cognitive load required for the viewer to accept your expertise. The visual costume accelerates credibility establishment.

**Step 4 — Hook with the verdict, not the setup.**
Start with the most interesting finding, then explain how you got there. "This one is the biggest waste of money in skincare right now" → then you can explain why. Do not start with "Today I'm going to rate five serums."

**Step 5 — Be specific with your verdicts.**
Vague expert opinions ("it depends") kill watch time. Even if the real answer is nuanced, the content version should commit to a verdict — you can add nuance after the verdict, not before.

## Topic Generation Template

For any industry, generate ranking topics by completing these prompts:
- "[Common expensive product in our category] — is it actually worth it?"
- "[Two competing approaches or products] — which is better for [specific customer type]?"
- "I tested [X products that look similar] — here's which one is actually different"
- "The [X] our customers keep asking about — here's my honest take after [years of experience]"

## After the First Video

If the first video performs (2K+ views for narrow niches, 5K+ for broad categories), the comments will contain the next 10 video topics. People ask follow-up questions. People name competitors or adjacent products they want rated. The comment section is the content roadmap — read it before planning what to film next.

Once consistent traction exists, add a ManyChat trigger: "Comment [keyword] and I'll send you [the full ranking / the comparison guide / the free checklist]." This moves warm, expert-qualified leads directly into DM conversation without a landing page.

## Execution Checklist
- [ ] List 10 comparison or ranking topics that your best customer is actively researching
- [ ] Pick the one with the most obvious information tension as your first video
- [ ] Identify and set up your recurring expert environment
- [ ] Gather the items to be rated (or set up the comparison physically if it's a service)
- [ ] Film 3–4 takes of the hook (first 5 seconds) — test different opening lines in the edit
- [ ] Post without CTA in the first 30 days
- [ ] Read every comment within 24 hours and respond with genuine expertise — this converts commenters into leads
