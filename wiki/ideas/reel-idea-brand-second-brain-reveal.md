# Idea: "I Built a Brand Strategist in Claude — Here's What It Knows"

## The Hook
"I uploaded every piece of brand content I've ever made and turned it into an AI brand strategist"
"This AI knows [brand] better than most employees do — here's how I built it"
"I asked my brand's AI clone to roast our latest campaign — it was uncomfortably accurate"

## The Content Format
**Screen-share yap** — camera of you plus screen showing the Claude/ChatGPT project being queried. Alternatively, green screen with slide of the setup behind you.

Walk through:
1. What you put into the project (transcripts, guidelines, past campaigns, audience profiles)
2. The custom instruction you wrote ("You are the in-house brand strategist for X...")
3. A live query — ask it something non-obvious and show the answer
4. Explain how the team or other collaborators now use it
5. CTA: the prompt template anyone can adapt

## Why This Works
- Shows a concrete, replicable workflow — not AI hype, but an actual use case
- The "brand clone" framing is provocative and gets comments
- The live query demo is proof — watching AI respond in-character is compelling
- Positions you as an AI-enabled strategist, not just someone who talks about AI

## Who Should Make This
- Brand strategists showing their methodology
- Agency creatives differentiating their process
- Consultants who work with multiple brands
- Creators who want to demonstrate their system

## The Prompt to Include in the Video
```
You are the in-house brand strategist for [Brand Name].
Brand value proposition: [describe]
Target audience: [describe]
Tone of voice: [describe — e.g., direct, irreverent, expert but accessible]
What you help with: campaign ideation, copy review, content briefs, competitor analysis
What you avoid: [brand-specific no-gos]
```

## Variation Ideas
- "I asked the brand AI clone 10 questions a new hire would ask — here are the answers"
- "Here's what happens when you put 3 years of content into Claude"
- "I tested my brand's AI clone vs. a junior strategist — here's how accurate it was"
- Tutorial format: step-by-step on how to set up a project in Claude with custom instructions

## Production Requirements
- A built project in Claude or ChatGPT with brand material uploaded
- Screen capture or camera facing screen during the demo
- The moment of the AI responding on-screen is the money shot
- Overlay your talking-head with the screen using graphic yap format

## Pitfall to Avoid
Don't make this abstract. The only thing that makes this compelling is the actual output — show the AI giving a real, specific, brand-accurate answer. If you summarize instead of showing, you lose the proof.
