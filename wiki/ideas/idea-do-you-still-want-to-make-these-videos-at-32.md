# Idea: Do You Still Want to Make These Videos at 32?

## Concept

The longevity problem with entertainment content. Most creators building on trend-reactive, personality-driven formats in their 20s haven't asked whether they'll still want to make this content at 32, 35, or 40. Value content inverts this: expertise compounds with age, making the content stronger the older you get. Entertainment content has a creator-age mismatch — the content that performs peaks early, while the creator's desire to make it typically fades.

## Why It Works

- Uncomfortable question most creators haven't consciously asked
- Validates the instinct many entertainment creators already feel but haven't named
- The reframe (value content compounds, entertainment fades) is genuinely useful
- High share rate: people tag friends who are "in their entertainment content era"

## Hook Options

**Option 1 (Single Open Question):**
"Do you still want to be making these videos at 32?"

**Option 2 (Stakes Escalation):**
"The content that makes you famous at 23 is the content you'll dread making at 32."

**Option 3 (False Belief Reframe):**
"You don't have a content strategy problem. You have a 20-year problem."

## Target Audience

- Creators in their 20s building on entertainment/trend formats
- Creators approaching their late 20s who are already feeling the friction
- Professionals considering starting content and trying to pick a format

## Visual Proof Options

**Primary:** Timeline graphic — two lines from age 20 to 40:
- Entertainment line: peaks at 24-26, flattens, declines
- Value/expertise line: steady rise, steepens after 30

**Secondary:** Side-by-side of same creator at 24 (entertainment content) vs 34 (expert content) — which one has a longer shelf life?

## Recommended Structure

1. Hook — open question, no setup (3 seconds)
2. The pattern: creators who built on entertainment in their 20s reaching their 30s (10 seconds)
3. The math: entertainment formats age out of the creator faster than the audience (10 seconds)
4. The inversion: value content compounds — your expertise grows, your content gets better, your credibility deepens (15 seconds)
5. Timeline visual — two lines (5 seconds)
6. The ask isn't "should you do entertainment?" — it's "are you doing it intentionally?" (10 seconds)
7. Close: "The 20-year position is the only question that matters. What do you want to be making in 20 years?"

## Notes / Variations

- Can be adapted as a LinkedIn post using the Stakes Escalation hook with a paradox close: "The content that makes you famous is the content that eventually traps you."
- Strong angle for the "value vs entertainment" concept thread — can be a multi-post series
